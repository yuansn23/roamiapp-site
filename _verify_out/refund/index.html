Our Commitment

Our eSIMs typically work flawlessly, but if you encounter network connectivity issues, we can offer a full or partial refund. We are committed to providing you with a fair and transparent service experience.

⚠️ Some conditions apply. Please read the following terms carefully.

1 Scope

This cancellation and return policy applies to all products purchased through the Roami online store, with no exceptions.

Once you purchase an eSIM, you will receive an order confirmation email containing a QR code. Once sent, we cannot retrieve the eSIM or prevent its use.

2 Refundable Cases

1 Compatibility Issues

If you purchased an eSIM without checking device compatibility, you may request a refund if all the following conditions are met:

  • ✅ QR code has not been scanned
  • ✅ No data has been used from the plan
  • ✅ eSIM purchased within the last 6 months
  • ✅ You provide screenshots and necessary information verifying incompatibility or device lock issues

2 Trip Cancellation

If you cancel your trip or no longer need the eSIM, you may request a full refund if the following requirements are met:

  • ✅ eSIM was purchased from the Roami website
  • ✅ eSIM has not been activated
  • ✅ Purchase was made within the last 6 months

📌 If the eSIM has been activated or used, we will provide the best solution based on your specific situation.

3 Network Connectivity Issues

If your eSIM fails to work due to issues on Roami's or the network provider's end, we can offer a full or partial refund.

  • 📧 Contact support team to report network connectivity issues
  • 📸 Provide information and screenshots to help our team identify the problem
  • 🔄 If the connectivity issue cannot be resolved, Roami will refund your order amount

💰 Refund calculation: Never connected to internet → Full refund; Partial usage → Prorated refund based on days without network
