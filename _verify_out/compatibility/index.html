Frequently Asked Questions about eSIM Compatibility

How do I check if my phone supports eSIM?

The fastest way is to open your phone’s dialer and enter *#06#. If a 32-digit EID (eUICC ID) appears, your device has eSIM hardware. If you only see IMEI numbers, your phone does not support eSIM. You can also check Settings: iPhone → Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM; Android → Settings > Connections > SIM Manager > Add eSIM.

Which iPhone models support eSIM?

eSIM is supported on iPhone XR, XS, XS Max, and every newer model — iPhone 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro Max, SE (2020 & 2022), and all models from iPhone 12 through iPhone 17. US iPhone 14 and later are eSIM-only. iPhone 8, 8 Plus, and iPhone X do NOT support eSIM. See our iPhone 11 eSIM guide for details.

Which Samsung Galaxy models support eSIM?

Galaxy S20, S21, S22, S23, S24, S25, and S26 series support eSIM in most regions, along with Z Fold and Z Flip series. Select A-series models (A35, A36, A54, A55, A56) support eSIM. The Galaxy S20 FE does NOT support eSIM, and US Snapdragon models often lack it. See our Samsung S20 eSIM guide.

Do Google Pixel phones support eSIM?

Yes. All Google Pixel models from Pixel 3 onwards support eSIM — Pixel 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 series. Pixel 2 and older do not. Some Japanese carrier-locked variants may be restricted. See our Pixel 6 eSIM guide.

Which phones do NOT support eSIM?

iPhone 8, X, and older; Samsung Galaxy S10 and older plus the Galaxy S20 FE; Google Pixel 2 and older; and most phones released before 2018. Phones sold in mainland China and Hong Kong usually lack eSIM. Check the ❌ No eSIM Support section above for the full list.

Can I use eSIM on a laptop or tablet?

Yes. eSIM works on Windows laptops with a cellular modem (Surface Pro, ThinkPad, Dell Latitude), cellular iPads, Samsung Galaxy Tab, and some cellular smartwatches. The device must have an eSIM chip and be unlocked. See our iPad & Apple Watch eSIM guide.

Does the iPhone 11 support eSIM?

Yes. The iPhone 11, 11 Pro, and 11 Pro Max all support eSIM. Each holds one physical nano-SIM plus one eSIM, so you can use a travel eSIM alongside your regular number. See our iPhone 11 eSIM guide for setup steps.

Does the iPhone XR support eSIM?

Yes. The iPhone XR was Apple’s first budget iPhone with eSIM. It holds one physical nano-SIM plus one eSIM. See our iPhone XR eSIM guide for details.

Does the Samsung Galaxy S20 support eSIM?

Yes. The Galaxy S20, S20+, and S20 Ultra support eSIM in most regions. The S20 FE does NOT support eSIM, and US carrier-locked models may lack it. See our Samsung S20 eSIM guide.

Does the Google Pixel 6 support eSIM?

Yes. The Pixel 6 and Pixel 6 Pro both support eSIM. See our Pixel 6 eSIM guide for setup.

How do I check if my US carrier supports eSIM on my phone?

First dial *#06# to confirm your phone has an EID. If it does, carrier support generally follows — provided your device is unlocked. T-Mobile and AT&T are typically the most flexible with unlocked Android devices, while Verizon is stricter with non-iPhone models. The safest check is to install a free trial eSIM before buying a plan.

Do phones bought in China support eSIM?

iPhones sold in mainland China do NOT support eSIM — this applies to all models from iPhone XR through iPhone 17. Hong Kong and Macau models may have two physical SIM slots without eSIM. Samsung domestic models in China also typically lack eSIM. Dial *#06# — if no EID appears, use a physical SIM instead.

Can I use two eSIMs at the same time?

Yes, on newer phones. iPhone 13 and later support dual eSIM (two active eSIMs). Samsung S24 and later support dual eSIM. Google Pixel 8 and later support dual eSIM. Older phones may support one eSIM plus one physical SIM.

My phone is eSIM compatible but carrier-locked. Can I use a travel eSIM?

Usually not. A carrier-locked phone can only use eSIMs from the carrier it is locked to — a T-Mobile locked phone cannot install a Roami or Airalo eSIM. Request an unlock from your carrier first. If you bought your phone directly from Apple, Samsung, or Google, it is almost certainly unlocked.

What should I do before traveling with an eSIM?

  1. Dial *#06# to confirm your phone has an EID. 2. Update your phone to the latest OS. 3. Buy a plan from a global provider like Roami before departure. 4. Install the eSIM profile while connected to Wi-Fi. 5. After landing, enable Data Roaming for the eSIM line. See our eSIM activation guide for full steps.
