Saudi Arabia eSIM rules start with your visa

Saudi Arabia runs mandatory real-name registration for every mobile line activated on a local network, and the CST enforces it through live database checks across all licensed operators. There are no anonymous SIMs in the kingdom. For a visitor, buying a local line means producing your original passport — photocopies are usually refused — plus your Saudi border number, the identifier immigration assigns when your passport is stamped at entry.

The counter process itself is short, typically five to ten minutes, and at operator kiosks it often includes a biometric fingerprint check. The line you walk away with is tied to your visa status: tourist SIMs are visitor packages whose validity is bounded by your stay, and an unregistered or improperly registered line can be deactivated within days of first use. CST rules also cap registrations — visitors can generally hold only one or two SIMs per passport, expatriate residents two per Iqama, and Saudi citizens up to ten.

One consequence matters more than any other: the three big carriers sell eSIMs, but a local eSIM still requires the same in-person registration as a plastic card. You visit a store, the staff verify your passport and border number, and only then do you receive a QR code to scan. A travel Saudi Arabia eSIM purchased before departure is issued abroad and roams onto the Saudi networks, so it falls outside the CST registration mandate — which is exactly why it is the default recommendation for short stays and peak-season arrivals.

Saudi eSIM rules: cruise, transit and GCC-resident arrivals

Three of those rows deserve a second look. Air passengers who hold qualifying Saudia or Flynas tickets can be issued a 48-hour transit sightseeing visa, and that entry record is enough for a visitor line at the airport. Cruise passengers and land arrivals follow the same registry logic once they clear immigration, though kiosk hours at smaller ports are less predictable than at Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Madinah airports. GCC citizens, who need no visa at all, register a line on a GCC national ID instead of a passport.

Whichever row you fall into, the underlying constraint is identical: the registry matches your name as spelled on the passport, so a mismatch between your booking, your visa and your passport is the most common reason a freshly bought Saudi Arabia eSIM shows no service. Note the border number at immigration and keep it in a notes app.

Is your phone compatible with a Saudi Arabia eSIM?

The good news is that Saudi networks are among the most standards-friendly in the region. 5G runs on n41 (2.5 GHz) and n78 (3.5 GHz), both common on international flagships, and 4G LTE coverage is effectively universal where people actually live. A phone that works on LTE in Europe or Asia will work in Riyadh.

Three checks before you pay for a Saudi Arabia eSIM

First, eSIM support: dial *#06# and look for an EID, or run your exact model through the eSIM compatibility quiz — model number, not marketing name. Second, carrier lock: a phone bought on a contract abroad may refuse any third-party profile until its original carrier unlocks it; Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock should read “No SIM restrictions”. Third, dual-SIM behavior: most visitors keep their home SIM active for SMS verification codes and point mobile data at the Saudi Arabia eSIM.

Hotspot sharing works on the carrier visitor packages; some travel eSIMs cap tethering, so check the terms if you plan to work off a laptop.

What peak season does to a Saudi Arabia eSIM

Congestion changes the shape of the problem, not its cause. Your line stays registered and your plan stays valid; what falls is the bitrate you actually receive, because one cell that serves a neighbourhood in ordinary weeks is serving a city’s worth of phones during Hajj. Switching to 4G manually can sometimes reattach you to a less loaded band, and data-saver modes help noticeably in the courtyards. Where you have a choice, pick the carrier with the deepest local capacity — Mobily’s consistency scores in Makkah and Madinah are why pilgrims based there often prefer it.

Paying for a Saudi Arabia eSIM: mada, cards and top-up channels

How to activate a Saudi Arabia eSIM and fix common problems

The generic install sequence — add the profile, label the line, set it as your data line, enable data roaming — is in our activation walkthrough, and the wider error catalogue lives in our eSIM activation troubleshooting guide. Saudi Arabia adds four failure patterns of its own.

Four Saudi eSIM failure patterns

A. The line never activates after the counter visit. Registration is the bottleneck, not the profile. If your SIM or eSIM shows no service hours after purchase, the passport-to-border-number match may have failed at the registry. Return to any branch of the issuing carrier with your original passport; mismatches in name spelling are the most common cause.

B. The QR code was scanned once and then deleted. Carrier-issued eSIM QR codes are single-use. Delete the profile and the only fix is a new code, which in Saudi Arabia usually means another store visit with your passport — so screenshot the QR and keep the profile even if you swap plans mid-trip.

C. Signal bars, but no data. Work the APN table above: wrong or missing APN values account for most carrier-profile data failures. Confirm data roaming is on and that the eSIM line, not your home SIM, is the selected data line.

D. Everything connects, nothing loads. That is congestion, not configuration — Hajj in the holy cities, Riyadh Season downtown, Friday evenings on the highways. Switch to 4G manually to reattach to a less loaded band, download offline maps for Makkah and Madinah in advance, and schedule uploads for off-peak hours.

Saudi Arabia eSIM FAQ: quick answers

Plenty more where these came from — the full FAQ library covers the rest.

Where to buy a Saudi Arabia eSIM

Zain KSA, stc and Mobily all staff counters in the arrivals hall, and eSIMs are sold on the spot. Bring your original passport and border number and allow for biometric verification. During Hajj and Umrah peaks the queues stretch badly — a travel eSIM installed before boarding is the workaround.

Do Saudi prepaid SIMs still work during Hajj season?

They do. No operator blocks visitor lines during Hajj, and networks in Makkah and Madinah are built for the surge. What changes is throughput: at peak hours around the holy sites, every network slows. Install offline maps, enable data-saver modes and expect delays on video calls.

How many SIMs can one passport register in Saudi Arabia?

Visitors are generally limited to one or two active lines per passport across all operators, residents to two per Iqama, and citizens to ten. The registry is centralized under the CST, so the cap counts every Saudi SIM you hold, not per carrier.

Does a Saudi tourist SIM stop working when my visa ends?

Effectively, yes. Visitor packages are tied to your visa validity, and tourist lines expire automatically after their validity window if not recharged. If your trip extends, recharge before expiry at the operator’s app or any recharge point.

STC vs Mobily: coverage compared

stc. It wins Opensignal’s Coverage Experience outright — 8.5 out of 10 in the October 2025 report — and multiple independent guides single it out for remote routes toward AlUla, Hail and the Empty Quarter. Off the highways, no carrier is dependable; download offline maps regardless.

Can I pay for a Saudi SIM with a foreign card?

Airport counters usually accept international credit cards, and contactless Apple Pay works with a foreign card. City stores favor mada debit cards and cash, so have Saudi riyals on hand as a fallback, and expect terminals to occasionally reject foreign cards at peak times.

Will a travel eSIM work in Makkah and Madinah?

Yes. Travel eSIMs roam onto the same local networks and require no CST registration. The same seasonal congestion applies around the holy sites, so a travel eSIM solves paperwork, not capacity. Keep your home SIM on for SMS codes and point data at the eSIM line.

Saudi Arabia APN reference

jawalnet.com.sa, with username and password left blank. Mobily uses web2 and Zain KSA uses zain. On most carrier-issued profiles the values arrive preloaded; only enter them manually if mobile data fails, then restart the phone.

Will my WhatsApp number change on a Saudi eSIM?

WhatsApp is tied to the number that receives the verification SMS, which stays your home number on a dual-SIM setup. A data-only travel eSIM changes nothing; a local Saudi line gives you a new number for calls but does not move your WhatsApp unless you re-verify.

Is 5G available everywhere in Saudi Arabia?

No. It is dense in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam, present in secondary cities, and absent between towns. Saudi 5G uses n41 and n78 bands; phones without them fall back to fast LTE, which in cities is frequently quicker than 5G elsewhere in the region.
