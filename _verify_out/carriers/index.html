Frequently Asked Questions about global eSIM carriers & networks

How do I choose the best eSIM carrier for my destination?

The choice depends on your itinerary. In cities, providers with the 'Fastest 5G' badge (like T‑Mobile in the USA or SoftBank in Japan) are ideal. For rural areas or islands, you should prioritise carriers with 'Best coverage' (e.g., Verizon or Docomo).

Is the speed of my eSIM as fast as that of a local physical SIM card?

Yes! Because our travel eSIMs work directly with tier‑1 carriers (like AIS, EE or Orange), you enjoy the same native 4G LTE or 5G speeds without expensive roaming fees.

Do these global eSIM providers support 5G networks?

Most of our partners worldwide now offer 5G. In countries like South Korea, Japan and the USA, 5G is widespread. Your device will automatically connect to 5G if it is compatible and you are in a coverage area.

Can my eSIM automatically switch between different carriers?

That depends on the plan. Many of our regional eSIMs (like the Europe eSIM) are multi‑network capable and automatically switch to the strongest signal when you cross borders.

Will the connection drop in remote areas or on islands?

That depends on the local infrastructure. In such cases we recommend eSIMs from market leaders that focus on rural coverage (e.g., Swisscom in the Alps or Viettel in Vietnam).
