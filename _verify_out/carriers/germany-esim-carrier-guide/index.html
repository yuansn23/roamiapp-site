Does my phone work with a German eSIM?

Three things decide whether your phone works on a German carrier: its bands, its lock status, and a short list of device-specific quirks. All three are covered below.

What devices are blocked from Germany eSIMs?

German carriers build 5G on specific bands, notably n78 at 3.6 GHz for capacity, n28 at 700 MHz for coverage, and n1 at 2100 MHz, while 4G LTE uses 800 MHz band 20, 1800 MHz band 3 and 2600 MHz band 7. Plenty of international handsets miss one or more of these, especially n28, and those phones still work: they fall back to LTE once you leave dense urban areas, which is fine downtown and frustrating in the countryside.

There is no need to memorise German band tables. The safest route is to compare the precise model number with the eSIM device checker. If you would rather see the internals first, what your phone does when it loads an eSIM profile walks through the process.

Will a SIM lock block your German eSIM?

A locked phone is the most common reason a German eSIM install simply fails, and Germany handles this differently from Canada. The European Union has no single mandatory unlock law like the CRTC Wireless Code, so phones are not legally required to ship unlocked. In practice, handsets bought in the EU or UK are almost always sold unlocked, which means the lock problem in Germany is almost always a phone still tied to a non-EU carrier, most often a US postpaid device still under contract.

Check it: on an iPhone, open Settings, then General, About and read the Carrier Lock line. If the entry shows “No SIM restrictions”, nothing stands in the way. Where the screen says “SIM locked”, a third-party eSIM cannot install until the lock is removed.

Fix it: reach out to the carrier that supplied the phone and ask them to unlock it. Paid-off devices are usually cleared within hours, and there is no German fee involved because the lock is held by your home carrier, not a German one. Only then try installing your eSIM.

If you picked up a second-hand phone, treat it as possibly locked and verify before your flight. Full detail sits with your home carrier, not a German regulator.

Germany eSIM prices compared

Four routes to a Germany eSIM exist, and they differ more in paperwork than in price: Telekom prepaid through customer service, Vodafone CallYa online, O2 prepaid direct, or a discount brand after a physical SIM. Each route, step by step:

Where visitors buy Germany eSIMs

Telekom’s own consumer plans are postpaid and want a German address and a credit check, but the prepaid route is open to visitors. MagentaMobil Prepaid starts at 4.95 EUR for 4 weeks with 1 GB of data and 50 minutes, rises through 8 GB at 9.95 EUR, 15 GB at 14.95 EUR and 25 GB at 19.95 EUR, and reaches an unlimited tier at 99.95 EUR for 4 weeks. All include 5G up to 300 Mbit/s and EU roaming.

Telekom eSIM, step by step:

  1. Order MagentaMobil Prepaid from telekom.de and choose the plan that fits, then receive the starter kit.
  2. Activate the physical SIM and complete identity verification through PostIdent or VideoIdent with your passport.
  3. Request the eSIM conversion through Telekom customer service, which issues the profile to your phone.
  4. No credit check, no term contract, but the eSIM step runs through support rather than a one-tap app install.

Telekom also sells eSIM directly on postpaid MagentaMobil plans, which is the cleanest eSIM path if you can pass the address and credit check.

Local SIM or travel eSIM in Germany?

For the local-SIM route, Vodafone is the easiest of the three big carriers for a visitor. CallYa is the prepaid brand and it offers a free eSIM ordered entirely online.

Vodafone CallYa eSIM, step by step:

  1. Go to vodafone.de, choose CallYa with eSIM, enter your details and pick a plan.
  2. Receive an order confirmation with login details and activation instructions by email.
  3. After arriving in Germany, complete identity verification through PostIdent at any Deutsche Post branch or by VideoIdent in the app.
  4. Receive the QR code or activation link by email, scan it in your eSIM settings, then top up.

CallYa plans run on four-week cycles: CallYa Start gives 1 GB for 4.99 EUR, CallYa Allnet Flat S gives 25 GB for 9.99 EUR, CallYa Allnet Flat M gives 50 GB for 14.99 EUR, CallYa Black is unlimited for 79.99 EUR, and the Jahrespaket M gives 250 GB for 99.99 EUR per year. All include EU roaming. Without completing identity verification the SIM stays locked, so treat the PostIdent step as the real gate.

The O2 route: a direct prepaid eSIM

O2, run by Telefonica Germany, sells a prepaid eSIM directly through its “Prepaid eSIM for Europe” offer: 20 GB for 9.99 EUR per 4 weeks or 40 GB for 14.99 EUR per 4 weeks, with EU roaming included and speeds up to 300 Mbit/s. After the high-speed allowance is used, O2 keeps you online at up to 1 Mbit/s down and 384 Kbit/s up rather than cutting you off.

O2 eSIM, step by step:

  1. Order the O2 prepaid eSIM from o2online.de and choose a plan.
  2. Verify your passport online during sign-up, which O2 supports for foreign passports though some non-EU documents take longer.
  3. Receive the QR code by email and install it before departure or on arrival.
  4. No German address is required for the prepaid route, only the identity check.

If you want O2 pricing but plan to stay in cities only, Aldi Talk and blau resell O2 and undercut it further, at the cost of the physical-SIM-first eSIM detour described above. O2 itself is the simpler eSIM path.

What to prepare before buying a German carrier eSIM

The APN screen is the last thing you should need and the first thing worth checking when a data connection dies. Below are the values for all three networks plus their discount brands, when manual entry is genuinely required, and the exact menu paths.

APN values for Telekom, Vodafone and O2 eSIMs

You will only need these if you bought your Germany eSIM directly from a German carrier or a brand on its network. When you run a travel eSIM, the profile carries its own APN, so you should leave those fields alone.

Leave username and password empty. For O2-based lines, internet is a usable fallback if pinternet.interkom.de does not connect, but the longer string is the current standard. Should a carrier demand extra values, the activation instructions bundled with your profile will spell them out.

When a German line needs the APN typed in

  • An older phone that will not pull carrier settings on its own
  • A profile added through a manual activation code rather than a QR scan
  • A carrier-issued prepaid eSIM whose automatic configuration never completed
  • On a travel eSIM this is almost a non-issue, which is the whole point of a managed profile

Where the APN fields live on your phone

  • iPhone: open Settings, choose Cellular, select the eSIM line, then Cellular Data Network and type the APN
  • Android: open Settings, go to Connections, Mobile Networks, Access Point Names and add a new APN

After saving, restart the phone. If data is still missing, be sure the eSIM, and not your home SIM, is the line picked for mobile data before you touch other settings.

Germany eSIM failure: four fixes

General activation errors, a profile that will not download, a scan that fails, an eSIM that installs but never registers, are covered in our activation error troubleshooting guide. The four failure patterns below are specific to Germany.

A. The eSIM will not install

  1. Confirm Carrier Lock shows “No SIM restrictions”
  2. Check the QR code is clear and has not yet been scanned, since most work only once
  3. The carrier can confirm whether the profile is awaiting release after verification

B. Installed, but no signal bars

  1. Re-check the lock status
  2. Settings, Cellular, Network Selection, select Telekom, Vodafone or O2 manually instead of automatic
  3. Reset all network settings, then reboot again

C. Signal bars, but no internet

  1. Check the APN against the table shown above
  2. Make sure the eSIM is the chosen data line, not the SIM from home
  3. Check you have not exhausted your allowance, O2 throttles to 1 Mbit/s rather than stopping, and other prepaid brands enforce a fair-use cap

D. “SOS” or “Emergency calls only”

  1. Confirm the eSIM is switched on in the SIM manager
  2. Turn off or remove any other active SIM or eSIM profile
  3. Reboot and reset network settings
  4. As a final step, delete the profile and reinstall it from a new QR code

Germany eSIM FAQ

Can I buy a prepaid eSIM directly from Telekom as a visitor?

Technically yes, but not in one tap. Telekom’s prepaid eSIM is issued through customer service after you activate a physical MagentaMobil Prepaid SIM and complete identity verification. Telekom’s postpaid MagentaMobil plans offer eSIM directly at checkout, but those want a German address and a credit check. For most visitors the prepaid route is the realistic one.

Does a Vodafone eSIM require a German address?

For CallYa prepaid, no fixed address beyond a contact point is needed to order, but you must complete identity verification, usually PostIdent at a Post branch, after arrival. Vodafone is the visitor-friendliest of the big three because the eSIM itself is free and ordered online. If you want the Vodafone network without that step, a travel eSIM on the Vodafone network is the alternative.

Can international visitors buy an O2 eSIM?

Yes. O2 sells a prepaid eSIM directly through its website, with passport verification handled online during sign-up. Plans start at 20 GB for 9.99 EUR per 4 weeks and include EU roaming. O2 is a strong choice for visitors who want a real German eSIM without the physical-SIM-first detour that Aldi Talk and Lidl Connect impose.

Which Germany network is best overall?

Vodafone, on the balance of evidence. Telekom leads on consistency and raw speed, but Vodafone holds signal better along highways and in smaller towns, and O2 is the weakest outside cities. If your trip leaves the major urban regions, favor Telekom or Vodafone, and download offline maps for the Black Forest and Alpine valleys regardless.

Which Germany carrier is the cheapest?

O2 and its discount brands. O2’s own prepaid eSIM starts at 20 GB for 9.99 EUR per 4 weeks, Aldi Talk and blau resell O2 at similar or lower prices, and Lidl Connect resells Vodafone from about 8.99 EUR for a four-week bundle. Telekom costs the most. Match the brand to your coverage need rather than buying the cheapest and losing signal on day two.

Is identity verification required for a Germany eSIM?

Yes, without exception. German law requires identification for every prepaid SIM and eSIM, so a passport check through PostIdent, VideoIdent, eID or an in-store visit is mandatory before the line goes live. This is the single biggest difference from a travel eSIM, which needs no German verification.

Device checks: IMEI and EID in Germany

Does a German prepaid eSIM work in the EU?

Yes. Because Germany sits inside the EU, prepaid plans follow Roam Like At Home, so your allowance works across the bloc within fair-use limits. The catch is Switzerland, which is not in the EU and is billed separately by nearly every German provider, so plan for it explicitly on a Switzerland day trip.

Should I buy from a German carrier or a travel eSIM?

Direct means one network, carrier-issued provisioning, and a passport check, and in exchange you get a real German number and unlimited domestic calling. A travel eSIM means instant delivery, no paperwork, automatic switching between Telekom, Vodafone and O2, and a fixed upfront price. Short trip, travel eSIM. For trips measured in months — or if a local number matters — buy from a carrier directly.

Do budget brands like Aldi Talk sell a real prepaid eSIM?

They sell prepaid plans on O2 or Vodafone, but the eSIM is not instant for new customers. Aldi Talk, Lidl Connect and Otelo require you to order and activate a physical SIM first, then convert it to eSIM in the account portal, and the same identity check still applies. They are cheap, not frictionless.

How do I unlock my phone for a German eSIM?

The lock is held by your home carrier, not a German one, because Germany has no mandatory unlock law. Contact the carrier the phone is locked to and request an unlock; paid-off devices clear within hours at no German fee. Check that status at Settings, General, About, Carrier Lock before you board your flight.

What if my Germany eSIM will not activate?

Work the four Germany-specific patterns above in order, lock status, network selection, APN and data line, then profile reinstall, and if it still fails, the wider error catalogue in eSIM problem-solving guide covers the rest. Keep your EID, order number and a screenshot on hand before you reach out to support.
