A Jordan eSIM choice splits with the country itself: fast 5G in Amman’s tech hub versus a lifeline signal on the southern tourist route — this guide covers both ends. Jordan is a small kingdom with a split personality, and its connectivity splits with it. In Amman, a genuine tech hub of startups, coworking spaces and the Zain Innovation Campus, you want the fastest 5G you can hold. Four hundred kilometres south, in the camps of Wadi Rum and the canyons of Petra, you want whichever network keeps a bar of signal alive. Ookla’s Speedtest Global Index put Jordan’s median mobile download at 48.53 Mbps in August 2026 — 81st worldwide, well below the 109.05 Mbps global median — while Cable.co.uk prices a gigabyte of local data at about USD 0.76, cheaper than most of Europe. Which Jordan eSIM fits you depends on which of the two Jordans you are visiting.

Jordan’s three carriers: Zain, Orange and Umniah

Three operators share the market, each holding roughly a third of it, all licensed by the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission (TRC). Umniah is owned by Bahrain’s Beyon group; Orange Jordan is the local arm of the French group; Zain Jordan belongs to the Kuwaiti Zain group, which also runs Zain KSA in Saudi Arabia. Do not confuse Zain Jordan with Zain Iraq — a different company on a different network, covered in our own Iraq guide.

Zain Jordan vs Umniah: coverage compared

Zain Jordan is the largest operator by subscriber base and the operator most international eSIM providers use for Jordanian coverage. On travellers’ own reporting it holds up best outside the cities — the King’s Highway toward Petra, the Wadi Rum visitor centre, and the long southern corridors where the other two thin out. If your itinerary is a loop rather than a single city, that reach is the argument.

Orange vs Umniah 5G: which is better in Jordan?

Orange Jordan is the second-largest carrier and the strongest on 5G availability: Opensignal measured its users spending 37.8% of connected time on a 5G signal, more than nine percentage points ahead of second place. It is also the only operator with a booth in the Queen Alia arrivals hall that runs around the clock, which makes it the default first stop for late-night arrivals.

Umniah eSIM prices and performance

Umniah is the cheapest of the three on most measured comparisons and won Ookla’s Best Mobile Network award for Q3–Q4 2025 with a Connectivity Score of 73.23, ahead of Zain Jordan’s 68.64 and Orange Jordan’s 68.46. It leads on consistency and reliability too. The trade-off is real: outside Amman and the northern cities, its coverage drops away faster than the other two.

How to read the Jordan eSIM carrier scorecards

Two patterns are worth noticing. First, national averages and local experience diverge: Zain Jordan’s download average sits behind Orange Jordan’s nationally, yet it is the network travellers report as most reliable on the rural routes — because averages reward dense urban performance. Second, the awards split by category rather than by operator. Umniah wins consistency and reliability; Zain Jordan wins raw 5G speed; Orange Jordan wins 5G reach. No single network takes everything, so no single “best carrier” answer exists.

What the scorecards mean for a Jordan eSIM

Put the awards side by side and one conclusion survives: pick by where you are going, not by who won last quarter. A Jordan eSIM that can fall back onto two networks beats any single-operator line on a route that crosses from Amman into the south.

A Jordan eSIM for the classic two-week loop

Amman, Petra, Wadi Rum, Aqaba, then back north to the Dead Sea: this is the itinerary most visitors run, and it is the one that rewards a coverage-first choice. Zain Jordan holds the widest footprint across all five stops, with Umniah a close second anywhere inside the cities.

The frontier traveller: border crossings and a Jordan eSIM

Jordan is also a transit country, and transit changes the maths. If you are crossing to or from a neighbouring state within a few days, a Jordan-only profile is dead weight on both sides of the border. A regional or multi-country travel eSIM costs a little more up front and removes the need to buy twice.

A Jordan eSIM for the Aqaba and Red Sea leg

Aqaba is the one place where all three carriers perform equally well, and where tax-free shopping gives you a genuine price advantage on a local line. For a diving week based in the city, buy in Aqaba rather than at the airport. A Jordan eSIM bought online before you fly is the alternative if you would rather not spend an hour of a short trip at a counter.

Buying a Zain Jordan, Orange Jordan or Umniah eSIM as a visitor

Registration is simple and unavoidable: a valid passport is required, the vendor scans it into the system at the point of sale, and anonymous SIMs are not permitted. Activation is usually immediate, with a welcome SMS confirming you are live.

Buying a Jordan eSIM at Queen Alia International Airport

The arrivals hall hosts an Orange booth that runs 24/7, plus Zain Jordan and Umniah kiosks nearby, all taking cash and international cards. Reported tourist offers at the time of writing: Orange’s visitors’ line at about JOD 15 for 20 GB with unlimited local calls for 30 days; Zain Jordan from about JOD 14.50 for 8 GB up to roughly JOD 29 for 50 GB; Umniah around JOD 10 for 30 GB on a 10-day short-stay line.

Buying in Amman city stores or tax-free Aqaba

City stores in Abdali, Mecca Mall and the downtown souks sell much the same packages without the airport premium, and staff in Amman are used to eSIM walk-ins. Aqaba is worth knowing about specifically: it is a tax-free zone, so SIM purchases there skip the 16% sales tax that applies elsewhere in the kingdom. If your trip starts in the south, buying in Aqaba can be meaningfully cheaper.

The Jordan SIM counter sequence, step by step

  1. Choose the visitor package and state that you want an eSIM, not a plastic SIM.
  2. Show your original passport; staff register the line in your name.
  3. Scan the QR code they issue, or install the profile from their app.
  4. Set the eSIM as your data line and enable data roaming.
  5. Test mobile data with Wi-Fi switched off before you leave the counter.

Jordan SIM registration: what to have at the counter

  • Your original passport, not a copy
  • A local address — your hotel name is enough
  • Cash in Jordanian dinars, plus a card as backup
  • An unlocked, eSIM-capable phone — check *#06# for an EID
  • Your departure date, so staff can match validity to your stay
  • Wi-Fi or a working QR scanner if you are installing an eSIM on the spot

Prepaid tourist plan prices in Jordan

Visitor pricing in Jordan is genuinely competitive — a couple of dollars per gigabyte on the short-stay offers — but the ladders change with promotions and the sources disagree on exact allowances. Read this as the shape of the market, and confirm the current tier at the counter.

Zain Jordan’s visitor tiers

Zain Jordan’s visitor line is usually the simplest menu: an entry tier from about JOD 14.50 for 8 GB over 30 days with a generous local-minute allowance, stepping up through mid tiers to roughly JOD 29 for 50 GB. Airport kiosks and city branches both sell it, and the eSIM uses the same registration flow.

Orange Jordan’s visitor tiers

Orange Jordan’s tourists’ line starts around JOD 15 for 20 GB over 30 days with unlimited local calls and SMS plus a small international-minute allowance, and steps to about JOD 30 for 60 GB over 90 days. Some sources list a shorter 10-day, 30 GB line nearer JOD 10 including tax, which is worth asking about if your trip is short.

Umniah’s visitor tiers

Umniah is the cheapest entrance to a Jordanian line in most comparisons: around JOD 10 for 30 GB on a 10-day short-stay package, with online-only offers reported near JOD 7 for 35 GB over 10 days and about JOD 17 for 100 GB over 30 days. The online-only tiers are worth checking in its app before you settle for the airport price.

Travel eSIM versus local Jordan SIM: costs compared

Shorter than a week, a travel eSIM’s time savings win. Longer, and Jordanian per-gigabyte pricing plus a local number for hotels and drivers starts to pay for itself.

Voice calls on a Jordan eSIM

Voice is the variable most comparisons miss. Travel eSIMs are almost always data-only, so the phone calls that hotels, restaurants and drivers still expect either run through your home number at roaming rates or move to WhatsApp data calls. Every Jordanian package listed above includes local minutes, which is why anyone driving between Amman, Petra and Aqaba for more than a few days tends to end up at a kiosk anyway.

Will my phone work with a Jordanian eSIM?

Checking EID, carrier lock and bands before you buy

First, eSIM capability: dial *#06# and look for an EID, then confirm your exact model on the eSIM compatibility page. Second, carrier lock: a phone still locked to a foreign operator will refuse any Jordanian profile until it is unlocked — check Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock. Third, bands: Jordan’s LTE rollout covers well over 90% of the population on standard international bands, and its 5G networks use common mid-band spectrum, so any recent global handset works.

Is personal hotspot included with Jordan eSIMs?

Keep your home SIM active for SMS verification codes, point mobile data at the eSIM, and switch off data roaming on the home line so it cannot bill you for background traffic. Most visitor eSIMs from the carriers support hotspot sharing; travel eSIMs sometimes cap it, so read the terms before relying on a laptop.

One regulatory footnote helps border-crossers: the TRC operates a white/grey/black device registry, and handsets brought in temporarily by visitors sit on the grey list — meaning your foreign phone keeps working normally on a local line without import registration.

Jordan eSIM on landing: activation and network selection

Profiles activate on network registration, not at install. If your Jordan eSIM shows no signal after touchdown, toggle airplane mode, then try manual network selection and pick Zain Jordan, Orange Jordan or Umniah by name before assuming a fault. Amman’s airport Wi-Fi is decent, but a profile that is already installed needs nothing from it.

Jordan offline prep: southern-route maps before you leave Wi-Fi

Before you leave Wi-Fi, download the maps, the Petra audio guide and any camp directions you will need. Between Wadi Musa and Wadi Rum the signal follows the highway, and past the visitor centre in Wadi Rum it stops entirely — no carrier changes that.

Buying and setting up a Jordan eSIM

The generic sequence — install on Wi-Fi, label the line, set it as your data line, enable roaming — is covered step by step in our eSIM activation guide, and the full error catalogue sits in our eSIM activation fixes guide. Four patterns cover most of what goes wrong in Jordan.

Will you need ID for a Jordan SIM?

The line must carry the passport holder’s exact name. If staff abbreviated or transliterated differently than your travel documents show, activation can stall — walk back to the kiosk with your passport and have it re-entered.

Steps to install a Jordan eSIM

If the eSIM shows no signal on landing, toggle airplane mode, then pick a network manually. A profile installed days earlier sometimes parks itself on a carrier it cannot use until you intervene.

Data sizing for Jordan itineraries

Confirm data roaming is enabled for the eSIM line, the eSIM — not your home SIM — is the selected data line, and the APN matches the table above. If the package ran dry, Jordanian carriers throttle or cut rather than warn; top up in the app and reboot.

Which network covers the most of Jordan?

That is geography, not breakage. Coverage south of the tourist spine follows the highway, and the open country between towns is dead on all networks. Expect the bars to return as you approach Aqaba.

Jordan’s four entry points for buying a SIM

For a normal trip, Queen Alia is where the decision happens, because it is the only entry point with carrier counters. The land crossings are transit infrastructure: useful for a visa stamp, useless for buying data, and they are the reason a pre-installed profile earns its keep.

Does a Jordan eSIM work elsewhere?

Jordanian packages do not include cross-border data. Cross at any of the land crossings and your Jordanian line drops onto international roaming rates or no service at all — which is why WhatsApp messages sent from the border queue until you are back inside coverage. A regional travel eSIM changes that: the profile simply attaches to the network on the other side under the provider’s own agreements.

Local SIMs in Jordan vs the travel option

There is no carrier counter at any Jordanian land crossing. If you arrive overland without a Jordan eSIM, plan to buy one in Amman, Aqaba or Irbid instead. Note too that a departure fee of JOD 10 applies at land and sea borders, though not at the airports — another reason arrivals by air are the smoother path.

The Jordan eSIM shortlist, in one line

City-only trip: take the best counter offer. Any trip that leaves Amman: Zain Jordan for reach, Umniah for value, Orange Jordan if 5G coverage in the capital is what you came for.

Common questions about data in Jordan

Jordan eSIM plan prices

You can buy on arrival. Orange runs a 24/7 booth in the AMM arrivals hall, and its visitors’ line is advertised at about JOD 15 for 20 GB over 30 days with unlimited local calls. Bring your original passport, and allow a few minutes for the profile install before your ride leaves.

Zain Jordan vs Umniah: which Jordan carrier is faster?

For raw 5G, Zain Jordan — Opensignal measured its users at 325.3 Mbps average 5G download in mid-2026. For the best all-round experience, Umniah took Ookla’s Best Mobile Network award for Q3–Q4 2025 with a Connectivity Score of 73.23, and it leads on consistency and reliability.

Does a Jordan eSIM work after crossing the King Hussein Bridge?

No. Jordanian packages do not include data beyond the kingdom’s borders, so the line drops to roaming or no service at the crossing. Buy a local option on the other side, or use a multi-country travel eSIM that covers both networks.

Town and country signal in Jordan

Signal almost never reaches into the canyon. The narrow canyon blocks mobile signal on all three networks, though the visitor centre and Wadi Musa town are well covered. Download the offline map and any audio guides before you enter, and expect photos to upload once you are back in town.

Will a Jordan eSIM work in the southern camps and villages?

Partly. The village and a number of camps have workable signal — Zain Jordan is generally the strongest there — but the open country beyond them is a dead zone. Save your camp’s location offline before leaving the highway, and do not count on streaming at a remote camp.

How much should I budget for a Jordan eSIM?

Recent advertised figures run from about JOD 7 for Umniah’s online-only 35 GB short-stay tier to roughly JOD 30 for Orange’s 60 GB line. Mid-range visitors’ lines cluster near JOD 10–15. Prices move with promotions, so treat them as ranges and confirm at the counter.

Do airport kiosks in Jordan check ID?

Yes — registration at the point of sale is mandatory for everyone, tourists included, and the vendor scans your passport into the system. The process takes minutes and the line usually activates on the spot, so there is no separate government office visit.

Jordan eSIM top-ups

You can top up online: All three carriers accept card payments in their apps and websites, so you can extend a package before or during the trip. Do it before your line expires, since reactivating a dormant number is slower than topping up in time.

Jordan APN settings, carrier by carrier

internet, with username and password left blank — zain works on some devices as an alternative. Orange Jordan uses internet and Umniah uses net. Carrier and travel eSIM profiles normally arrive preconfigured, so touch these settings only if data fails.

Where 5G lands in Jordan

Aqaba, yes in patches — Umniah has rolled 5G across hotspots including the seaport and airport, and the other carriers cover the city centre. Petra and Wadi Musa remain primarily 4G, which at Jordanian speeds is plenty for maps, bookings and messaging.

Can I use a Jordan eSIM and keep my home number?

Yes, and it is the standard setup. Keep the home SIM live for calls and verification codes, point mobile data at the Jordan eSIM, and disable data roaming on the home line so background traffic does not bill you. Remember that most travel profiles cannot receive SMS.
