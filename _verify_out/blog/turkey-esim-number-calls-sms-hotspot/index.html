IMSI vs MSISDN: Why Most eSIMs Are Data-Only

Most travel eSIMs are data-only because they use an IMSI tied to a wholesale roaming agreement, not an MSISDN assigned to a verified local subscriber. Understanding this difference explains every limitation on this page.

How a Turkey eSIM Connects to a Local Network

Your travel eSIM has an IMSI. It is a unique identifier tied to the provider’s wholesale agreement with a Turkish carrier. When your phone connects to Turkcell, Vodafone or Türk Telekom, the carrier authenticates the IMSI and routes the data session.

Because you are not a local subscriber, the carrier does not assign you an MSISDN. A Turkish phone number starting with +90 5XX is allocated to verified local subscribers. Assigning one to a roaming IMSI requires the provider to complete passport registration on your behalf. Most travel eSIM providers cannot do this.

What the GSMA Specification Says About Turkey eSIM Voice

The GSMA SGP.22 eSIM specification defines how eSIM profiles are provisioned and switched. It does not mandate voice or SMS support. Those are optional features. This is why the same eSIM technology can deliver a full-featured plan in one country and a data-only plan in another.

When You Actually Need a Turkish Phone Number

A Turkish +90 number is not required for most tourist use. You need one only if you must receive SMS from Turkish banks or government services, call Turkish landlines that do not accept VoIP, call businesses that screen by caller ID, register for local services that require a Turkish number, or use Turkish apps that verify by SMS.

What a Turkish Number Cannot Do

  • It does not avoid the BTK block on foreign eSIM platforms.
  • It does not remove the 120-day IMEI registration clock.
  • It does not give you access to foreign eSIM provider apps from inside Turkey. Read the block and IMEI rules for the full regulatory picture.

Do Virtual Numbers Actually Work for Turkish 2FA?

Some apps offer virtual numbers that can receive SMS verification codes. These are separate from your Turkey eSIM. Most Turkish banks will not send 2FA codes to a virtual number, and Turkish government services often reject virtual numbers outright.

Popular Virtual Number Services

  • Google Voice
  • TextNow
  • Burner
  • Hushed

Where Virtual Numbers Fall Short

  • Most Turkish banks will not send 2FA codes to a virtual number.
  • Turkish government services often reject virtual numbers.
  • Virtual numbers are not real +90 mobile numbers.
  • They work over data, so they depend on your Turkey eSIM connection.

What Actually Works for 2FA with Turkey eSIM

The reliable setup for bank and WhatsApp 2FA is:

  1. Keep your home SIM active for Voice & SMS.
  2. Set the Turkey eSIM as the Mobile Data line.
  3. Disable “Allow Mobile Data Switching.”
  4. Your home number receives SMS over roaming.

You do not need a Turkish phone number to make calls in Turkey. You need a data connection and the right app. VoIP apps handle voice and video calls over your Turkey eSIM data connection.

International Calls from a Turkey eSIM

Calling out of Turkey splits the same way. A data-only Turkey eSIM has no phone number, so it cannot place conventional international calls at all — but over data, apps like WhatsApp and Skype handle international dialing to real phone numbers, often free app-to-app and cheap per-minute to landlines with credit. If you carry a local Turkish SIM with a real number, conventional international calls work but at Turkish carrier rates, which are not designed to be cheap. The practical pattern for most travellers: keep your home SIM reachable for inbound calls, and make outbound international calls over the eSIM’s data with an app. If you decide you do need a real Turkish number, local carriers sell tourist voice packs online as well as in city stores — compare those against the product types above before paying.

Is VoIP Blocked in Turkey?

VoIP apps are widely used in Turkey. WhatsApp, Telegram and FaceTime work over data. Some corporate networks and hotels block VoIP ports. On Turkish mobile networks, VoIP is not blocked by the BTK. The BTK block targets eSIM provider platforms, not VoIP services.

How Does Wi-Fi Calling Work Over an eSIM?

If your home carrier supports Wi-Fi Calling (VoWiFi) and allows it in Turkey, you can use your Turkey eSIM data connection to make and receive calls and SMS on your home number. This is the closest thing to a native number experience on a data-only Turkey eSIM.

How Wi-Fi Calling Routes Over Data

  1. Home SIM remains active for Voice & SMS.
  2. Turkey eSIM handles Mobile Data.
  3. Wi-Fi Calling routes calls and SMS over the data connection.
  4. You can call home numbers, receive bank SMS and answer calls as if on your home network.

Wi-Fi Calling Requirements

Satellite SOS: a Nice Backup, Not a Plan

iPhone 14 and newer support Emergency SOS via satellite. Turkey may not have satellite partner coverage. Do not rely on it as a primary safety tool.

SMS and 2FA Dual-SIM Workaround

A data-only Turkey eSIM has no MSISDN and cannot receive SMS. The fix is simple: keep your home SIM active for Voice and SMS, set the Turkey eSIM for Mobile Data, and disable mobile data switching.

How Dual-SIM SMS Delivery Works with Turkey eSIM

When both a home SIM and a Turkey eSIM are installed, your phone supports two active lines. SMS is delivered to the line associated with the recipient number.

Why Unlimited Turkey eSIM Plans Restrict Hotspot

Tethering consumes more data than phone-only use. A laptop running video calls, cloud sync and web browsing can consume 3–5 GB per day. Providers cap hotspot to prevent disproportionate network use.

How Providers Detect Turkey eSIM Hotspot

  • TTL inspection — tethered traffic has a different TTL
  • User-Agent analysis — desktop browsers send different headers
  • Data volume patterns — sudden spikes
  • Deep packet inspection — identifying desktop OS traffic

Some providers throttle or block hotspot when detected. Check plan terms.

FAQ: Turkey eSIM Number, Calls, SMS and Hotspot

Can I get a Turkish phone number with an eSIM?

Yes, but only through a local Turkish carrier eSIM from Turkcell, Vodafone Turkey or Türk Telekom. Purchasing requires in-person registration with a passport. Travel eSIMs are data-only.

Does Turkey eSIM support SMS?

No. Data-only travel eSIMs cannot send or receive SMS. Keep your home SIM active for SMS verification codes and bank alerts.

Can I make calls with a Turkey eSIM?

Yes, using VoIP apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, Skype or FaceTime over your data connection. Native cellular calls require a local Turkish SIM with a phone number.

Does Turkey eSIM work with WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp works over a Turkey eSIM data connection. You can make voice and video calls, send messages and share media.

Can I use Turkey eSIM for 2FA?

Your Turkey eSIM cannot receive SMS, but your home SIM can. Keep home SIM active for Voice & SMS and set the Turkey eSIM for Mobile Data.

Does Turkey eSIM support hotspot?

Most fixed-data plans allow hotspot. Some unlimited plans restrict or cap tethering. Check plan details before purchasing.

Does Wi-Fi Calling work over a Turkey eSIM?

It can, if your home carrier supports Wi-Fi Calling and allows it in Turkey. Your phone routes calls and SMS over the Turkey eSIM data connection. Check with your home carrier before departure. See Apple’s Wi-Fi Calling support documentation for iPhone setup steps.

What is the best hotspot plan for a family in Turkey?

Look for a plan with unrestricted hotspot and at least 20 GB. Saily and Roami allow unrestricted hotspot. Holafly caps hotspot at 1 GB/day.

What is the best setup for receiving 2FA in Turkey?

Keep your home SIM active for Voice & SMS in the physical slot, set the Turkey eSIM as the Mobile Data line, and disable Allow Mobile Data Switching.

Can I use a Turkey eSIM for hotspot and still receive SMS?

Yes. Keep your home SIM active for Voice & SMS. The Turkey eSIM handles data and hotspot. SMS arrives on your home number.

Which local Turkish carrier has the best tourist voice plan?

Türk Telekom is cheapest at 420 TL (~$9) for 25 GB, 750 minutes and 750 SMS. Vodafone offers 20 GB, 750 minutes and 1,000 SMS with stronger English support. Turkcell has the best coverage but costs ~$38.

Can I use a virtual number for Turkish bank 2FA?

Most Turkish banks will not send 2FA codes to virtual numbers. Use your home SIM or a local Turkish SIM.

Is VoIP legal in Turkey?

Yes. WhatsApp, Telegram and FaceTime work over data. Some corporate networks block VoIP ports, but Turkish mobile networks do not block VoIP.

What happens to my home SIM when I use a Turkey eSIM?

Yes. The Turkey eSIM handles data and hotspot regardless of which network you connect to. Keep your home SIM active for Voice & SMS and disable mobile data switching to avoid roaming charges.
