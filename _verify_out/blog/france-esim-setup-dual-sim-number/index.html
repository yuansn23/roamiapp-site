France eSIM Device Compatibility: Which Phones Work

Key takeaways: Completing a France eSIM setup takes under five minutes, and data-only eSIMs require no passport registration, no visit to a carrier store, and no French address. The single most important step is installing the eSIM on home Wi-Fi before departure, and the second is turning off data roaming on your home SIM to prevent accidental charges. Most travelers do not need a French phone number — a data-only eSIM plus WhatsApp covers practical needs. Orange Travel is the only eSIM that includes a genuine French phone number, and it costs more for that convenience.

Not every phone supports eSIM. Before purchasing a France eSIM, verify that your device is compatible — our eSIM compatible devices list covers every supported iPhone, Samsung, Pixel, and other model. The eSIM standard has been adopted across most flagship phones released since 2018, but budget and older models may lack support.

All iPhones from the XS onward support dual SIM with one physical SIM and one eSIM, or dual eSIM on newer models. The iPhone 14 and later US models support dual eSIM with no physical SIM slot. For France eSIM iPhone setup, the process is identical across all compatible models.

For official details and the full compatibility list, refer to Apple’s eSIM support documentation at Apple Support — Using eSIM on iPhone.

Pre-Trip France eSIM Installation: Buy and Install Before Departure

The single most important step in your France eSIM setup is completing the installation before you leave. Installing an eSIM requires an internet connection to download the profile. Doing this at home on Wi-Fi eliminates the risk of arriving in France without connectivity to install the eSIM in the first place.

Step 1: Purchase the eSIM Online

Every major eSIM provider operates entirely online. Here is the standard purchase flow:

  1. Go to the provider’s website and select France as your destination
  2. Choose a data plan based on your trip length and expected usage
  3. Enter your email address and complete payment
  4. Receive an email with your eSIM QR code and France eSIM installation instructions

Payment is processed in USD or EUR depending on the provider. Most accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Plans are delivered within seconds to minutes after payment confirmation.

Can I buy a France eSIM online before my trip? Yes, and you should. Buying in advance locks in the price, gives you time to test the installation, and ensures you have connectivity immediately upon arrival. There is no penalty for installing before activation — the plan’s validity clock starts when the eSIM connects to a French network, not when you install it.

Step 2: Verify Your Installation Email

The email from your eSIM provider contains:

  • A QR code image (scan this in your phone settings)
  • An SM-DP+ address and activation code (alternative manual entry method)
  • APN settings (save these for later)
  • Your plan details: data allowance, validity period, and network partner

Do not delete this email until your trip is over. You may need the QR code again if you reset your phone or switch devices.

Step 3: Connect to Wi-Fi

Before scanning the QR code, ensure you are connected to a stable Wi-Fi network. The eSIM profile downloads during installation. A dropped connection can cause the download to fail, requiring a new QR code from the provider.

Step 4: Scan the QR Code and Complete Installation

Follow the generic steps below. Detailed instructions for France eSIM iPhone and France eSIM Android setup follow in later sections.

  • Open your phone’s cellular or mobile network settings
  • Select “Add eSIM” or “Add Mobile Plan”
  • Scan the QR code from your email
  • Label the new plan as “France Data” for easy identification
  • Complete the installation

If your phone does not recognize the QR code, most providers offer an alternative activation method using an SM-DP+ address and confirmation code that you enter manually.

Step 5: Keep the France eSIM Disabled Until You Land

After installation, the eSIM profile sits on your phone in an inactive state. Turn it off until you arrive in France. On iPhone, go to Settings > Cellular and toggle the France eSIM off. On Android, go to Settings > Connections > SIM Manager and deactivate the France eSIM.

On the tarmac at Charles de Gaulle or Orly, enable the eSIM, turn on data roaming for that line, and your connection activates within seconds. This is the moment your France eSIM activation completes.

For travelers preferring a guided setup experience, the France eSIM guide on this site provides a visual walkthrough of the process.

France eSIM iPhone Setup: Step-by-Step Installation Guide

Apple’s eSIM implementation is the most streamlined across all smartphone brands. The setup flow is consistent across iPhone models, with minor variations depending on iOS version. This France eSIM iPhone guide covers the complete installation process.

Installing the eSIM Profile on iPhone

  1. Open Settings > Cellular (labeled “Mobile Data” in some regions)
  2. Tap Add eSIM (or “Add Cellular Plan”)
  3. A camera viewfinder appears — position the QR code from your email within the frame
  4. iOS automatically detects the eSIM profile and displays plan details
  5. Tap Add Cellular Plan
  6. If prompted for a confirmation code, enter the one from your email (usually not required for standard eSIMs)
  7. The eSIM profile downloads and installs — this takes 10-30 seconds on a stable Wi-Fi connection

Naming Your eSIM

After installation, iOS asks you to label the plan. Choose “France Data” or “France eSIM” — something you will recognize when managing multiple plans. You can change this label later in Settings > Cellular > [plan name] > Cellular Plan Label.

Setting Default Lines

iOS prompts you to configure which line to use by default:

  • Default Voice Line: Choose your home SIM if you want to make and receive calls on your regular number. Choose the France eSIM if you plan to use a French phone number for local calls.
  • Default Data Line: Choose the France eSIM. This routes all mobile data through the French network.
  • iMessage and FaceTime: You can receive iMessages on both numbers if you enable both lines.

Enabling Data Roaming

The France eSIM requires data roaming to be turned on at the eSIM level:

  1. Go to Settings > Cellular
  2. Tap the France eSIM plan
  3. Toggle Data Roaming ON
  4. Verify that Enable 5G is set to “Auto” or “5G On”

Activating Upon Arrival

When your plane lands in France:

  1. Turn off Airplane Mode
  2. Swipe into Control Center and toggle Cellular Data ON
  3. If you toggled the France eSIM off earlier, go to Settings > Cellular and toggle it back on
  4. Wait 30-60 seconds for the phone to register on the French network
  5. The carrier name (Orange, SFR, Bouygues, or Free, depending on your provider) appears at the top left

If no carrier name appears after 60 seconds, go to Settings > Cellular > Network Selection, turn off Automatic, and manually select Orange or the network your eSIM uses. This is a common France eSIM activation step for troubleshooting.

Installing Without QR Code (Manual Entry on iPhone)

If you cannot scan the QR code — for example, if you purchased the eSIM on a different device — iPhone supports manual entry:

  1. Tap Add eSIM then Use Details to Add Plan
  2. Enter the SM-DP+ address from your email (looks like “SM-DP+: rsp.airalo.com”)
  3. Enter the activation code if provided
  4. Tap Next to download and install

This method is useful when you purchase an eSIM on your laptop and need to activate it on your phone without displaying a QR code on screen. For official step-by-step guidance, see Apple’s support page on installing an eSIM on iPhone.

France eSIM Android Setup: Step-by-Step Installation Guide

Android eSIM installation varies slightly depending on whether you use a Samsung, Google Pixel, OnePlus, or other device. The underlying process is the same, but menu labels differ by manufacturer and Android version. This France eSIM Android guide covers the most common devices.

Samsung Galaxy Setup

Samsung’s eSIM implementation is accessed through the SIM Manager:

  1. Open Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager
  2. Tap Add eSIM
  3. On the “Add eSIM” screen, tap Scan QR code (not “Download eSIM”)
  4. Scan the QR code from your email
  5. Confirm the plan details and tap Add
  6. The eSIM profile downloads — this takes 15-30 seconds
  7. Once installed, tap the eSIM to configure it

After installation, go back to SIM Card Manager and:

  • Set the France eSIM as Primary for Mobile Data
  • Leave your home SIM as Primary for Calls and SMS (or switch if you have a French number)
  • Enable Data Roaming for the France eSIM

Samsung phones running One UI 5.0 and later also support dual eSIM, allowing two eSIMs alongside the physical SIM.

Google Pixel Setup

Pixel phones use the standard Android eSIM flow:

  1. Open Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs
  2. Tap Add eSIM (or the “+” icon)
  3. Tap Download a SIM instead? when prompted about carrier activation
  4. Tap Next, then scan the QR code
  5. The eSIM installs — typically in under 20 seconds
  6. The new SIM appears in the SIMs list, labeled as “Downloaded SIM”

To configure:

  • Tap the France eSIM entry
  • Toggle Use SIM ON
  • Enable Data Roaming
  • Set it as preferred for Mobile Data

Pixel phones running Android 13 and later show a notification when an eSIM is installed, allowing you to configure it directly from the notification shade.

OnePlus Setup

OnePlus devices starting with the OnePlus 11 use this flow:

  1. Open Settings > Mobile Network > SIM Management
  2. Tap Add eSIM
  3. Scan the QR code or enter details manually
  4. The profile installs within 30 seconds
  5. Name the profile and configure data routing

OnePlus OxygenOS 14 and later support dual eSIM with dual standby, meaning both eSIMs can receive calls and SMS simultaneously.

Common Android Setup Issues

“QR code not recognized”: Android camera apps sometimes fail to detect eSIM QR codes. Use the built-in scanner in SIM Manager instead of the standard camera app.

“Can’t download eSIM”: Ensure you are on Wi-Fi, not mobile data. Some Android versions require Wi-Fi for the initial eSIM profile download.

“No option to add eSIM”: Your phone model or carrier variant may not support eSIM. Contact your carrier to verify, or check your IMEI against the GSMA eSIM compatibility database.

If you are testing eSIM compatibility before your trip, a trial eSIM works on both iPhone and Android and confirms whether your specific device can install and activate eSIM profiles.

France eSIM Dual SIM Configuration: Home SIM and Travel eSIM Together

The advantage of eSIM technology is that you can keep your home SIM active for calls and SMS while using the France eSIM for mobile data. This France eSIM dual SIM setup eliminates the need to remove your physical SIM or carry a second phone. Here is how to configure it correctly on both platforms.

What Dual SIM with eSIM Means

In a France eSIM dual SIM configuration with one physical SIM and one eSIM:

  • Home SIM (primary line): Handles voice calls and SMS from your regular number. You remain reachable at your usual phone number.
  • France eSIM (secondary line): Handles all mobile data traffic. Internet, app usage, maps, and streaming go through the French network.
  • Both lines active simultaneously: Both SIMs remain on and connected to their respective networks. You can receive a call on your home SIM while using data on the France eSIM.

iPhone Dual SIM Configuration

  1. Install the France eSIM following the steps above
  2. Go to Settings > Cellular
  3. You see both lines listed: “Primary” (your home SIM) and “France Data” (the eSIM)
  4. Tap Cellular Data and select the France eSIM
  5. Tap Default Voice Line and select Primary (your home number)
  6. Scroll down and tap each line to configure individual settings:

For your home SIM:

  • Data Roaming: OFF (you want data on the France eSIM only)
  • Enable 5G / VoLTE: As preferred

For the France eSIM:

  • Data Roaming: ON (required for the eSIM to connect)
  • Enable 5G: Set to Auto

iOS allows you to choose which line to use for iMessage and FaceTime. You can enable both lines, meaning iMessage works with your regular number and the France eSIM number (if it has one).

Call forwarding between lines: iPhone does not automatically forward calls between the home SIM and eSIM. If someone calls your home number, it rings on your home SIM. If you want calls forwarded, set up call forwarding with your home carrier before departure.

Android Dual SIM Configuration

Android’s dual SIM management varies by manufacturer, but the core settings are consistent.

Samsung Galaxy

  1. Open Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager
  2. Under “SIM cards,” you see SIM 1 (physical) and SIM 2 (eSIM)
  3. Tap Mobile Data and select SIM 2 (the France eSIM)
  4. Tap Preferred SIM for Calls — select SIM 1 (home) or “Ask every time”
  5. Tap Preferred SIM for SMS — select SIM 1 (home)

For each SIM, tap its entry to toggle data roaming and network mode individually.

Google Pixel

  1. Open Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs
  2. Tap the France eSIM entry
  3. Toggle Data Roaming ON
  4. Go back and tap your home SIM — ensure Data Roaming is OFF
  5. Under “Preferred SIMs,” configure:
    • Calls: Home SIM
    • SMS: Home SIM
    • Mobile Data: France eSIM

Preventing Unexpected Roaming Charges

The most common issue with dual SIM travel is accidental data usage on the home SIM. Here is how to prevent it:

  • Turn off data roaming on your home SIM. This is the single most important setting. Even if your phone shows the France eSIM as the default data line, some apps may route data through the home SIM under certain conditions (MMS downloads, carrier update checks).
  • Disable “Allow Cellular Data Switching” on iPhone. Go to Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data > turn OFF “Allow Data Switching.” This prevents iOS from automatically routing data through your home SIM if the eSIM signal weakens. Without this toggle off, your phone may switch to your home SIM in a tunnel or elevator and incur roaming charges.
  • On Android, set data to the eSIM only. In SIM Manager, explicitly set the France eSIM as the exclusive data SIM. Do not set it to “Ask every time.”
  • Disable MMS auto-download on your home SIM. On iPhone: Settings > Messages > turn OFF “MMS Messaging.” On Android: Messages app > Settings > Advanced > turn OFF “Auto-download MMS.” Group messages containing images can trigger MMS downloads over your home SIM.

Dual SIM Calling Behavior

When your home SIM is set as the default voice line:

  • Outgoing calls use your home number by default. You can manually switch to the France eSIM for a specific call (on iPhone: tap the “Calls” button during dialing; on Samsung: tap the SIM selector before dialing).
  • Incoming calls arrive on the line they were placed to. Both lines ring independently.
  • Voicemail is handled per line. Your home number voicemail remains unchanged.

A eSIM for France configured as the data line with your home SIM handling calls is the optimal setup for most travelers. It keeps your regular number active for banking verification codes, two-factor authentication, and family calls while all internet traffic routes through the French network.

France eSIM APN Settings for Orange, SFR, Bouygues and Free

Most eSIM providers configure APN settings automatically during installation. When this works correctly, you never touch APN settings. However, certain providers — particularly those using Orange or Free Mobile networks — may require manual APN entry, especially on Android devices. This is a key part of Orange eSIM setup for travelers using Orange-connected plans.

When Manual APN Configuration Is Necessary

  • Your eSIM is installed and shows signal but has no data connection
  • Internet works on some apps but not others
  • You see “4G” or “5G” in the status bar but cannot load web pages
  • MMS (picture messages) do not send or receive
  • Visual voicemail fails to connect

If any of these occur, manual APN configuration typically resolves the issue.

How to Access APN Settings

iPhone

  1. Go to Settings > Cellular
  2. Tap the France eSIM plan
  3. Tap Cellular Data Network
  4. Enter the APN in the “APN” field (username and password are typically blank)
  5. Tap Back to save

The APN field appears only when you have selected the correct eSIM line. If you do not see “Cellular Data Network,” the eSIM may not be active or properly installed.

Android (Samsung)

  1. Go to Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager
  2. Tap the France eSIM entry
  3. Tap Access Point Names
  4. Tap Add (or the “+” icon)
  5. Enter the APN and tap the menu > Save
  6. Select the new APN as active

Android (Google Pixel)

  1. Go to Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs
  2. Tap the France eSIM
  3. Tap Access Point Names
  4. Tap Add and enter the APN details

Orange is France’s largest network and the most common partner for eSIM providers. The Orange APN (orange) works on both Orange and Orange Travel eSIMs for data. However, Orange Travel eSIMs (which include a French phone number) use a separate APN for MMS:

Most international eSIMs on the Orange network use orange as the APN and do not require MMS configuration since they are data-only.

Verifying APN Settings

After entering APN values, test the connection:

  1. Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then OFF
  2. Open a browser and load any website

France eSIM Setup on iPhone vs Android: What Actually Differs

The two platforms expose the same settings under different names, and that naming gap causes more failed setups than any network problem. On iPhone the France eSIM appears under Settings > Cellular, and the data line is chosen there; the eSIM’s own Data Roaming switch lives one level deeper, on the line itself. On Android the equivalent controls sit in Settings > Connections > SIM Manager, and the data line is labelled Mobile Data rather than Cellular Data. An eSIM profile can install successfully and still show no data until the roaming toggle on that specific line is on — a state that looks like a dead eSIM but is a two-tap fix.

One hardware difference worth knowing: iPhone models sold in mainland China have no eSIM hardware at all, so a France eSIM cannot be installed on them regardless of settings. Android support is fragmented by manufacturer — Google Pixel and recent Samsung Galaxy flagships support eSIM, while several OnePlus and Xiaomi models do not, and imported or region-locked handsets may lack the French network bands even when eSIM itself works.

Most travelers do not need a French phone number. Data-only eSIMs work with WhatsApp, iMessage, and all internet-based communication. If you need a French number for SNCF, Navigo, or restaurant reservations, Orange Travel is the only carrier eSIM that includes one. A France eSIM with phone number costs more than data-only alternatives.

Does my France eSIM work as soon as I land?

Yes, if you installed the eSIM before departure. When your plane lands in France, turn on the eSIM and enable data roaming. The eSIM connects to a French network (Orange, SFR, Bouygues, or Free) within 30-60 seconds. Your France eSIM activation completes automatically upon first network connection.

What APN settings do I need for my France eSIM?

Most eSIMs configure APN settings automatically. If you need manual entry, use the provider’s APN (e.g., “globaldata” for Airalo, “hola” for Holafly, “ubigi” for Ubigi). For Orange network eSIMs, use “orange” as the APN. Orange Travel eSIMs use “orange.ovh” with username “orange.”

Why is my France eSIM not connecting?

If your France eSIM not connecting, check that data roaming is enabled for the eSIM, APN settings are correct, and you are in a coverage area. Try toggling Airplane Mode on and off, or manually select Orange or another network in Network Selection. Restart your phone if the issue persists.

Can I use my France eSIM to make calls and send texts?

Data-only eSIMs do not support calls or SMS. Use WhatsApp, FaceTime, or Google Voice for calls over data. Orange Travel eSIMs include a French phone number with voice and SMS capability.

Do I need to register my passport to use a France eSIM?

No, data-only travel eSIMs from providers like Airalo, Ubigi, and Holafly do not require passport registration. Orange Travel does require passport upload because it issues a French phone number with voice and SMS capability, which is required by French law.
