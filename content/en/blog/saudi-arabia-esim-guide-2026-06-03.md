### VoIP and Network Restrictions

Saudi Arabia's Communications, Space and Technology Commission (CST) enforces restrictions on internet calling services. An eSIM routes data through international gateways, which sometimes provides more consistent VoIP access than a local SIM with a Saudi phone number, though this is not guaranteed.

## 2. Saudi Arabia eSIM Carrier Coverage Deep Dive: STC, Mobily, and Zain KSA

Which carrier powers your Saudi Arabia eSIM is the single most important factor for your actual connectivity. All three carriers have distinct geographical strengths, and your choice of provider determines which network your data travels on.

### STC (Saudi Telecom Company): The Coverage King

STC commands over 60% market share with 20,000+ tower sites nationwide. It was the first Middle Eastern operator to launch commercial 5G (2019), with 5G now reaching 85% of populated areas. [Opensignal's 2025 Global Network Excellence Index](https://www.opensignal.com/reports/2025/global-network-excellence) ranks Saudi Arabia first globally for mobile download speed among large-landmass countries, led by STC's average 5G speed of 252.4 Mbps.

**Coverage strengths:** Over 5,000 small cells and macro towers serve the Grand Mosque perimeter and Mina, Arafat, and Muzdalifah holy sites — the most network-dense square kilometer on Earth during Hajj. STC operates the largest indoor DAS installation within the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, ensuring connectivity in basement prayer halls. Comprehensive 5G covers Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam/Khobar/Dhahran. On intercity highways (Riyadh-Dammam 380 km, Jeddah-Madinah 410 km), STC maintains signal for over 70% of the journey — the only carrier achieving this.

**Weaknesses:** The Empty Quarter (Rub' al Khali / 鲁卜哈利沙漠) has zero coverage from any carrier. STC's signal fades 50-100 km into the desert interior.

### Mobily: Strongest Urban Competitor

Mobily, Saudi Arabia's second-largest carrier backed by UAE's Etisalat, covers approximately 90 cities with 5G. Users spend 65% of time on 5G — the highest percentage among Saudi carriers.

**Coverage strengths:** Excellent urban coverage in Riyadh, Jeddah Corniche, and Al-Balad historic district. For Hajj 2026, Mobily doubled 5G capacity in the Mashaer holy sites to 2.3 Tbps using AI-driven optimization. Competitively priced prepaid data packages.

**Weaknesses:** Coverage inside the Grand Mosque is noticeably spottier than STC during peak congestion. Rural highway blind spots of 30-60 minutes are common. At AlUla heritage sites, Mobily achieves 93% daily availability — meaning roughly 20 minutes per day without signal.

### Zain KSA: Eastern Province Specialist

Zain KSA was first to deploy 5G Standalone (SA) architecture in Riyadh and Jeddah. Users enjoy 86.4 Mbps average speed (combined 4G/5G) — the highest blended speed among Saudi operators.

**Coverage strengths:** Exceptionally strong in Dammam, Khobar, and Dhahran. Flexible short-duration plans including daily 1GB packages (approximately SAR 10 / $2.67). 5G covers 64 cities.

**Weaknesses:** Coverage gaps on intercity highways. Significantly less density than STC in Makkah and Madinah — pilgrims should exercise caution. Weakest coverage at AlUla heritage sites.

## 5. Saudi Arabia Attraction Connectivity Report: Where Signals Excel and Where They Vanish

Not all Saudi destinations are equal for mobile signal. This field-informed report covers the most-visited attractions. ### Makkah: Grand Mosque (Al-Masjid al-Haram / 麦加大清真寺)

STC has deployed the densest small-cell network on Earth within the Grand Mosque perimeter. Even surrounded by 100,000+ worshippers, STC-connected devices maintain 3-5 Mbps in the central mataf area and higher speeds in outer halls. During the five daily prayer peaks — especially Maghrib (sunset) and Isha (night) — congestion peaks. Mobily and Zain KSA exhibit more frequent data stalls. **Critical tip:** Download offline Google Maps of Makkah before entering the central mosque — GPS works without data, so you can still navigate to your hotel if data stalls.

### Madinah: Prophet's Mosque (Al-Masjid an-Nabawi / 先知寺)

STC's indoor DAS provides consistent coverage across all prayer hall levels, including the basement expansion areas where Mobily and Zain KSA signals weaken. During Ramadan Taraweeh prayers, video streaming buffers but audio streaming (Quran recitations) functions reliably.

### AlUla: Hegra, Elephant Rock, and Maraya

AlUla town has solid 4G/5G from all carriers. Hegra archaeological site (22 km north) is a different story — STC maintains usable 4G at the entrance and first tomb clusters (including Qasr al-Farid), but signal becomes intermittent deeper into the site. Mobily and Zain KSA drop off more quickly. Local guide wisdom: 来AlUla一定要用STC卡 (when visiting AlUla, you must use an STC network). Elephant Rock has decent STC coverage with a café offering backup Wi-Fi. **Practical advice:** Download offline maps covering the entire AlUla region before departing town.

### Riyadh and Jeddah

Both cities offer excellent multi-carrier 5G. Riyadh's Diriyah UNESCO site, Kingdom Centre observation deck, and Boulevard Riyadh City all have strong coverage, though Boulevard experiences congestion on weekend evenings (Thursday-Friday). Jeddah's 30 km Corniche has full coverage; Al-Balad's narrow historic streets favor STC. Red Sea diving trips lose signal 5-8 km from shore — download reference materials before departing the marina.

### The Empty Quarter (Rub' al Khali / 鲁卜哈利沙漠): Zero Signal

The Empty Quarter desert — 650,000 square kilometers, roughly the size of France — has absolutely no cellular signal from any carrier. This is not a coverage gap; it is a complete infrastructure absence. For desert excursions, arrange satellite communication (Garmin inReach or satellite phone), download offline maps with 50+ km buffer zones, and inform contacts of your itinerary.

## 6. How to Buy and Install Saudi Arabia eSIM Before Departure

eSIM installation takes under five minutes. Complete everything before departure to land with data ready. Getting it set up correctly before you fly is the key to a seamless arrival experience.

### Step 1: Verify Device Compatibility

Dial `*#06#` on your phone. If a 32-digit EID number appears, your device supports eSIM. Compatible models include iPhone XS/XR and newer (except mainland China/Hong Kong/Macau models), Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer, and Google Pixel 3 and newer. Confirm your specific phone model supports eSIM before purchasing. **Critical:** Your phone must be carrier-unlocked.

### Step 2: Purchase Your Plan

Visit your chosen provider's website or app, search "Saudi Arabia," select your plan (reference Sections 3-4), apply code **web20** for Roami purchases, and complete payment. After purchase, you receive a QR code by email and within the app.

### Step 3: Install While Connected to Wi-Fi

Go to Settings > Cellular/Mobile > Add eSIM. Scan the QR code or use one-tap installation from the provider's app. Label the eSIM "Saudi Arabia eSIM." Keep your home SIM set as the data line — do not switch yet. If running two SIMs simultaneously, check our [dual eSIM troubleshooting guide](/faq/Dual-eSIM-Not-Working-12-Fixes-for-iPhone/) for managing both lines. iPhone users can follow our dedicated [step-by-step iPhone eSIM activation guide](/faq/how-to-activate-esim-on-iphone/) for model-specific instructions with screenshots.

### Step 4: Configure Before Departure

Toggle the eSIM OFF after installation. Leaving it on triggers failed connection attempts before you reach Saudi Arabia. Data Roaming must be enabled, but only toggle it on after landing.

### Step 5: Activate Upon Arrival

After landing, toggle the eSIM ON, enable Data Roaming for the eSIM line, and switch Cellular Data from home SIM to the travel eSIM. Connection establishes within 30-60 seconds. If no connection after 2 minutes: toggle Airplane Mode for 10 seconds, verify Data Roaming and data line settings, manually try STC as the network, or restart your phone — most issues resolve within 2-3 minutes.

### Data Management Tips

Reference daily usage: WhatsApp text 5-10 MB, voice notes (10/day) 30-50 MB, Maps (1 hour) 20-40 MB, social browsing (1 hour) 100-200 MB, YouTube 1080p (10 min) 200-300 MB. Save data by downloading offline maps on Wi-Fi, setting Instagram to Data Saver mode, disabling background app refresh, and using hotel Wi-Fi for video and downloads.

## 7. FAQ: Saudi Arabia eSIM

No. Travel eSIMs are data-only — you do not receive a Saudi number. WhatsApp, Telegram, and other messaging apps work normally linked to your home number. Most travelers find a data-only Saudi Arabia eSIM sufficient since messaging and ride-hailing apps do not require a local number.

**Q: Can I use WhatsApp voice and video calls?**
