## How to Activate Your USA eSIM

All three providers support **instant eSIM activation** before you leave home. Here’s the general process:

1. Purchase a plan online (or via the provider’s app).
2. You’ll receive a **QR code** or an **installation link** via email.
3. On iPhone: go to **Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM** and scan the QR code.  
   On Android: **Settings > Connections > SIM Manager > Add eSIM**.
4. Label your eSIM (e.g., “USA Data”) and set it as the default for cellular data.
5. Upon landing in the US, your eSIM will automatically connect – no need to find a store.

> 💡 **Pro tip**: Install your eSIM before departure while you still have Wi‑Fi. It will activate as soon as you connect to a US network.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an unlocked phone for a USA eSIM?
Yes. Your phone must be **carrier‑unlocked** to use any eSIM from a different provider.

### Can I keep my home SIM active while using a US eSIM?
Absolutely. Set the US eSIM for **Cellular Data** and your home SIM for **Voice & SMS** (dual SIM dual standby).

### Is 5G available everywhere in the US?
Not yet. While 5G covers most cities and highways, many rural areas still use 4G LTE. All providers fall back to 4G seamlessly.
