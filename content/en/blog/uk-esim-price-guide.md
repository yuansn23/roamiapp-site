## What Hidden Fees Do UK eSIM Plans Have?

UK eSIM plans can carry seven types of hidden fees that inflate costs beyond the headline price: activation fees, top-up minimums, speed throttling after data thresholds, 5G access restrictions, hotspot tethering blocks, currency conversion fees, and restrictive refund policies. Some providers charge a separate activation or "connection" fee on top of the plan price. Always check the total at checkout. Roami and most major travel eSIM providers don't charge activation fees, but smaller resellers sometimes do — typically GBP 1-3. It's a trivial amount, but annoying on principle.

**2. Top-up minimums**

**3. Speed throttling after "unlimited" thresholds**
This is the big one. "Unlimited" plans from many providers throttle your speed to 128kbps or 256kbps after a certain daily or total data threshold. At those speeds, you can send WhatsApp messages but you cannot load a web page or use maps effectively. The plan effectively becomes unusable for most of your trip. This is covered in depth in the [UK unlimited data guide](/blog/uk-esim-unlimited-data-guide/).

**4. 5G access restrictions**
Not all plans include 5G. Some budget plans cap you at 4G, which is fine for most tasks but noticeably slower in congested areas like central London train stations at rush hour. If 5G matters to you, check that it's explicitly included.

**5. Hotspot / tethering blocks**
Some providers, particularly Holafly and some unlimited plans, block or restrict hotspot tethering. If you plan to use your phone as a hotspot for a laptop or tablet, confirm tethering is allowed before buying. Discovering this block mid-trip is infuriating.

**6. Currency conversion fees**
Most travel eSIM providers price their plans in USD or EUR even for UK plans. If your bank charges foreign transaction fees, your actual cost will be a few percent higher than the listed price. Local MVNOs (giffgaff, Lebara, SMARTY) price in GBP, so there's no currency surprise there — but for travel eSIMs, expect USD.

**7. Refund policies**
Most eSIM providers do not offer refunds after activation. Some offer a 24-hour money-back guarantee if you haven't used any data. Read the refund policy before buying, especially if you're buying your eSIM weeks before your trip. Plans change. Circumstances change. Don't assume you can get your money back.

**Can I use one eSIM across Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland?**
Yes — and paying twice is an easy mistake to make. UK networks price by data allowance rather than by nation, so a plan bought for London works unchanged in Belfast, Cardiff or the Highlands. The only genuine boundary is the Republic of Ireland, which sits outside the UK.

**Do I need to buy an eSIM before I land in the UK?**
Yes, it's strongly recommended. Airport kiosks at Heathrow charge 100-200% more than online prices. Install your eSIM at home on WiFi before departure, and it'll activate automatically when you land.
