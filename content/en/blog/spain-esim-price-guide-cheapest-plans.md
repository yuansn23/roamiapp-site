Finding the cheapest Spain eSIM for your trip starts with understanding how pricing works. This price guide compares plans from 1GB to 50GB across local carriers and international providers, so you can find the best value for your specific travel needs.

Spain eSIM prices range from EUR 1.50 for a 1GB/7-day plan to EUR 50 for a 50GB/30-day package. Key pricing factors:

- **Provider type:** Local carriers EUR 0.50-2/GB vs international EUR 1-3/GB
- **Data tier:** Larger allowances reduce per-GB cost (up to 70% savings)
- **Duration:** Longer terms reduce daily cost
- **Network priority:** Full 5G and multi-carrier costs more
- **EU roaming:** Adds EUR 1-5 to plan price

According to [CNMC](https://www.cnmc.es), the Spanish telecom regulator, the mobile market is highly competitive. For carrier network comparison, see our [Spain network guide](/blog/spain-esim-movistar-orange-vodafone-comparison/). The cheapest per-GB rate comes from local carriers at EUR 0.30/GB. Understanding these variables helps you avoid overpaying for data you do not need.

## Spain eSIM Prepaid: Local Carrier Pricing Explained

Spain has four major mobile network operators — Movistar, Orange, Vodafone, and Yoigo — along with several mobile virtual network operators (MVNOs) such as Lowi, Finetwork, Pepephone, and Digi. Local carriers offer prepaid Spain eSIM plans that typically provide the lowest per-GB pricing available in the market, especially for longer durations. Movistar, for example, offers prepaid data packs at approximately EUR 0.80-1.50 per GB for 30-day plans, while Yoigo and Digi can go as low as EUR 0.50 per GB on their highest-tier packages. However, local carrier eSIMs come with trade-offs. Many require in-store registration with a passport, a Spanish tax ID (NIE), or a local bank account for activation. Customer service is usually in Spanish, and while some providers offer English support through their apps, it is not guaranteed. Coverage, on the other hand, is excellent — Movistar and Orange both operate extensive 5G networks covering over 85% of the Spanish population according to [CNMC](https://www.cnmc.es) data. Local carriers typically structure their prepaid eSIM offerings in two ways: rechargeable plans where you top up as needed, and fixed-term pack plans with a set data cap that expires after a specific number of days. [Movistar](https://www.movistar.es) and [Orange](https://www.orange.es) both offer these structures through their prepaid portals, while [Vodafone](https://www.vodafone.es) and [Yoigo](https://www.yoigo.com) have streamlined app-based activation for many of their fixed-term packs. Rechargeable plans offer more flexibility for long-stay visitors, while fixed packs suit short-term travelers who want a set-it-and-forget-it option.

## Spain eSIM Plans: International Provider Pricing vs Local

International eSIM providers such as Roami, Airalo, Holafly, and Nomad source data from local carrier networks through wholesale agreements. A [free eSIM trial](/free-esim/) is available to test device compatibility before purchasing. They then resell this data in prepaid packages designed specifically for travelers. These plans typically cost more per GB than local carrier plans because they include value-added services such as English-language customer support, instant digital delivery, eSIM compatibility checks, and multi-carrier network switching. International eSIMs tend to charge EUR 1-3 per GB for Spain, with the lowest rates appearing on mid-sized plans (10-20GB) and the highest per-GB costs appearing on small 1GB plans. The convenience premium ranges from roughly 30% to 100% over local carrier pricing, depending on the specific plan and provider.

## Data Tier and Duration

The two most significant pricing factors are data tier and duration. Short plans (7 days or less) carry the highest per-GB cost because providers amortize their fixed costs — delivery, support, platform maintenance — over a smaller data allowance. A cheapest Spain eSIM 1GB plan for 7 days might cost EUR 1.50-4, which equates to a very high per-GB price. By contrast, a Spain eSIM 50GB long stay plan for 30 days might cost EUR 25-45, dropping the per-GB cost to as low as EUR 0.50-1.00. Duration also drives price because providers know that travelers on longer trips have fewer alternatives. A 30-day plan from an international provider might be only 15-20% more expensive than a 15-day plan for the same data allowance, which means the cost per day drops significantly on longer terms.

## Network Priority and Data Speeds

Not all Spain eSIM plans deliver the same network experience. Some international eSIM providers restrict data speeds to 4G or cap throughput at a certain level, while others offer full 5G access. Local carrier plans generally provide unrestricted 5G where available, but MVNOs and budget carriers sometimes deprioritize traffic during peak hours, leading to slower speeds in crowded areas like Madrid's Puerta del Sol or Barcelona's Las Ramblas. Network access is also governed by agreements between eSIM providers and local carriers. Some international eSIMs only connect to a single local network, which means if that network has poor coverage in your area, you are stuck with it. Premium multi-network providers automatically switch between Movistar, Orange, Vodafone, and Yoigo to maintain the best available connection, a feature that carries a slight pricing premium but eliminates the frustration of dropped signals.

## EU Roaming and Extra Features

If your trip includes travel outside Spain — a day trip to Portugal, a weekend in France, or a layover in another EU country — you need a plan that supports EU roaming. EU regulations mandate that roaming within the EU should not incur additional charges under the "Roam Like at Home" framework, as documented by the official [EU roaming](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/consumer-rights/roaming-tariffs/index_en.htm) guidelines. However, not all prepaid and eSIM plans comply. Some international eSIMs explicitly restrict data usage to Spain only, while others include EU roaming at no extra cost. Features like personal hotspot (tethering), eSIM transfer between devices, and multi-device support also vary by provider. If you need to share your connection with a laptop or tablet, check that the plan allows tethering before purchase.

### Where the Savings Really Are

The sharpest price-per-GB savings appear at two points: the jump from 1GB to 5GB plans and the jump from 10GB to 20GB plans. A Spain eSIM 1GB plan from an international provider might cost EUR 2 but works out to EUR 2/GB. Moving up to a 5GB plan often drops the per-GB cost by 25-35%, with some providers offering 5GB plans for as little as EUR 7-8.50. The next efficiency threshold is around 10-20GB, where per-GB cost typically falls below EUR 1.20 for international providers and below EUR 1 for local carriers. This matters because many travelers overestimate their data needs. A 1GB plan is actually sufficient for light usage — messaging apps, maps, email, and occasional social media — for 3-5 days. The cheapest [best value Spain eSIM plans](/spain-esim/) 1GB plans from multi-network providers cost around EUR 1.50-2.50, which is often the most cost-effective choice for a weekend trip to Barcelona or a short business visit to Madrid.

**Takeaway:** If you are staying 7 days or less and your data usage is light (under 1GB total), buy the smallest plan available from an international provider. If you stay longer or use more data (5GB+ per week), local carriers offer the lowest per-GB pricing in Spain, but international providers remain competitive on mid-tier 10-20GB plans and offer vastly simpler setup.
