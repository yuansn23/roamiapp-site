## Spain eSIM No Service After Arrival: Step-by-Step Fix

The most common support issue is landing at Madrid-Barajas (MAD), Barcelona-El Prat (BCN), or Malaga (AGP) airport and seeing "No Service." Your eSIM was installed before the trip, the plan shows active, but there's no mobile signal. This is almost always caused by one of three issues.

### Fix #1: Enable Data Roaming

Even with a local Spain eSIM, your phone treats it as a secondary line and disables roaming by default on secondary SIMs.

**iPhone:** Settings > Mobile Data > Mobile Data Options > toggle "Data Roaming" ON. Also enable "Allow Mobile Data Switching." Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then OFF.

**Samsung:** Settings > Connections > SIM Manager > tap your Spain eSIM > toggle "Data Roaming" ON. Also check Settings > Connections > Mobile Networks > ensure Data Roaming is ON here too (Samsung has two locations). Restart the phone.

**Google Pixel:** Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs > tap the Spain eSIM > toggle "Roaming" ON. Ensure "Mobile data" is also ON. Restart.

**Critical note for dual-SIM users:** Data roaming must be enabled specifically for the Spain eSIM line, not just globally. Check each line's individual roaming setting.

### Fix #2: Check if Your Phone Is Carrier-Locked

Your phone must be carrier-unlocked to use any eSIM from a provider other than your home carrier.

**How to check:**
- **iPhone:** Settings > General > About > look for "Carrier Lock." If it says "No SIM restrictions," you're unlocked.
- **Samsung:** Settings > Connections > SIM Manager > if you see carrier branding (e.g., "T-Mobile SIM lock"), it's likely locked.
- **Google Pixel:** Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs > tap your home SIM > check for "Network unlock" status.

**If locked:** Contact your home carrier to request an unlock. US carriers (AT&T, T-Mobile) unlock upon request after contract completion. Verizon unlocks automatically after 60 days. UK and EU carriers unlock free of charge upon request. If you're already in Spain and locked, use your home carrier's roaming plan, rent a mobile hotspot at the airport, or rely on Wi-Fi.

### Fix #3: Manual Network Selection

Some phones try to stay connected to a previous country's network rather than registering on a Spanish network.

**Fix:**
1. Settings > Mobile Data > Network Selection
2. Turn OFF "Automatic"
3. Wait 10-20 seconds for the network list to populate
4. Try **Movistar** first – it has partner agreements with most eSIM providers
5. If Movistar shows "Not Available," try **Orange**
6. Then try **Vodafone ES** (or "Vodafone Spain")
7. Then try **Yoigo** (or "Xfera" on some phones)
8. Once connected on manual selection, leave it on that network for 24 hours

**Why this works:** Your eSIM may only have roaming agreements with one or two of Spain's four carriers. Manual selection lets you try each one until you find the working network.

## Spain eSIM APN Settings Not Working: Complete Fix Guide

APN (Access Point Name) settings tell your phone how to connect to the carrier's data network. If these are missing or incorrect, you'll see full signal bars with the carrier name but no working internet.

**How to know APN is the problem:**
- Signal bars show 4G/5G with carrier name, but internet doesn't work
- iMessage/FaceTime shows "Waiting for activation"
- WhatsApp shows "Connecting" indefinitely
- Browsers show "Server not found" or "DNS error"

### Generic APN Setup Instructions

**iPhone:** Settings > Mobile Data > Mobile Data Network > enter APN > leave username/password blank unless specified > tap Save > restart.

**Samsung:** Settings > Connections > Mobile Networks > Access Point Names > tap "+" > enter Name and APN > Save > select the new APN > restart.

**Google Pixel:** Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs > tap Spain eSIM > Access Point Names > tap "+" > enter APN > Save > select it > restart.

### Provider-Specific APN Settings

| Provider | APN | Username | Password | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-Network | (auto-configured) | (blank) | (blank) | Manual entry rarely needed |
| Airalo (Movistar network) | airalo.movistar.es | (blank) | (blank) | Case-sensitive, exact format |
| Airalo (Orange network) | airalo.orange.es | (blank) | (blank) | Verify exact format |
| Holafly | holafly | (blank) | (blank) | All lowercase only |
| Nomad | nomad | (blank) | (blank) | Simple, rarely fails |
| Movistar (direct) | movistar.es | MOVISTAR | MOVISTAR | All caps for username/password |
| Orange (direct) | orange.es | orange | orange | Also works with blank credentials |
| Vodafone (direct) | vodafone.es | vodafone | vodafone | Or "vodafone ES" on some plans |
| Yoigo | yoigo | (blank) | (blank) | Usually auto-configured |

**Troubleshooting APN fields that appear greyed out (iPhone bug):**
- **Fix 1:** Insert a physical SIM from any carrier temporarily – APN fields become editable. Enter APN, then remove the SIM. The APN persists.
- **Fix 2:** Reset network settings (Settings > General > Transfer or Reset > Reset > Reset Network Settings). This erases saved Wi-Fi passwords but unblocks APN editing.
- **Fix 3:** Update to the latest iOS version – Apple has fixed APN editing bugs in recent updates.

**Critical:** Hidden spaces at the beginning or end of the APN text string will cause APN failure. Type the APN manually rather than pasting it – copy-pasting from emails can include invisible characters.

## Spain eSIM Activation Failures: Provider-Specific Fixes

Activation failures occur when the eSIM profile installs correctly but doesn't activate on the mobile network. The profile appears in SIM manager, but the carrier hasn't enabled service.

### Understanding Activation Timing

Most Spain eSIMs activate based on one of two triggers:
1. **Immediate activation:** Plan starts at purchase – valid for 30-90 days to install, but data clock starts at purchase.
2. **Activation on first data connection:** Plan activates when the eSIM first connects to a Spanish network – install in advance, activates on arrival.

**Activation failed error most often occurs with immediate activation plans installed before travel** – the 7 or 14-day validity may expire before you land. Check your plan's activation date in the provider's app or email.

### Provider-Specific Activation Issues

| Provider | Common Issue | Specific Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-Network | Auto-activates on first connection | Ensure roaming ON, wait 5 min after landing, restart |
| Airalo | May need manual activation in app | Open Airalo app > "My eSIMs" > tap "Activate" |
| Holafly | Requires Wi-Fi for initial activation | Connect to airport/hotel Wi-Fi first, install, then switch to data |
| Nomad | Delayed activation (up to 15 min) | Wait 15 min, restart, contact support if still not working |
| Movistar (direct) | Requires ID verification | Upload passport photo – may take 1-24 hours for approval |
| Orange (direct) | May need in-app activation step | Download Orange app, log in, tap "Activate my line" |
| Vodafone (direct) | May need APN after activation | Add APN from confirmation email before data works |

### eSIM Stuck on "Activating" – What to Do

If your eSIM shows "Activation in Progress" for more than 30 minutes:

1. Connect to Wi-Fi (some eSIMs need internet for the initial activation handshake)
2. Restart your phone completely
3. Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 60 seconds, then OFF
4. Check for carrier settings updates: Settings > General > About (wait 30 seconds on this screen)
5. If still stuck, delete the profile and reinstall from scratch
6. If reinstalling fails, contact support – the activation code may have expired

## Spain eSIM Slow Data Speeds: Causes and Solutions

Slow speeds are commonly caused by network congestion, incorrect band selection, or provider throttling.

### Check Your Network Type First

Before troubleshooting speed, check what network generation you're connected to:
- **iPhone:** Status bar shows "5G," "LTE," "4G," or "3G"
- **Samsung:** Settings > About Phone > Status > SIM Card Status > Mobile Network Type
- **Google Pixel:** Settings > Network & Internet > Internet > tap gear icon > Current network type

If you're on 3G (HSPA/HSPA+), maximum speed is ~5-20 Mbps. Force the phone to 4G or 5G.

### Fix #1: Network Congestion (Most Common in Tourist Areas)

During peak season (June-September) and business hours (10 AM-12 PM, 4-6 PM), Spanish mobile networks experience significant congestion.

| Location | Peak Congestion Time | Speed Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Central Madrid (business district) | 10 AM-12 PM, 4-6 PM weekdays | 40-50% drop |
| Barcelona Gothic Quarter | All day, peak tourist season | 50-60% drop |
| Costa del Sol resorts | 8 PM-11 PM, August | 60-70% drop |
| Benidorm high-rise area | 8 PM-11 PM, August | 60-70% drop |

**Fixes for congestion:**
- **Switch carriers manually** – Settings > Network Selection > turn OFF Automatic > try a different carrier. If you're on Orange, try Movistar or Vodafone. Different carriers have different congestion levels at the same location.
- **Move to a different location** – 50-100 meters can connect you to a different tower with less load.
- **Use data off-peak** – schedule heavy downloads for early morning (6-9 AM) or after 11 PM.
- **Toggle 5G OFF** – Settings > Voice & Data > select 4G. In some areas, 5G is more congested than 4G.

### Fix #2: Connected to a Distant Tower

Even with strong signal bars, data can be slow if you're connected to a distant tower.

**Fix:** Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 30 seconds, then OFF to force reconnection to the nearest tower. Walk 20-50 meters in any direction and test again.

### Fix #3: Provider Throttling

Some budget eSIM plans throttle speeds after reaching a daily data threshold – this is often hidden in the fair use policy.

**Check:** Review the fine print for "speed caps," "fair use limits," or "data deprioritization." Some "unlimited" plans throttle to 2-5 Mbps after 5-10 GB of daily use.

**Fix:** If you're being throttled, there's no technical workaround. Consider topping up with a different plan or provider.

## QR Code Problems: When Your Spain eSIM Won't Scan

| Cause | Frequency | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Screen brightness too low | 40% | Increase to 80%+ or max brightness |
| QR image distorted/damaged | 20% | Request manual activation code from provider |
| Camera lens dirty | 15% | Clean with microfiber cloth |
| Phone case blocking camera | 10% | Remove case entirely |
| QR displayed too small | 10% | Zoom in on the QR image |
| QR code expired | 5% | Contact support for new QR |

### Manual Code Entry – Universal Workaround

Every QR code contains an embedded activation code (starting with "LPA:1$" or "SM-DP+"). If scanning fails, enter this code manually.

**How to find your manual code:**
- Check the provider's confirmation email – most include the manual activation code below the QR code
- Log into the provider's app or dashboard – look for "Manual Activation" or "Activation Code"

**Manual entry on iPhone:**
1. Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM
2. Tap "Enter Details Manually" (bottom of QR scanner)
3. Enter SM-DP+ Address (URL format, from email)
4. Enter Activation Code (long alphanumeric string)
5. Tap "Continue" and follow on-screen instructions

**Manual entry on Samsung:**
1. Settings > Connections > SIM Manager > Add eSIM
2. Tap three-dot menu > "Enter activation code manually"
3. Enter SM-DP+ and activation code > "Connect"

**Manual entry on Google Pixel:**
1. Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs > Add SIM > "Download a SIM instead?"
2. On QR scanning screen, look for "Need help?" or "Enter code manually"
3. Enter full activation code including "LPA:1$" prefix if included

## Dual SIM Conflicts: Resolving Line Switching Issues

Many travelers keep their home SIM active alongside a Spain eSIM. This often causes connectivity issues when data routes through the wrong line.

### Quick Fix – Set Spain eSIM as Default Data Line

**iPhone:** Settings > Mobile Data > Mobile Data > select "Spain eSIM" (blue checkmark). Turn OFF "Allow Mobile Data Switching" to prevent accidental home roaming charges.

**Samsung:** Settings > Connections > SIM Manager > under "Mobile Data," ensure radio button is on your Spain eSIM.

**Google Pixel:** Settings > Network & Internet > SIMs > tap "Mobile data" and select Spain eSIM.

### Common Dual-SIM Problems

| Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong default data line | Internet uses home SIM – high roaming charges | Set Spain eSIM as default data line |
| Home line stuck searching | Phone keeps scanning for home carrier | Turn off home SIM or set to manual with no network |
| iMessage/FaceTime fails | Services try home line instead of eSIM | Settings > Messages > Send & Receive > select Spain eSIM |
| "Allow Mobile Data Switching" causing roaming | iPhone uses home line data when eSIM weak | Turn OFF "Allow Mobile Data Switching" |

## Emergency Calls Only: Fixing Network Registration Errors

"Emergency Calls Only" means your phone cannot register on any mobile network for normal service.

### Why This Happens

1. **Carrier not supported by your eSIM** – Your plan may only have agreements with 1-2 Spanish networks. If those aren't available at your location, you'll see this message.
2. **Network registration queue full** – At busy airports and tourist attractions, the network may be temporarily full. This resolves in 5-30 minutes.
3. **Corrupt eSIM profile** – The profile data may be incomplete, preventing authentication.

### Fixes to Try in Order

1. **Manual network selection (most important):**
   - Settings > Mobile Data > Network Selection > turn OFF "Automatic"
   - Wait 10-20 seconds for the network list
   - Try each network in this order: Movistar, Orange, Vodafone ES, Yoigo
   - One should allow registration – leave manual selection ON for 24 hours

2. **Restart and wait longer:**
   - Some registrations take 5-15 minutes, especially during congestion
   - Restart, wait 5 full minutes before trying other fixes

3. **Remove and reinstall the eSIM:**
   - If manual selection doesn't work on any carrier, the profile may be corrupt
   - Delete the profile, reinstall using original QR or manual code
   - If QR expired, contact support for a new one

4. **Contact provider support:**
   - If none of the above works, the issue is on the provider's side
   - Contact support with your eSIM ICCID (Settings > General > About > scroll to eSIM line)
   - Provider may need to reprovision your line

## Frequently Asked Questions About Spain eSIM Issues

**Why is my Spain eSIM not connecting to network?**  
The most common causes are data roaming disabled, incorrect network selection, or phone being carrier-locked. Enable data roaming, try manual network selection (Movistar first), and verify your phone is unlocked.

**How do I fix Spain eSIM APN settings?**  
APN settings are provider-specific. For Airalo on Movistar, use "airalo.movistar.es." For Holafly, use "holafly." For Orange direct plans, use "orange.es." Full APN tables are above. Type the APN manually rather than pasting to avoid hidden spaces.

**Which Spain eSIM provider has the best coverage?**  
Movistar has the widest coverage in Spain, followed by Orange. Vodafone is strongest in cities. For automatic switching across all networks, multi-network eSIMs provide the most reliable connectivity.

**Does Airalo work well in Spain?**  
Yes, Airalo connects to Movistar and Orange networks. If you experience issues, check the APN is set to "airalo.movistar.es" or "airalo.orange.es" and data roaming is enabled.

**Can I get a Spain eSIM with a phone number?**  
Yes, Movistar, Orange, and Vodafone offer prepaid eSIMs with Spanish phone numbers. These require in-person purchase and passport verification. Data-only providers like Airalo, Holafly, and Nomad do not include phone numbers.

**Why is my Spain eSIM so slow?**  
Slow speeds are usually caused by network congestion in tourist areas, connection to a distant tower, or provider throttling. Try manual carrier switching, toggling Airplane Mode, or moving to a different location. Toggling 5G OFF and using 4G can also help.

**What is the best eSIM for Spain and Portugal?**  
If traveling to both countries, a regional Europe eSIM or multi-country plan offers coverage across both without needing to switch at the border.

**How do I get unlimited data on my Spain eSIM?**  
Holafly offers unlimited data for tourists. Some other providers offer "unlimited" but throttle after a daily fair-use limit. Check the fine print before purchasing.

**Can I use the same Spain eSIM in Madrid and Barcelona?**  
Yes, all major providers (Movistar, Orange, Vodafone, Yoigo) have coverage in both cities. Rural coverage varies – Movistar has the best rural coverage.

**How do I buy an eSIM for Spain?**  
Buy online from international providers (Airalo, Holafly, Nomad, multi-network) before departure, or purchase directly from Movistar, Orange, or Vodafone at Spanish airports or city stores. For the best rates, compare plans on our price guide.
