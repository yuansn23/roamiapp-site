## Hidden Thailand eSIM Costs & Price Traps

**In short:** Watch for FUP throttling, hotspot restrictions, foreign transaction fees, QR-code expiry, and activation-triggered countdowns. These can make a cheap plan more expensive.

The sticker price isn’t always what you pay. Watch for these common traps.

**1. Fair-usage throttling on “unlimited” plans.** Many unlimited plans drop speed after a daily allowance (e.g. 2GB/day at full speed, then heavily throttled). Read the FUP before buying.

**2. Hotspot restrictions.** Some cheap plans block hotspot sharing or charge extra. Confirm hotspot support if you plan to share with a laptop or travel companion.

**3. Currency and foreign-transaction fees.** Most platforms price in USD. If your card is in another currency, your bank may add a 1–3% foreign-transaction fee. Some platforms show local currency at checkout — using that can save the fee.

**4. QR-code expiry.** Most QR codes are valid for 30 days from purchase. Buy within 30 days of departure, or confirm validity before purchasing.

**5. Activation-triggered countdown.** Some plans start counting from installation, not first network connection. Installing a week early can cost you a week of validity.

**6. “Unlimited low-speed” after high-speed data ends.** Some plans advertise “15GB + unlimited” — but the “unlimited” part is heavily throttled and too slow for video. Read the fine print.

> **Protect yourself:** before buying, check (1) FUP details, (2) hotspot support, (3) activation trigger, (4) QR validity, (5) refund policy.

**Key takeaway:** A cheap plan can become expensive if it lacks hotspot, throttles early, or expires before you activate. Always read the fine print.

## How to Compare Thailand eSIM Prices Like a Pro

**In short:** Compare cost per GB, validity, hotspot support, FUP, and activation trigger — not just the sticker price. A cheap plan with poor terms can cost more in the long run.

Use this checklist before buying any cheap Thailand eSIM:
