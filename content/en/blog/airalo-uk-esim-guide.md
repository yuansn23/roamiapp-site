## Brexit Roaming Special Section

This issue distinguishes the UK from other European countries, worth a detailed explanation.

### What Happened After Brexit

The UK officially left the EU in 2020, and the EU's "Roam Like at Home" policy no longer automatically applies to the UK. From 2022 onwards:

- **UK operators** (EE, Vodafone, Three) have reinstated roaming charges for the EU (O2 partly retains free allowances).
- **EU operators** (Orange, Vodafone EU version, etc.) have mostly reinstated roaming charges for the UK.

This means: **If you buy a card in the EU or travel between the UK and the EU, your mobile card may charge you daily or per-MB roaming fees when in the UK.**
