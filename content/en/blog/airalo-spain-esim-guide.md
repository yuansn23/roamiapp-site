## Why Choose eSIM Instead of a Traditional SIM?

If you're planning a trip to Spain, **Spain eSIM** is currently the most convenient way to get online. An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a virtual SIM card written directly into your phone's chip—no physical card slot needed. Compared to traditional SIMs, the advantages are clear:

- **Instant activation**: Buy a **Spain eSIM** online and receive a QR code within minutes—no waiting for delivery or queuing in stores.
- **No need to swap cards**: Keep your home number for receiving SMS (like bank verification codes) while using your **Spain eSIM** for data.
- **Flexible plans**: From 1 day to 30 days, from 1GB to unlimited data—choose what fits your needs.
- **Eco-friendly & secure**: No small card to lose, less plastic waste.

### Operator Details

#### Movistar (Telefónica)

- **Market share**: ~29% (Spain's largest operator)
- **Core strength**: Inherited the former state telecom infrastructure, **widest rural and remote coverage**, especially in Andalusia, Galicia, Castile‑León, etc. Choosing a **Spain eSIM** that uses Movistar gives you the best rural performance.
- **Best for**: Travelers planning car rentals, small town visits, or the Camino de Santiago.
- **5G bands**: n28 (700MHz), n78 (3.5GHz)

#### Orange

- **Market share**: ~22%
- **Core strength**: **Spain's highest urban 5G density**, excellent 5G experience in Barcelona, Madrid, Valencia, and other large cities. Many **Spain eSIM** brands (like Airalo) use Orange.
- **Best for**: City sightseers who mainly stay in large cities and prioritise high‑speed networks.
- **5G bands**: n28 (700MHz), n78 (3.5GHz)

#### Vodafone

- **Market share**: ~23%
- **Core strength**: Excellent 5G speeds in major cities (often above 400 Mbps peak), strong signal at transport hubs (airports, high‑speed train stations). Rural coverage is slightly weaker than Movistar.
- **Best for**: Business travellers, users who need fast uploads/downloads.
- **5G bands**: n28 (700MHz), n78 (3.5GHz)

#### Yoigo (MásMóvil Group)

- **Market share**: ~15%
- **Core strength**: Aggressive low‑pricing, but **limited independent rural coverage**, and only 4G in some areas. Airalo uses Yoigo for some Spain plans.
- **Best for**: Extremely budget‑conscious users who stay only in city centres; not recommended as a primary travel choice.

## Setting Up Your Spain eSIM

After purchasing a **Spain eSIM**, you'll receive an email with a QR code. It's very straightforward:

1. **iPhone**: Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM → Scan QR code → Label as "Travel" or "Data".
2. **Android**: Settings → Connections → SIM card manager → Add eSIM → Scan QR code (paths may vary by brand).
3. **Activation**: Upon arrival in Spain, enable "Data Roaming" in settings and ensure the eSIM is selected as the primary data card. Network registration usually happens within 2 minutes.

### How Each Brand's Activation Differs

- **Airalo**: Install via the App, no manual QR scanning needed; the App guides you through the process.
- **Holafly**: Provides QR via email, also installable via App. Important: Ensure your phone's date/time is set to "Automatic" before activation.
- **Roami**: QR via email; scan to install. After installation, it activates automatically upon arrival in Spain—no extra steps.

### Fixing Activation Problems

> **Can't connect to the network? Try these steps:**
>
> ① **Check Data Roaming**: Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data Options → Data Roaming (turn ON)
>
> ② **Manual network selection**: Settings → Cellular → Network Selection → turn off "Automatic" → manually choose Movistar, Orange, or Vodafone
>
> ③ **Restart phone**: Simple but effective
>
> ④ **Check APN settings**: Some brands require manual APN setup (usually "internet" or "mobile"); see your purchase email for details
>
> ⑤ **Confirm activation period**: Some plans have an expiry date; activation after expiry may fail
>
