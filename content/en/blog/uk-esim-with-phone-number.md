## Why You Might Need a UK Phone Number with Your eSIM

Many essential UK services require a phone number for SMS verification, which data-only eSIMs cannot provide. giffgaff charges between GBP 6 and GBP 20 per month for a UK phone number with data, but data-only eSIMs from providers like Roami cost less and work well for users who do not need to receive SMS verification codes from Uber, banks, or food delivery services.

Understanding what you actually need before you buy will save you money and frustration. Let's break down the options so you can choose the right setup for your trip.

## Can I Get a UK Phone Number with eSIM?

Short answer: yes, absolutely. Several UK providers now offer plans that include a real UK mobile number, giving you full calling, SMS, and data capabilities. Understanding how to get a UK phone number with eSIM starts with choosing between data-only and full-service providers based on your needs. These aren't VoIP numbers or virtual SIMs — they're proper mobile lines that work exactly like any UK phone number.

The longer answer is that it depends on the provider and the type of plan you pick. Most providers fall into two camps:

**Data-only plans** — these give you a data connection only. No phone number, no SMS, no calls. Think of it like a data-only tablet plan. Roami falls into this category, and honestly, a lot of travellers find that's all they actually need — as long as they're prepared for the limitations. The UK eSIM data only vs with calling plan decision ultimately comes down to whether you need SMS verification for banking, Uber, and other services.

**Full-service plans** — these include a UK phone number with voice minutes, SMS, and data. They're essentially a regular mobile plan delivered as an eSIM. Providers like giffgaff, Lebara, and Lyca Mobile all offer these, and they're the closest thing to having a proper UK SIM without needing a physical card.

The catch? Full-service plans often cost more than data-only options, especially for short trips. And you'll typically need to go through some form of ID verification to get one, because UK regulations require mobile providers to verify your identity before assigning a number.

### Do I Need ID to Buy an eSIM in the UK?

Yes. Since 2022, UK regulations require all mobile providers to verify your identity before activating a line. This applies to both physical SIMs and eSIMs. The exact process varies by provider, but you'll generally need to submit a photo of your passport or national ID card, and sometimes a proof of address.

For data-only plans like Roami, the verification process is much simpler — usually just an email address and payment. That's because data-only services aren't classified the same way under UK telecoms regulations. So if you want a UK phone number, be prepared for a bit of paperwork.

### Do I Need a UK Address for an eSIM?

Some providers ask for a UK address during registration, but most full-service providers that cater to tourists don't require one. giffgaff, for instance, lets you sign up with just your passport and any address — even if it's back home. Lebara does the same. The key is sticking with providers that are used to dealing with visitors rather than ones primarily targeting UK residents.

If a provider insists on a UK postcode during online signup, you could use your hotel address or an accommodation address, but honestly, just pick a provider that doesn't need one. There are plenty that don't.

## Why You Need a UK eSIM for Uber and Essential Apps

Before we dive into specific plans, let's talk about why you might, or might not, need a UK number in the first place. This will help you decide whether a full-service plan or data-only option makes more sense for your trip.

### Banking and Financial Services

This is the big one. If you're planning to open a UK bank account — and many long-term visitors and digital nomads do — you'll need a UK phone number for SMS verification. Almost every UK bank uses two-factor authentication via text message. Monzo, Starling, Revolut, HSBC, Lloyds — they all do it. Without a UK number, you'll hit a wall.

Even if you're just using your existing credit card abroad, you might run into issues. Some payment systems trigger SMS verification for transactions they deem unusual. Having a UK number to receive those OTPs can save you from a declined payment at a critical moment.

If you're unsure whether your phone supports eSIM, [Apple's official guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118670) has a full list of compatible models. For Android devices, the [GSMA's eSIM database](https://www.gsma.com/esim/) — maintained by the [GSMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSMA), the global mobile industry association — is the most comprehensive compatibility resource available.

### Ride-Hailing and Food Delivery Apps

Uber, Bolt, Deliveroo, Just Eat, Uber Eats — every single one of these requires a phone number to create and verify your account. Can you use your home number? Sometimes. But if your home number doesn't work for SMS while you're abroad (and many don't, or they charge exorbitant roaming rates), you'll be stuck.

The real hassle comes when you need to contact your driver or delivery person. Uber's in-app messaging helps, but occasionally the driver needs to call you. If they dial your home number and you're paying international roaming rates, that's not ideal. A UK number solves this cleanly. This is exactly why a UK eSIM for Uber and public transport apps is so valuable.

### NHS and Healthcare Access

If you need to register with a GP, book an appointment, or access NHS services, you'll need a UK phone number. The NHS uses SMS for appointment reminders, test results, and prescription notifications. Even registering for NHS login requires a UK mobile number for verification.

For shorter trips this probably won't matter, but for anyone staying more than a few weeks — especially students, digital nomads, or people visiting family — it's worth having.

### Job Applications and Professional Contact

Looking for work while in the UK? Putting a foreign number on your CV is a red flag for many employers. They want to be able to call you, and they don't want to deal with international dialling codes. A UK number makes you look local and accessible.

### Public Wi-Fi and Services

You might be surprised how many public Wi-Fi networks in the UK require SMS verification to connect. Train stations, coffee shops, libraries, and shopping centres often use SMS-based authentication. Without a UK number, you're stuck with mobile data, which defeats the purpose of connecting to free Wi-Fi.

### So, Do You Really Need One?

Here's the honest take: for a short trip of a week or less, staying mostly in London and major cities, you can probably get by with a data-only [UK eSIM](/united-kingdom-esim/) plus a few workarounds. But for any trip longer than that, or if you're planning to do anything beyond basic sightseeing, having a UK number makes life much easier. A [UK eSIM](/united-kingdom-esim/) combined with a voice line gives you the flexibility of fast data on a multi-network connection while keeping a UK number for calls and SMS verification.

## UK eSIM Data Only vs with Calling Plan: VoIP Workaround

If you've already got a data-only [UK eSIM](/united-kingdom-esim/) — from Roami or another provider — and you're realising you need a UK number, don't panic. You've got options. Adding a voice-enabled eSIM alongside your existing [UK eSIM](/united-kingdom-esim/) data plan is straightforward on any modern dual-SIM phone.

### VoIP Services as a Workaround

Voice over IP services can give you a UK phone number that works over your data connection. Here's how they stack up:

**Skype** — Skype offers UK phone numbers starting at about £3.50/month. You get a real UK number that can receive calls, and voicemail is included. SMS reception is hit-or-miss, though. Skype-to-Skype calls are free, but calling UK landlines and mobiles uses your Skype credit.

**Google Voice** — This one's tricky. Google Voice numbers are generally only available to US users, so if you're not in the US, you probably can't get one. If you already have a Google Voice number from home, it'll work over data in the UK, but it's not a UK number.

**Zadarma** — Less well-known but works well for UK virtual numbers. Prices start around £2/month for a UK number, and you pay for calls as you use them. SMS support is limited, so don't rely on it for OTPs.

**TextNow** — Free US and Canada numbers, but UK numbers require a paid plan. Not ideal for most travellers.

The big limitation with all VoIP options is SMS verification. Many banks, government services, and apps won't send verification codes to VoIP numbers because they're flagged as non-mobile. So while a VoIP number works for receiving calls, it won't reliably handle the OTPs you actually need.

### When VoIP Works

- Making and receiving voice calls
- Voicemail
- WhatsApp and iMessage registration
- Non-SMS two-factor apps (authenticator apps work fine)

### When VoIP Falls Short

Bank SMS verification (almost always blocked), Uber, Deliveroo, Just Eat account creation, NHS services, Government service logins, and Any SMS-based OTP system

## UK eSIM Can't Receive SMS Verification: Common Fixes

This is probably the most common problem people run into. You've got a UK eSIM with a phone number, but SMS messages just aren't arriving. Here's what to check.

If your UK eSIM cannot receive SMS verification codes, the first thing to confirm is whether your plan actually includes SMS capability rather than being data-only.

### Is It Actually a Full-Service Plan?

Double-check that your eSIM includes SMS. Some travel plans advertise as "with phone number" but only support data and maybe voice calls, not SMS. Read the fine print. If you bought a data-only plan that claimed to include a number, that might be your problem.

### Is SMS Properly Configured?

On iPhones, go to Settings > Messages > Send & Receive and make sure your UK number is selected. On Android, check that your messaging app is set to use the correct SIM for SMS.

In a dual SIM setup, your phone might be trying to send SMS from your home SIM, or the UK line might not be set as the default for SMS. You can usually set this in Settings > Cellular or Settings > Mobile Data > Default Voice Line.

### Is Your Provider Blocking SMS?

Some budget providers route SMS differently from standard mobile networks. Certain shortcodes used by banks and apps may not reach your eSIM. This is more common with MVNOs that use secondary routing for SMS.

If you consistently can't receive SMS from a specific service, try:

1. Switching to manual network selection and trying a different UK network
2. Checking with your provider whether they support SMS from shortcodes
3. Using an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator or Authy) instead of SMS for 2FA
4. Contacting support to see if they can use a voice call for verification instead

### Is iMessage Interfering?

If you're on iPhone and have iMessage enabled, SMS from other iPhone users gets delivered through iMessage rather than traditional SMS. If iMessage activation is pending or stuck, you won't receive messages from iPhone users.

Go to Settings > Messages and toggle iMessage off and on again. You can also check Settings > Messages > Send & Receive to make sure your UK number is listed and verified.

## Making Calls with UK eSIM: What You Need to Know

### Can You Make Calls with a Data-Only UK eSIM?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a UK phone number with eSIM?

Yes. Providers like giffgaff, Lebara, and Lyca Mobile all offer eSIMs with a proper UK mobile number. You'll need to verify your identity with a passport or ID card, and you'll get a standard UK number that supports calls, SMS, and data.

### Do I need a UK address for eSIM?

No, not for the tourist-friendly providers. giffgaff and Lebara let you register with your home address and a passport. Some providers may ask for a UK address, in which case your hotel or Airbnb address should work for verification purposes.

### Do I need ID to buy an eSIM in the UK?

Yes, if you want a UK phone number. UK regulations require mobile providers to verify your identity before assigning a number. You'll typically need to submit a photo of your passport. Data-only plans don't require ID since they don't include a phone number or voice/SMS services.

### Can I keep my existing number with UK eSIM?

You can't port your non-UK number to a UK eSIM, but you can keep your existing number active through dual SIM. Your home SIM stays in the phone (physical or eSIM) while your UK eSIM runs alongside it. Calls and SMS to your home number still come through, and you use the UK eSIM for data and optionally a UK number.

### Do tourists need passport for UK eSIM?

For full-service plans with a UK number, yes. giffgaff, Lebara, Lyca Mobile, and all major UK providers require passport or national ID verification. Data-only plans don't require ID.

### Can I make calls with UK eSIM?

With a full-service plan (giffgaff, Lebara, Lyca), yes — you get standard voice calling. With a data-only plan (Roami), no — you'll need to use VoIP apps like WhatsApp, Skype, or Zoom for voice calls.

### Why can't I receive SMS with my UK eSIM?

Common causes: The plan is data-only (check your plan), SMS isn't properly configured in your phone settings, the wrong line is set as default for SMS in a dual SIM setup, or your provider doesn't support SMS from certain shortcodes used by banks and apps.

### Can I use WhatsApp with UK eSIM?

Yes. WhatsApp works completely over data. Install it with your home number before you travel, and it'll work throughout your trip on your UK plan's data connection. If you want to use a UK number for WhatsApp, you can change your WhatsApp number in the app settings.

### Is a UK eSIM with phone number more expensive than data-only?

Generally, yes. Full-service plans that include a phone number, voice, and SMS cost more than data-only plans. However, the difference is often modest — Lebara's £6.90 plan is only slightly more than a data-only plan of similar size. The hybrid approach (data plan + cheap voice eSIM) can sometimes work out cheaper than a single full-service plan with comparable data.

### Can I use my UK number for bank verification?

Generally, yes — as long as the plan includes SMS. Full-service plans from giffgaff and Lebara work fine with UK banks. Some budget eSIMs may have issues with certain shortcodes, so stick with established providers for anything involving financial services.

### What happens to my UK number after I leave the UK?

Most UK eSIMs with phone numbers are prepaid monthly plans. When your month ends, the number is held for a grace period (usually 30-90 days) during which you can top up and reactivate. After that, the number is recycled. If you plan to return to the UK regularly, keep the account active with the minimum top-up to hold your number.

### Can I receive calls on my UK eSIM while outside the UK?

It depends on whether your plan includes international roaming for voice. giffgaff and Lebara allow limited EU roaming on most plans, but roaming outside Europe typically isn't included. Calls to your UK number while you're abroad may go to voicemail or fail entirely. Check your provider's roaming policy.

### Is a UK eSIM with phone number suitable for business use?

For light business use (client calls, conference calls, professional WhatsApp), yes — giffgaff and Lebara are perfectly adequate. For heavy business use (multiple calls per day, reliable conference calling, professional voicemail), consider a proper business plan from EE or Vodafone, or use a VoIP service like RingCentral over a data plan.

### Can I port my UK number to another provider if I decide to switch?

Yes, number porting works the same way for eSIMs as for physical SIMs in the UK. If you want to switch from giffgaff to Vodafone, for instance, you can request a PAC code from giffgaff and give it to Vodafone to transfer your number. The process takes 1-2 working days.

### How do I top up or extend my UK eSIM with a phone number?

Each provider handles this differently:
- **giffgaff**: Top up via the app or website. Credit is added to your account, and goodybags are purchased separately
- **Lebara**: Auto-renewal is available, or you can manually extend through the app
- **Lyca Mobile**: Top up online or through the app
- **Roami (data-only)**: Top up through the website or app for additional data

### What if I lose my phone with the UK eSIM installed?

If you lose your phone, contact your provider immediately to suspend the line. For full-service plans with a phone number, the provider can deactivate the profile and issue a new one when you get a replacement phone. For data-only plans, the remaining plan value is typically lost, though some providers may transfer it to a new device.

### Do UK eSIMs support emergency services?

All UK mobile networks support 999 emergency calls from any active SIM, including eSIMs. Even if your eSIM has no credit or data remaining, you can dial 999 for emergency services. This applies to both full-service and data-only plans.
