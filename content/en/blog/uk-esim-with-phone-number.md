## What if my UK eSIM cannot receive SMS verification codes?

If your UK eSIM can't receive SMS verification codes, first confirm your plan actually includes SMS capability -- many data-only plans can't receive SMS at all, regardless of settings. That's the first thing to check and the most common cause.

**Common causes and fixes:**

**Is the plan actually full-service?** Double-check that your plan includes SMS. Some travel plans advertise as "with phone number" but only support data and maybe voice -- not SMS. This misleading marketing has been documented across multiple budget providers. Read the fine print.

**Is SMS properly configured?** On iPhone: Settings > Messages > Send & Receive, make sure your UK number is selected. On Android: check that your messaging app is set to use the correct SIM for SMS. In a dual SIM setup, your phone might be sending SMS from your home SIM.

**Is your provider blocking shortcodes?** Some budget providers route SMS differently. Certain shortcodes used by banks and apps may not reach your eSIM. If you consistently can't receive SMS from a specific service, try switching to manual network selection, checking with your provider about shortcode support, using an authenticator app instead of SMS for 2FA, or contacting support to request voice call verification instead.

For example, some Lebara eSIM users report that SMS from Monzo's shortcode (60777) won't arrive until they manually switch the network to Vodafone-only in phone settings. Automatic network selection sometimes routes SMS through a partner that blocks shortcodes -- a fix that can take nearly an hour to diagnose.

**Is iMessage interfering?** On iPhone, if iMessage activation is pending or stuck, SMS from other iPhone users gets delivered through iMessage rather than traditional SMS. Go to Settings > Messages and toggle iMessage off and on. Check that your UK number is listed and verified under Send & Receive.

Ofcom coverage data (2025) confirms this: EE (via Lyca) holds signal through rural stretches like the Pennines; O2 (via giffgaff) shows coverage gaps near Penrith that can last 15 minutes or more.

Roami's unique advantage is auto carrier switching -- instead of locking you to one network, Roami's eSIM automatically selects the strongest available network at your location, switching between EE, Vodafone, O2, and Three. If you're moving around the UK and especially if your trip includes both cities and countryside, this auto-switching makes a real difference in day-to-day reliability.

But auto-switching isn't magic. In the Cairngorms, all four networks go dead. No amount of switching helps when there simply aren't any towers. Download offline maps before you head into seriously remote areas.
