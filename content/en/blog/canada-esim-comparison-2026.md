### How to Read the Canada eSIM Tables

- **Starting Price:** The lowest-cost plan available for Canada. Often a small 1GB plan with short validity.
- **Unlimited Plan Price:** The cost for a plan advertised as "unlimited." Always check the Fair Use Policy—many have daily high-speed caps.
- **Network:** Which Canadian carrier networks the eSIM can access. Multi-network access means better coverage in rural areas.
- **Phone Number:** Whether the plan includes a real Canadian or US number for calls/SMS. Data-only plans do not.
- **Hotspot:** Whether you can share your data with other devices. Some providers limit or block hotspot.
- **5G:** Whether the provider offers 5G speeds in supported areas.
- **Per-GB Cost (Large Plan):** Calculated by dividing the total price by the data volume for the largest plan. Useful for heavy data users.
- **Common Complaints:** The most frequent negative feedback from public user reports.
