# Morocco eSIM: The Convenient Way to Stay Connected

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For most travelers, **Maroc Telecom** offers the widest 4G/5G coverage across Morocco, including remote desert areas and the Atlas Mountains. If you prioritize faster city speeds in Casablanca and Marrakech, **Orange Morocco** is an excellent alternative. Both support <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> with most modern smartphones, and you can activate before you leave – no roaming fees, no SIM swap.

## Things you must know before buying a Morocco eSIM


### Check your device for eSIM compatibility {#check-your-device-for-esim-compatibility}
Most modern smartphones (iPhone XS and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer) support eSIM. If you're unsure, visit our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page to verify your device.

### Activate before you depart {#activate-before-you-depart}
We recommend installing and activating your Morocco eSIM at home using Wi-Fi. The eSIM will automatically connect to the local network as soon as you land in Morocco – no need to hunt for a SIM card shop.

### Data roaming must be enabled {#data-roaming-must-be-enabled}
Even though you're using a local eSIM, your phone settings require data roaming to be turned on for the eSIM line. This is normal and does not incur extra charges.

### No physical SIM removal needed {#no-physical-sim-removal-needed}
You can keep your home SIM in your phone for calls and SMS while using the Morocco eSIM for data. Just set the eSIM as your primary data line in settings.
