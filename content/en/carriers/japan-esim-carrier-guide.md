# Japan eSIM: Cost-Effective Travel Data Plans

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For most travelers, NTT DOCOMO offers the widest 5G coverage across Japan, including rural areas like Hakone. SoftBank provides excellent speeds in Tokyo and Osaka. Avoid expensive roaming by using a local eSIM. Perfect for budget-conscious tourists who want to avoid roaming fees and enjoy seamless connectivity at famous landmarks.

## Things you must know before buying a Japan eSIM


### Check your device compatibility {#check-your-device-compatibility}
Before purchasing a Japan eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels (3 and later), and Samsung Galaxy S20 and later models are compatible. Visit our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page to verify your device.

### Understand data-only vs. voice plans {#understand-data-only-vs-voice-plans}
Most travel eSIMs for Japan are data-only. For voice calls, use apps like WhatsApp, Skype, or LINE. If you need a local number, consider a traditional SIM or a hybrid eSIM that includes voice.

### Activation tips {#activation-tips}
Install your eSIM before departure. Upon arrival in Japan, enable data roaming in your phone settings. The eSIM will automatically connect to the local network. No physical SIM removal is needed.
