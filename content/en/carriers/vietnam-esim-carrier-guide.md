## Things you must know before buying a Vietnam eSIM


### Check your device compatibility {#check-your-device-compatibility}
Before purchasing a Vietnam eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most recent models from Apple, Samsung, Google, and Huawei are compatible. If you're unsure, check our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page for a full list of supported devices.

### Choose the right data plan {#choose-the-right-data-plan}
Vietnam eSIM plans vary by data allowance and validity. If you're a heavy user (streaming, video calls), opt for a plan with at least 10GB. For light users (maps, messaging), 3-5GB is sufficient. Many plans offer unlimited data at reduced speeds after the high-speed cap.

### Activation and setup tips {#activation-and-setup-tips}
Most Vietnam eSIMs activate instantly upon purchase. After installation, enable data roaming in your device settings. You do not need to remove your physical SIM – the eSIM works alongside it. No APN configuration is typically required.
