# Norway eSIM: Instant Activation with Zero Wait Time

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For the best balance of speed and coverage across Norway's rugged terrain, choose Telenor's eSIM. It delivers reliable 4G/LTE in remote fjord areas and fast 5G in cities like Oslo. Telia is a strong alternative for urban-centric travelers who prioritize slightly lower prices. Both support instant activation with zero wait time, perfect for avoiding roaming fees on a Norway trip.

## Things you must know before buying a Norway eSIM


### Device compatibility check {#device-compatibility-check}
Before purchasing, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most newer models from Apple, Samsung, Google, and Huawei are compatible. Check our detailed <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> list for your specific device model to avoid any surprises.

### Activation and setup {#activation-and-setup}
All Roami Norway eSIMs feature instant activation with zero wait time. After purchase, you'll receive a QR code via email. Scan it with your phone's camera, follow the on-screen instructions, and your eSIM profile will be installed. Once you land in Norway, simply enable the data line and turn on data roaming. No physical SIM swap is required.

### Data roaming and APN settings {#data-roaming-and-apn-settings}
For both Telenor and Telia eSIMs, data roaming must be enabled on your device. In most cases, the APN is automatically configured. If you encounter connection issues, manually set the APN to \"roami\" (without quotes). This step is rarely needed but is provided for troubleshooting.
