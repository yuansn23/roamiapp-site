### How to install your Ecuador eSIM {#how-to-install-your-ecuador-esim}
1. Purchase and receive your eSIM QR code via email.\\n2. Go to **Settings > Cellular > Add Cellular Plan** on your iPhone or **Settings > Connections > SIM card manager > Add eSIM** on Android.\\n3. Scan the QR code and label the plan as \"Ecuador Data\".\\n4. On arrival, enable data roaming for this line and disable your primary SIM to avoid charges.
