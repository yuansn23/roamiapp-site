faq:
- q: Can I use my existing phone number with a Jordan eSIM?
  a: Yes, if your phone supports dual SIM (one physical + one eSIM), you can keep
    your home SIM active for calls and SMS while using the Jordan eSIM for data. This
    helps avoid roaming fees for data.
- q: Will the Jordan eSIM work immediately upon landing?
  a: Most eSIMs activate before departure and connect automatically when you arrive
    in Jordan. Ensure data roaming is enabled. If not, a quick manual network selection
    usually resolves it.
- q: Which Jordan eSIM is best for streaming video?
  a: Zain Jordan and Orange Jordan both offer 5G speeds suitable for HD streaming.
    Zain has slightly better coverage in tourist areas like Petra and the Dead Sea.
- q: Do I need to register my eSIM with local authorities?
  a: No, eSIMs from reputable providers are pre-registered with Jordanian networks.
    Simply install and use.
- q: Can I top up my Jordan eSIM if I run out of data?
  a: Yes, most eSIM providers allow easy top-ups via their app or website. Check your
    provider's policy before travel.

    and messaging.
