## How local eSIM rules shape every purchase

Four dated facts frame everything else on this page:

- Mid-2024: authorities blocked Signal and the major VPNs, closing the most common workarounds travellers used to reach blocked services.
- January 2025: the military regime enacted the [Cybersecurity Law](https://apnews.com/article/internet-online-censorship-law-repression-8128ba7a2c02555217c6a64ab641eaf6), codifying censorship mandates, restricting VPN providers and imposing local data-retention requirements.
- Throughout 2025: civil-society tracking at the [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/) logged scores of localised internet cuts across townships in most of the country's states and regions — often with no notice and no schedule.
- Ongoing: the UN human rights office has documented how connectivity denial accompanies military operations, particularly around the [election-period environment documented in late 2025](https://bangkok.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/2025-12/OHCHRMyanmar-Background-paper-on-military-elections-Nov25.pdf).

The practical translation for a traveller: your eSIM is a local data connection on a monitored, occasionally interrupted network. Plan around outages rather than pretending they will not happen.

Market position: MPT is the country's original operator, with a legacy footprint that follows the trunk roads and the temple zones where newer entrants thin out. If your itinerary spends more time outside Yangon and Mandalay than in them, MPT is the network you want attached.

**The visitor's buying process, step by step:**
1. Go to an MPT counter at Yangon (RGN) or Mandalay airport, or any MPT shop.

## Troubleshooting a Myanmar eSIM: what actually fails here

A Myanmar eSIM that will not work is rarely a broken SIM. The install sequence — add the profile, scan, label the line, enable roaming — is covered in our [eSIM activation walkthrough](/faq/how-to-activate-an-esim/), and the standard fault list sits in the [eSIM problem-solving guide](/faq/esim-activation-errors-troubleshooting-guide/). In this country, four very specific situations account for nearly every failure, and none of them is the phone's fault.

### Myanmar eSIM shows full bars but nothing loads: a local shutdown

This is the signature of a localised shutdown rather than a fault in your eSIM. Independent trackers log cuts township by township, and they arrive with no notice and no schedule. The tells are consistent: the signal indicator is healthy, every carrier is equally dead, nothing you change makes any difference, and service returns on its own. Do not keep toggling settings or buying top-ups — wait it out, move on, or fall back on whatever you downloaded in advance. In a border or conflict township the same reading means there is simply no network to attach to, and that is the one problem no settings screen can solve.

### Does Myanmar require SIM registration?

Every Myanmar number, eSIM included, is registered in person against a passport and a visa or entry stamp. There is no foreigner self-signup with document upload the way there is in the Maldives or Thailand, so a profile that arrives without a completed registration will never attach. Airport counters at Yangon and Mandalay, operator stores and authorised kiosks are the routes — bring both documents and expect it to be finished on the spot.

### Why your home SIM shows no service in Myanmar

This is not a broken handset. International roaming onto Myanmar networks stopped working after 2021, so a home SIM expecting full-strength service will read "no service" from the moment you land. A locally registered SIM or a travel eSIM activated by its provider is the only data path; no amount of settings work revives roaming here. Leave the home SIM switched on for SMS verification codes, with its data roaming firmly off.

### APN values for ATOM, MPT and Mytel eSIMs

Myanmar's operators use different APNs — `mptnet` on MPT, `internet` on ATOM and on U9, `mytel` on Mytel — and a hand-added profile can easily point at the wrong one, especially if a SIM has moved between operators. Compare the APN screen against the table above, then confirm the eSIM, not the home SIM, is the line carrying mobile data.

## The registration counter experience

- **Passport plus visa or entry stamp.** Both are checked together at registration; neither alone is accepted.
- **In person, at a counter or store.** Yangon (RGN) and Mandalay (MDL) arrival counters handle MPT, ATOM and U9, and registration happens on the spot.
- **No online foreigner route.** Unlike the Maldives, there is no document-upload signup — which is precisely why travel eSIMs, registered by the provider on your behalf, are the lower-friction option.
- **Kyat in hand.** Kiosk top-ups are cash-first and foreign cards are refused often.
- **Tourist starter packs suit arrival day.** MPT's counter pack pairs a small data bucket with calling credit, which is handy before you have sorted a longer-validity top-up.
- **The number stays tied to you.** Registration attaches the SIM to your identity for as long as it is active, with no anonymous option and no way to hand it on.

### VPNs in Myanmar: what a VPN will and will not do

Restrictions on VPN use were codified in the January 2025 cybersecurity law and enforcement is real, so we do not recommend leaning on one to reach blocked services. A VPN can obscure traffic; it cannot conjure a connection where the network has been switched off.

## Short answers, real questions

### Is it safe to use mobile internet in Myanmar?

We are not a security adviser, but the factual picture is documented at the top of this page: Freedom House rates Myanmar 9/100 for internet freedom, the VPN ban is codified in law, and SIM data feeds surveillance systems. Use messaging apps you already trust, assume traffic is monitored, and do not rely on a VPN to bypass restrictions — doing so carries legal risk.

### Who has the strongest rural signal in Myanmar?

MPT has the broadest geographic footprint, especially outside cities and into temple regions like Bagan and Inle. ATOM (ex-Telenor) is the most dependable for the main tourist corridors. We do not quote per-carrier speed leaderboards because independent mobile benchmarks are unreliable post-2021.

### What to bring when buying a Myanmar SIM

Yes. Registration against a passport (and visa/entry stamp) is mandatory for every SIM and eSIM. Travel eSIM providers handle this during activation so you do not upload documents yourself.

### Myanmar eSIMs in neighboring countries

No. International roaming onto Myanmar networks stopped functioning after 2021, so a local-network eSIM or a local SIM is the only way to get data. Arrive with a plan installed.

### ATOM vs MPT: which is better in Myanmar?

No. When authorities cut mobile data in a conflict or border area, no carrier or eSIM will connect there. Download offline maps and tell people at home you will be unreachable for stretches.

### How do I know if a shutdown is in effect where I am?

Civil-society monitoring at the Myanmar Internet Project publishes township-level tracking of cuts, and local contacts usually know faster than any map. The practical signs: your phone shows full bars but nothing loads, other carriers are equally dead, and it resolves without you changing anything.

### Can I use my eSIM and home SIM together?

Yes. Keep your home SIM for calls/SMS (disable its data roaming to avoid charges) and run data on the eSIM. Most data-only eSIMs cannot receive SMS, so verification codes still arrive on your home number.

### Why do travellers avoid Mytel?

Mytel is one of the largest networks by subscribers but is military-linked, and many visitors choose to skip it on principle. It is a working network; the avoidance is an ethical choice, not a coverage one.

### What is U9, and is it worth using?

U9 is the former Ooredoo Myanmar, renamed from 20 September 2025. It is now a smaller player with modest data packs — fine in cities, not a serious rural option, so most visitors pick ATOM or MPT instead.

### What size plan suits a Myanmar itinerary?

A 5 GB pack (~US$9 on MPT) covers a week of maps, messaging and light browsing in the cities. Temple-region and lake trips use less than you expect because you are often offline between sites — download offline maps before you leave the trunk roads.

### Top-up options for Myanmar eSIMs

Yes. MPT top-ups run via `*682#` and ATOM via its app or kiosks, but foreign-card friction is real, so carry some kyat or use a travel eSIM billed in your currency.

### ATOM vs MPT: coverage compared

The official e-visa is the standard route for most nationalities (visa-on-arrival is suspended for many). You will not have roaming data on arrival, so buy a registered SIM at the airport counter or install a travel eSIM before you fly.

### Where to find a Myanmar eSIM

Not realistically. Every Myanmar SIM must be registered in person against your passport and visa, whether at an airport counter, an operator store or an authorised kiosk. There is no online foreigner sign-up with document upload the way there is in the Maldives or Thailand — which is exactly why travel eSIMs, activated by the provider on your behalf, are the lower-friction route.

### Should I bring a VPN?

A VPN is legally restricted under the 2025 cybersecurity law and enforcement is real, so we do not advise relying on one to reach blocked services. Plan for a monitored, occasionally interrupted connection instead.
