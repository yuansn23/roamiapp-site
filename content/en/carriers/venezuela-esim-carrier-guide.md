# Venezuela eSIM: Skip the SIM Kiosk Lines, Connect Online

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For Venezuela, skip physical SIM kiosks and use an eSIM from **Digitel** for the best nationwide 4G/5G coverage, especially in remote areas like Angel Falls. **Movistar** offers strong urban speeds in Caracas and Maracaibo. Both avoid roaming fees and connect instantly. Ideal for avoiding language barriers and long lines.

## Things you must know before buying a Venezuela eSIM


### Device compatibility – is your phone ready? {#device-compatibility--is-your-phone-ready}
Most modern smartphones support eSIM. Check your device's <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> before purchasing. iPhones from XS/XR, Google Pixel 3a and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer are generally compatible.

### Activation & setup – no physical SIM needed {#activation--setup--no-physical-sim-needed}
After purchase, you'll receive a QR code via email. Scan it in your phone's cellular settings, enable data roaming, and you're online. No need to visit a kiosk or speak Spanish to buy a local SIM.

### Coverage considerations – power outages & remote areas {#coverage-considerations--power-outages--remote-areas}
Venezuela experiences occasional power outages, especially in rural regions. Carry a power bank. For trips to Angel Falls, Los Roques, or the Andes, Digitel offers the best rural coverage.
