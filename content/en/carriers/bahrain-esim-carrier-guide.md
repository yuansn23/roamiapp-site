## The Registration Process



Bahrain's Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (TRA) issued [Decision No. 1 of 2026](https://www.vista-compliance.com/news/newsposts/2026/newspost260324), covering **international-only eSIM roaming services**. The practical points:



- Licensed operators may issue eSIMs that work **only on foreign networks**, are **data-only**, and **cannot be used inside Bahrain**.

- These are **exempt from the standard SIM registration and verification** that a normal Bahraini SIM requires.

- They are capped at **90 days** and must stay technically locked to international use.

- Crucially: if such an eSIM is ever **activated within Bahrain**, standard registration rules snap back into force.



For a visitor this matters because it is the regulatory basis for the travel eSIM model — buy before you fly, install at home, and you arrive online with no passport registration at the counter. It also means a Bahrain eSIM bought from a global provider is a different animal from a local Batelco/Zain/stc prepaid SIM.



## Before you buy a Bahrain eSIM: documents, biometrics and fees



Because the island is small, the friction is at the counter, not on the mast. Get these ready so a two-minute Bahrain eSIM purchase doesn't become a 20-minute ordeal.



| What | Detail | Why it matters |

|:---|:---|:---|

| **Original passport** | Digital photos / copies are refused | Bahrain law requires in-person ID registration; no passport, no SIM |

| **Fingerprint biometrics** | The counter takes a fingerprint, not just the passport | This is unusual — budget 10–15 minutes, longer than a passport-only country |

| **Bahraini dinars (BHD)** | Foreign cards rejected at some top-up points | Keep cash for recharges; 1 BHD ≈ USD 2.65 |

| **Wi-Fi** | Install a travel eSIM at home, not at the airport | Bahrain Airport Wi-Fi is free but busy on arrival waves |

| **Hotel / local address** | Some counters ask for a stay address | Have your hotel name ready if prompted |



The biometric step is the one that surprises people (see [Bahrain SIM guidance](https://www.expatfocus.com/bahrain/guide/bahrain-landlines-and-mobile-phones)). Even a local Bahraini eSIM can't be issued remotely — the fingerprint must be captured at the counter — which is exactly why a pre-installed travel eSIM (no passport, no fingerprint) is the lower-friction Bahrain eSIM path for a short visit.

Batelco is the former monopoly and the operator most visitors name first. It is consistently the fastest in Manama's centre and the financial district, and it runs the broadest retail footprint. It has also rebranded its consumer business under **Beyon**, so you'll see both names.

**How a visitor buys Batelco:**

## Crossing the King Fahd Causeway with a Bahrain eSIM



This is the cross-border reality every Bahrain itinerary eventually hits, and it is where a Bahrain-only plan stops dead.



- The **King Fahd Causeway** is a ~25 km road-and-bridge link to Saudi Arabia. Your Bahraini plan has **no coverage once you cross** — there is no automatic roaming onto Saudi masts, and direct roaming is expensive.

- On the causeway itself there is a **Viva/stc kiosk at the Bahrain Customs building** before the toll plaza that sells prepaid SIMs, handy if you are driving across.

- stc Bahrain sells dedicated [KSA roaming packs](https://wikitechlibrary.com/stc-bahrain-internet-packages/): roughly **BHD 7 for 3 GB** (free incoming + GCC calls) and **BHD 20 for 40 GB**, plus a 16 GB / 300-min option — far cheaper than pay-as-you-go roaming.

- A **Gulf regional eSIM** that names both Bahrain and Saudi Arabia is the clean fix if your trip takes in more than one Gulf state.

- **Near the causeway**, switch off automatic network selection so your phone doesn't grab a Saudi tower and bill Bahrain roaming while you are still on the island side.

## Manual APN Setup



Only needed if you bought a local SIM or eSIM straight from Batelco, Zain or stc. A Bahrain eSIM sold as a travel product sets its own APN — don't touch it.



| Carrier | APN | Username | Password |

|:---|:---|:---|:---|

| Batelco (Beyon) | `internet.batelco.com.bh` | blank | blank |

| Zain Bahrain | `internet.zain.com` | blank | blank |

| stc Bahrain | `stc.internet` | blank | blank |



When manual entry is required (older handset, or a profile installed from a code):



- **iPhone:** enter it under Settings → Cellular → your eSIM line → Cellular Data Network

- **Android:** add a new entry under Settings → Connections → Mobile Networks → Access Point Names



Save and reboot, then point mobile data at the eSIM line rather than your home SIM.

### Before you fly: what a Bahraini line will and will not do



Install the profile at home. Bahrain International's Wi-Fi is free but heavy on arrival waves, and kiosk prices sit above town. Settle the difference in kind, too: a local Batelco/Zain/stc prepaid line means a **passport plus a fingerprint at the counter**, and an internet-voice policy that — unlike the UAE or Qatar — still allows WhatsApp and FaceTime. A travel eSIM skips the counter entirely and is data-only, so plan to keep your home line for calls.



### The eSIM is flagged because it was switched on inside Bahrain



This is the new 2026 trap. Under the TRA's [Decision No. 1 of 2026](https://www.vista-compliance.com/news/newsposts/2026/newspost260324), **international-only eSIMs** are exempt from local registration only while they stay on foreign networks. Activate one inside the kingdom and standard registration rules snap back, and the line can be held until you register in person — fingerprint included. Install and first-connect abroad, and keep using it as a travel product rather than a Bahraini number.



### APN values for Batelco, Zain Bahrain and stc Bahrain eSIMs



Bahraini APNs are easy to mistype: `internet.batelco.com.bh`, `internet.zain.com`, `stc.internet`. Re-enter the one that matches your line and reboot. If it still stalls, suspect the allowance: Zain's "Unlimited" prepaid and other capped plans slow down rather than stopping, so a sudden crawl is the fair-use cap, not a mast failure.



### stc Bahrain eSIM tethering blocked on prepaid: the fix



stc blocks hotspot/tethering on prepaid by default, per [its prepaid plan terms](https://www.stc.com.bh/content/upcoming-prepaid-voice-plans). To feed a laptop you must add a data add-on or ask for tethering to be switched on — changing plan alone will not fix it.



### Bahrain GP weekend congestion: why the network crawls



On Grand Prix weekends the Sakhir area funnels an island-sized crowd through one cell cluster. Bars stay full while throughput collapses; Batelco copes best but no network is immune. Expect it between practice and the race, and lean on hotel Wi-Fi for uploads.



### Is your phone eSIM-ready for Bahrain?



Before the King Fahd Causeway toll plaza the strongest signal can already be Saudi, and a Bahrain-only plan will either go dark or — if an operator add-on is live — bill Gulf roaming while you are still on the island side. Set network selection to **manual** on the approach and pick Batelco, Zain or stc by hand, or move to a Gulf regional eSIM that names both countries.

## Common questions



### Can you buy a Bahrain eSIM from Batelco?



Yes. Batelco (Beyon) sells eSIM in-store and via online ordering, but in-store activation carries a **BHD 8** fee as of 2024 and you register with your passport and a fingerprint. A travel eSIM on Batelco's network skips both.



### Bahrain local SIM and travel eSIM compared



Yes. All three operators run arrivals kiosks at Bahrain International Airport, and both Batelco and Zain allow ordering a prepaid line online for airport pickup. Prices at the airport run a little above town. A travel Bahrain eSIM ordered before departure avoids the kiosk, the passport queue and the fingerprint entirely.



### Will you need ID for a Bahrain SIM?



For **international-only eSIMs** — data-only plans that stay on foreign networks and are never activated inside Bahrain — yes, standard registration is waived. A normal Bahraini prepaid SIM still requires passport registration and a fingerprint.



### Bahrain speed test results



Batelco consistently tests fastest in Manama and the main districts; Ookla's August 2026 Global Index puts the whole country 7th globally at 243.48 Mbps median. Zain and stc are close behind in everyday use.



### Where data is cheapest in Bahrain



Roughly comparable for the plan itself. The difference is fees: a local in-store eSIM adds BHD 3.125 (Zain/stc) or BHD 8 (Batelco), while a travel eSIM has no activation fee and no passport step.



### Will my eSIM work driving into Saudi Arabia?



A Bahrain-only plan stops at the King Fahd Causeway. Either buy a Saudi/regional roaming add-on from your operator (stc sells KSA packs from BHD 7) or use a Gulf regional eSIM that names Saudi Arabia.



### Batelco vs Zain Bahrain prices compared



Zain is usually the value leader on prepaid (starters from ~6.30 BHD, visitor plans from 2.5 BHD), with stc and Batelco close behind and pricier tourist bundles. Cable.co.uk prices local data at ≈ USD 1.31/GB, so even the "expensive" options are cheap by world standards.



### Are WhatsApp calls blocked in Bahrain?



No — unlike the UAE or Qatar, Bahrain keeps an open policy on internet voice, so WhatsApp, FaceTime and similar apps generally work on both local networks and Wi-Fi. You can choose a plan on price and data alone without worrying about call-app blocking.



### Why does the counter need my fingerprint?



Bahrain requires biometric registration for any SIM, not just a passport scan. Budget 10–15 minutes at the counter; it's the main reason a travel eSIM (no biometrics, no passport) is lower-friction for short visits.



### Can you keep your home line active while using a Bahrain eSIM?



Yes — keep your home line for calls/SMS, run data on the eSIM, and turn off data roaming on the home line to avoid charges.



### Does tethering work on a Bahrain eSIM?



Not by default — stc prepaid blocks hotspot/tethering unless you add a data add-on or request it. Other Bahraini operators are more permissive, but if sharing matters, confirm at the counter or with travel-eSIM support.
