After purchase, you'll receive a QR code via email. Scan it in your phone's settings, enable data roaming, and you're connected. No physical SIM swap needed.

### Coverage and speeds {#coverage-and-speeds}
Turkcell offers the best nationwide 5G coverage, while Vodafone excels in cities. Türk Telekom is a solid budget option. All support hotspot tethering.
