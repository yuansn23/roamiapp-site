# Kuwait eSIM: Instant Plug-and-Play Travel Internet

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For most travelers, Zain eSIM offers the best nationwide 5G coverage and consistent speeds, ideal for navigating Kuwait City and visiting the Grand Mosque. If you need affordable high-speed data for streaming and social media, Ooredoo eSIM is a strong alternative. Both support instant activation and avoid roaming fees, making them perfect for short trips.

## Things you must know before buying a Kuwait eSIM


### Device compatibility check {#device-compatibility-check}
Before purchasing, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern phones (iPhone XS and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer) are compatible. Check our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page for a full list.

### Activation and setup {#activation-and-setup}
After purchase, you will receive a QR code via email. Scan it with your phone's camera, follow the on-screen prompts, and your eSIM will be ready. **Make sure to enable data roaming** in your mobile settings once you land in Kuwait.

### Network coverage in Kuwait {#network-coverage-in-kuwait}
Both Zain and Ooredoo provide excellent coverage in Kuwait City and major highways. In remote desert areas, Zain has a slight edge. 5G is widely available in urban centers.
