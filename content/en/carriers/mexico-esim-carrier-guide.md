# Mexico eSIM: Perfect for Dual-SIM Devices

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For most travelers, Telcel offers the widest 4G/5G coverage across Mexico, including remote areas near Chichén Itzá. AT&T Mexico provides strong urban speeds and is ideal for dual-SIM users who need reliable connectivity without roaming fees. Both operators support <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> on recent devices.

## Things you must know before buying a Mexico eSIM


### Check device <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> {#check-device-a-hrefcompatibility-classfont-bold-text-blue-600esim-compatibilitya}
Before purchasing, verify that your smartphone supports eSIM. Most recent iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels (3a and later), and Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer models are compatible. Visit our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page for a full list.

### Understand data roaming settings {#understand-data-roaming-settings}
After installing your eSIM, enable data roaming in your mobile settings. Your eSIM will automatically connect to the local network (Telcel or AT&T Mexico) upon arrival. No physical SIM swap is needed.

### Keep your home SIM active {#keep-your-home-sim-active}
With a dual-SIM setup, you can keep your home SIM for calls and texts while using the Mexico eSIM for data. This avoids roaming fees and lets you receive important messages from home.
