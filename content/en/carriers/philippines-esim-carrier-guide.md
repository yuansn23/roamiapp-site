# Philippines eSIM: Supports Seamless Hotspot Tethering

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For most travelers, Globe eSIM offers the best balance of coverage and speed across the Philippines, especially in tourist hubs like Cebu and Palawan. Smart eSIM is a strong alternative for remote island hopping with its extensive rural 4G/LTE reach. Both support seamless hotspot tethering, making them ideal for digital nomads and group travelers needing shared connectivity.

## Things you must know before buying a Philippines eSIM


### Check your device compatibility {#check-your-device-compatibility}
Before purchasing a Philippines eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most recent iPhone models (XS and newer) and many Android devices (Google Pixel, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer) are compatible. Visit our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page to verify your device.

### Understand local network bands {#understand-local-network-bands}
Philippines carriers use specific frequency bands (e.g., 1800 MHz, 2100 MHz, 700 MHz) for 4G and 5G. Most international phones support these bands, but some older Chinese or US models may have limited <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a>. Check your phone's specs against Globe and Smart's band lists.

### Plan your data needs {#plan-your-data-needs}
Consider how much data you'll need for navigation, social media, and hotspot tethering. A 3GB plan usually suffices for a week of light use, but heavy streamers or remote workers should opt for 10GB or more. Both Globe and Smart offer flexible eSIM data plans.
