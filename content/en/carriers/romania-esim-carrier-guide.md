# Romania eSIM: Perfect for Dual-SIM Devices

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For the best overall coverage in Romania, choose Orange eSIM – it leads in 5G availability and rural reach. Vodafone eSIM offers excellent urban speeds, while Digi eSIM is the budget-friendly option for data-heavy travelers. All work seamlessly on dual-SIM devices, eliminating roaming fees.

## Things you must know before buying a Romania eSIM


### 📱 Device compatibility check {#-device-compatibility-check}
Before purchasing a Romania eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most modern iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels (3a and later), and Samsung Galaxy S20+ and later models are compatible. For a full list, check our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page.

### 🔒 Keep your home number active {#-keep-your-home-number-active}
With a dual-SIM setup, you can use a physical SIM for your home number (calls/SMS) and the eSIM for Romanian data. This way, you avoid roaming fees while staying reachable on your usual number.

### 📶 Network selection tips {#-network-selection-tips}
Upon arrival, your device may automatically connect to a partner network. For the best experience, manually select your preferred Romanian operator (Orange, Vodafone, or Digi) in your device's mobile network settings.
