## 4 Deadly Mistakes That Ruin Your eSIM Activation

**Read this before you start.** These are the most common user errors. Avoiding them will save you hours of frustration.

| Mistake | What Happens | How to Avoid |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| **Scanning the QR code with the Camera app** | The camera recognizes the code but does nothing — no profile installs | Always open **Settings > Cellular > Add eSIM** first — never use the standalone Camera app |
| **Deleting the eSIM to "fix" a connection issue** | The profile is permanently removed — you lose all remaining data and must buy a new plan | Never tap "Delete eSIM" unless the plan is fully expired. Deleting does not "refresh" — it destroys |

## Prerequisites – what you need before you start

Before you begin the **iPhone eSIM setup**, verify these requirements. Skipping them is the #1 cause of activation failures.

### 1. Check iPhone compatibility

Apple introduced eSIM starting with the iPhone XS. Compatible models include:
- iPhone XS, XS Max, and XR
- iPhone 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, and 17 series
- iPhone SE (2nd and 3rd generation)

> **Critical difference:** If you purchased an iPhone 14, 15, 16, or 17 in the **USA**, your phone is **eSIM-only** and has no physical SIM tray. All other regions still include a physical SIM slot alongside eSIM support.

### 2. Ensure your iPhone is carrier-unlocked

If you are installing a travel eSIM or switching to a different carrier, your iPhone must be unlocked.

1. Go to **Settings** > **General** > **About**.
2. Scroll down to **Carrier Lock** (or Network Provider Lock).
3. If it says **"No SIM restrictions"**, your iPhone is unlocked and ready.

**If it says "SIM locked":** Contact your current carrier to request an unlock. US carriers are legally required to unlock devices once they are fully paid off. The process typically takes 1–3 business days.

### 3. Connect to a stable Wi-Fi network

You **must** have an active internet connection (Wi-Fi is strongly recommended) to download the carrier profile. The profile is pulled from your carrier's secure SM‑DP+ server — cellular data alone will not work for the initial download.

### 4. Know your iPhone's lock screen passcode

During installation, iOS will prompt you to enter your iPhone's passcode (the same one you use to unlock the screen). This is a security measure to prevent unauthorized installations.

**Make sure you know your passcode before starting.** If you don't, you won't be able to complete the installation.

### 5. Get your QR code ready

Your provider will send a QR code via email. If it's on another device (laptop, tablet, or printed), have it ready. If you're trying to install an eSIM on the same device where the QR code is displayed (e.g., you received the QR code in an email on your iPhone and need to scan it with the same phone), **save the QR code image to your Photos app first**, then use the "Choose Photo" option during activation to select it from your library.

For more in-depth troubleshooting if your carrier lock persists, check our **[Dual eSIM Not Working? 12 Fixes for iPhone](/faq/dual-esim-not-working-12-fixes-for-iphone/)** guide.


## How to check if your iPhone supports eSIM – compatibility check

Before buying an eSIM plan, verify your device has the required hardware. Here are two quick methods:

### Method 1: Dial `*#06#` (most reliable)
1. Open the Phone app and dial `*#06#`.
2. If a 32‑digit **EID** (Embedded Identity Document) appears, your iPhone supports eSIM.
3. If you only see IMEI numbers, your iPhone does not support eSIM (iPhone 8 or earlier).

### Method 2: Check Settings
1. Go to **Settings** > **General** > **About**.
2. Scroll down – if you see **Digital SIM** or **Available SIM** with an EID number, your device is ready.

For the complete list of **eSIM compatible phones** across all brands, see our **[full compatibility list](/compatibility/)**.


## How to install & activate eSIM on iPhone – 3 main methods

Depending on your carrier or provider, there are three primary ways to **activate an eSIM on an iPhone**. Choose the method that matches what you received from your provider.

### Method A: Using a QR code (most common)

This is the standard method for most **travel eSIMs**. Your provider will email you a QR code after purchase.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Cellular** (or **Mobile Data** depending on your region).
2. Tap **Add eSIM** (or **Add Cellular Plan** on older iOS versions).
3. Select **Use QR Code**.
4. **Maximize the QR code** on the screen you're scanning from (make it as large as possible).
5. **Set the display brightness to 100%** on the device showing the QR code — this is the #1 reason scanning fails.
6. Point your camera at the QR code and hold steady. If using the same iPhone where the QR code is saved, tap **"Choose Photo"** and select the QR code image from your Photos library.
7. When the "Cellular Plan Ready to Be Added" notification appears, tap **Continue**.
8. You may be prompted to enter your **iPhone lock screen passcode** — do so to authorize the installation.
9. Follow the on-screen prompts to label your new plan (e.g., "Travel" or "Europe").

**What if the QR code won't scan after 3 attempts?**
- Tap **Enter Details Manually** at the bottom of the camera screen.
- Enter the SM-DP+ Address and Activation Code from your provider's email.
- See Method B below for full manual entry instructions.

### Method B: Manual entry (if you can't scan the QR code)

What if you bought the eSIM on your phone and can't scan the QR code from your own screen? Or the QR code is damaged? You can enter the details manually — this bypasses the camera entirely.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Cellular** > **Add eSIM** > **Use QR Code**.
2. Tap **Enter Details Manually** at the bottom of the camera screen.
3. Input the **SM-DP+ Address** and **Activation Code** — these are provided in your confirmation email.
4. Tap **Next** and follow the prompts to finish the installation.
5. When prompted, enter your **iPhone lock screen passcode**.

> **When to use manual entry:** This is the most reliable fallback when QR scanning fails due to camera issues, damaged codes, or when you're viewing the QR code on the same device you're activating.

### Method C: Direct installation via carrier app

Many modern providers (Roami, Airalo, Holafly, and major carriers) offer in-app installation with a single tap — no QR code needed.

1. Download your provider's app from the App Store.
2. Log in and locate your purchased plan (under "My eSIMs" or "My Plans").
3. Tap the **Install eSIM** or **Activate** button.
4. Your iPhone will prompt you to allow the app to install a cellular plan. Tap **Allow** or **Continue**.
5. Enter your **iPhone lock screen passcode** when prompted.
6. The app automatically communicates with your Settings to install the carrier profile — no QR scanning required.

**This is the fastest method** and avoids the "can't scan your own screen" dilemma entirely.

### After installation – what happens next?

- **Immediately after tapping "Continue"**: The carrier profile downloads and installs. This takes 10–30 seconds.
- **Signal bars appear**: Within 30–60 seconds of completing installation, your iPhone will register on the network. Signal bars should appear in the status bar.
- **If signal bars don't appear after 2 minutes**: Proceed to the [Troubleshooting section](#troubleshooting-common-esim-activation-errors) below. **DO NOT delete the profile** — wait and follow the troubleshooting steps first.

> **Wait time tip:** Some travel eSIMs take up to **2 minutes** to register on the local network after installation — especially in countries where the provider uses multiple carrier partners. Be patient before assuming it failed. If it takes longer than 2 minutes, try toggling Airplane Mode on and off before doing anything else.

### How to confirm activation was successful

After installation, run this quick 3‑step check:

| Step | What to Check | Pass Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Status bar shows signal bars | At least 1‑2 bars visible (not "No Service") |
| **2** | Settings > Cellular > [your eSIM] > "Turn On This Line" | Toggle is ON (green) |
| **3** | Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data | Your eSIM is selected as the data line |
| **4** | Open Safari and load a webpage | Page loads successfully |

If all four pass, your eSIM is fully activated and working. If Step 1 fails (no signal), go to Troubleshooting. If Step 3 fails (bars but no data), go to APN Settings.


## How to configure your eSIM settings for travel

Installing the digital carrier profile is only half the battle. If you are traveling abroad, you need to configure your settings correctly to **prevent expensive roaming charges** on your primary home SIM.

### Step 1: Set your default voice & SMS line

During setup, your iPhone will ask you to choose a default line:
- **Default Voice Line:** Select your Primary (Home) SIM if you want to keep receiving calls and iMessages on your regular number.
- **iMessage & FaceTime:** Select your Primary SIM to keep using your home number for messaging.

### Step 2: Set up cellular data – the critical step for travelers

This determines which line your phone uses for internet. Set it correctly to avoid roaming charges:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Cellular** > **Cellular Data**.
2. Select your new **Travel eSIM** for data.
3. **"Allow Cellular Data Switching"** — what to do with this setting:

| Setting | What It Does | When to Use |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **OFF (recommended for travelers)** | Phone ONLY uses the selected line for data. Never switches to the other line. | **Default for travel.** Prevents accidental roaming charges when your travel eSIM temporarily loses signal. |
| **ON** | Phone may temporarily switch to the other line for data if the selected line loses signal. | Only turn ON if you have **unlimited** or **inexpensive** data on both lines, OR if you're staying in one country and both lines have local data. |

**Why OFF is safer:** If your travel eSIM loses signal for 5 seconds in an elevator, the phone might switch to your home SIM for data — triggering a roaming charge of $10–$15 for that day. Keep it OFF unless you're absolutely sure.

### Step 3: Data Roaming settings

- **On your Primary (Home) SIM:** Go to its settings and ensure **Data Roaming is turned OFF**.
- **On your Travel eSIM:** Ensure **Data Roaming is turned ON** (most travel eSIMs require this to connect to local partner networks).

> **Why Data Roaming ON for travel eSIM?** This is a technical requirement — travel eSIMs connect through partner networks. Toggling it ON does not incur roaming fees; it simply allows the phone to register with the local tower.

### Step 4: APN settings – fix connectivity issues

If your eSIM shows signal bars but **no data connection**, APN settings may need adjustment:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Cellular** > **Cellular Data Network**.
2. Enter the APN value from your provider's website or email.
3. Common APN values:
   - Roami: `internet`
   - Airalo: `globaldata`
   - Holafly: `holafly`
   - Nomad: `nomad`
   - Ubigi: `ubigi`
4. Most travel eSIMs auto‑configure the APN. Only adjust if data isn't working.

Save and restart your iPhone if data doesn't connect after entering APN details.


## eSIM Quick Transfer (iPhone to iPhone)

Apple's built-in Quick Transfer lets you move an existing carrier profile from your old iPhone to a new one without a new QR code. This is the easiest way to **transfer eSIM to new iPhone**.

**How it works:**
1. During setup of your new iPhone, choose **"Transfer eSIM from nearby iPhone"** when prompted.
2. Keep both phones close (within 10cm) with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled.
3. Confirm the transfer on your old iPhone.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes for the profile to download to the new device.

**If Quick Transfer doesn't appear:**
1. On the new iPhone, go to **Settings** > **Cellular** > **Add eSIM**.
2. Select **Transfer from Nearby iPhone**.
3. Follow the prompts.

**If Quick Transfer fails:** Contact your carrier to request a new QR code for the new device.

**If the eSIM appears on the new iPhone but shows "No Service":**
- Some carriers require you to **delete the eSIM from the old iPhone** before it will activate on the new one.
- On the old iPhone: Settings > Cellular > [eSIM] > Delete eSIM.
- Then restart the new iPhone and wait 1–2 minutes for the profile to register.

### What if my old iPhone is already sold, reset, or lost?

If you've already sold, reset, or lost your old iPhone and didn't transfer the eSIM first:

**For carrier eSIMs (Verizon, T‑Mobile, AT&T, etc.):**
1. Contact your carrier's customer support.
2. Tell them you have a new device and need to re‑activate your eSIM.
3. They will ask for your new iPhone's **EID** and **IMEI** (Settings > General > About).
4. They'll push a new profile to your new iPhone or send a QR code.

**For travel eSIMs (Roami, Airalo, Holafly, etc.):**
1. Check your original confirmation email — some providers allow you to download a new QR code from a link in the email.
2. If not, contact their support with your order number.
3. Most travel eSIMs are single‑use, so you may need to purchase a new plan.

**Pro tip:** Before selling or resetting your old iPhone, always delete the eSIM first (Settings > Cellular > [eSIM] > Delete eSIM). This releases the profile from the device and makes it easier to re‑activate on a new one.


## Cross-platform transfer (Android ↔ iPhone)

Transferring an eSIM between iPhone and Android is rarely supported directly. Most carriers and travel providers do not allow this because the carrier profile is bound to the original device's EID.

**What to do instead:**
1. Contact your carrier to request a new QR code for the new device.
2. Or, if using a travel eSIM, purchase a new plan for the new device — most travel eSIMs are single‑use and cannot be transferred across platforms.

**Exception:** Some carriers (like Google Fi, US Mobile, and some European operators) allow you to download a new QR code from their app after deactivating the old device. Check your carrier's policy before assuming you need a new plan.


## Troubleshooting common eSIM activation errors

Running into issues? Here are the most common errors and how to fix them — grouped by when they occur.

### Before activation (QR code / setup issues)

| Error / Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| **QR code says "Invalid" or "Expired"** | QR code may have expired (most are time‑limited) | Contact carrier for a new QR code |
| **"Cellular Plan Cannot Be Added"** | iPhone is carrier-locked, or QR code already used | Check Carrier Lock under `Settings > General > About`. If unlocked, contact your provider for a new QR code |
| **QR code won't scan** | Poor lighting, camera focus, or **low screen brightness** | Tap **Enter Details Manually** and input the SM‑DP+ address and activation code instead. Also, set brightness to 100% on the device showing the QR code |
| **"Add eSIM" option is grayed out** | iPhone is carrier‑locked or an older model | Check Carrier Lock. If locked, contact your carrier for unlock. If older than iPhone XS, eSIM is not supported |
| **QR code won't scan from same iPhone** | Can't scan a QR code on your own screen | Tap "Enter Details Manually" or save the QR code to Photos and use the "Choose Photo" option |

### During activation (download / server issues)

| Error / Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| **Stuck on "Activating..." for more than 2 minutes** | Weak Wi‑Fi or carrier network congestion | **DO NOT DELETE.** Toggle Airplane Mode on for 30 seconds, then off. Restart iPhone. Wait 5‑10 minutes. If still stuck, delete the partial profile and try again — but only after trying the above first |
| **"eSIM profile not supported" error** | Carrier profile requires features your phone lacks | Update to the latest iOS version. Contact provider to confirm compatibility |
| **Download fails / cuts out** | Unstable Wi‑Fi connection | Delete the partial eSIM (`Settings > Cellular > tap incomplete eSIM > Delete`), restart, and retry on a more stable network |
| **"Unable to download profile – network error"** | Wi‑Fi connection issues or carrier server problems | Switch to a different Wi‑Fi network or use mobile hotspot. Try again in 5‑10 minutes — carrier servers may be temporarily overloaded |

### After activation (connection / service issues)

| Error / Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| **"No Service" after activation** | Data Roaming not turned ON, or eSIM line not active | Double-check **Data Roaming** is ON for the eSIM line. Ensure the eSIM is toggled ON in `Settings > Cellular` |
| **No data connection (signal bars show)** | APN settings missing or incorrect | Manually configure APN settings (see APN section above) |
| **"eSIM already in use" / bound to another device** | Profile never released from old device | On the old device, delete the eSIM if still visible. If the old device is lost, contact your carrier to force‑release the profile |
| **eSIM installed but doesn't appear in Settings** | iOS bug or incomplete installation | Go to **Settings > General > About** and scroll to "Available SIM" or "Digital SIM". If the eSIM appears there but not in Cellular, restart the iPhone |
| **"No Service" on travel eSIM after crossing border** | The eSIM plan may be country‑specific and doesn't include the new country | Check your plan's coverage map. If you need coverage in multiple countries, buy a regional or global plan instead |
| **Signal drops every few minutes** | Network selection issue | Go to **Settings > Cellular > [eSIM] > Network Selection** → turn OFF Automatic → pick a different network manually |

### Advanced troubleshooting: "Cellular Plan Cannot Be Added" with no carrier lock

If your phone is carrier-unlocked but still shows this error:

1. **Reset network settings:** Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Reset > Reset Network Settings. (Warning: this forgets Wi‑Fi passwords.)
2. **Check for a pending iOS update:** Settings > General > Software Update.
3. **Delete other stored eSIM profiles:** Go to Settings > Cellular — delete any eSIMs you no longer use.
4. **Factory reset** (last resort): Back up your data first.

## eSIM and Apple Watch – what you need to know

If you have an Apple Watch paired to your iPhone, here's how the digital SIM interacts with it:

- **Apple Watch can mirror ONE line** from your iPhone. It cannot use both lines simultaneously.
- During Apple Watch setup, you'll be asked which cellular plan you want to use on the watch.
- Choose the line you want the watch to use — typically your primary home number, not the travel eSIM.
- If you want the watch to use a different line, you'll need to unpair and re-pair the watch, selecting the other line during setup.

**Important:** The Apple Watch cannot use a travel eSIM independently of the iPhone. It mirrors a line from the iPhone's active SIMs.


## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many eSIMs can I have on my iPhone?**
You can store 8 or more eSIM profiles on an iPhone. However, depending on your model (iPhone 13 and newer), you can only have **two eSIMs active at the same time** (Dual eSIM). Older models (iPhone XS–12) support one active eSIM plus one physical SIM.

**Can I use my physical SIM and eSIM at the same time?**
Yes — iPhones support Dual SIM functionality. You can keep your physical home SIM active for calls and texts while using a travel eSIM for data. In the US, iPhone 14 and newer are eSIM‑only, so you'll use two eSIMs instead of a physical SIM.

**Can I delete my eSIM after my trip?**
Yes. Once your trip is over and the plan has expired, go to **Settings > Cellular**, tap the eSIM, and select **Delete eSIM**.
> ⚠️ **WARNING:** NEVER delete your eSIM while you are still traveling. Most QR codes are single‑use. If you delete it, you cannot re‑scan the same code — you will need to buy a new plan.

**How long does eSIM activation take?**
The installation itself takes 10–30 seconds. However, network registration can take up to 2 minutes. If you don't see signal bars within 2 minutes, proceed to the troubleshooting section. **Do not delete the profile during this waiting period.**

**Does using an eSIM drain my iPhone battery faster?**
Using an eSIM consumes roughly the same power as a physical SIM. Running two active lines in an area with poor signal can cause slight battery drain as your phone searches for networks, but the difference is minimal (1-3% per day).

**What is the difference between an eSIM and a physical SIM?**
An eSIM is a digital SIM that cannot be physically removed. It offers the same functionality as a physical SIM but with greater flexibility: you can switch carriers, store multiple profiles, and activate plans instantly without waiting for a physical card. For travelers, **eSIM vs physical SIM** is a clear win.

**How do I know if my phone is eSIM compatible?**
Dial `*#06#` — if an EID appears, your phone supports eSIM. Or check **Settings > General > About** for a Digital SIM or EID entry. The official [Apple support page for eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108072) provides a detailed list of compatible models.

**Can I transfer my eSIM from one phone to another?**
Yes — using Quick Transfer (iPhone to iPhone) or via your carrier (iPhone ↔ Android). See our **[eSIM Cross-Platform Transfer guide](/faq/how-to-transfer-esim-between-iphone-and-android/)** for step‑by‑step instructions.

**What is a travel eSIM?**
A **travel eSIM** is a data‑only eSIM designed for international trips. You buy it before departure, install it at home (while on Wi‑Fi), and activate it upon arrival by turning on Data Roaming — no roaming fees, no airport SIM kiosks, no language barriers.

**Do I need Data Roaming turned on for a travel eSIM?**
Yes — for most travel eSIMs, Data Roaming must be turned ON for that line. This does not incur roaming charges; it simply allows the phone to register with the local partner network. Many travelers miss this step and end up with "No Service."

**Why is my eSIM showing "Activating" for more than 10 minutes?**
The activation server may be busy, or your Wi‑Fi connection is unstable. Toggle Airplane Mode on and off. If it stays stuck, delete the pending eSIM (`Settings > Cellular > tap the eSIM > Delete`) and try again on a more stable network. Only delete after you've waited at least 10 minutes and tried Airplane Mode.

**Can I use an eSIM without a physical SIM?**
Yes — if your phone supports eSIM, you can use it as your only line. This is especially relevant for US iPhone 14 and newer models, which have no physical SIM slot at all.

**What's the most common reason for "No Service" after activation?**
Data Roaming is turned OFF for the eSIM line. Go to Settings > Cellular > [your eSIM] and toggle Data Roaming ON. This is the #1 fix.

**Why does my eSIM have signal bars but no internet?**
This is almost always an APN issue. See the APN configuration section above for step‑by‑step instructions. The most common APN values are listed there.

**Can I install an eSIM on my iPhone without Wi‑Fi?**
No — you need a stable internet connection (Wi‑Fi) to download the carrier profile from your carrier's secure server.

**What if I accidentally delete my eSIM while traveling?**
Contact your provider immediately. For travel eSIMs, deletion is usually permanent — you'll need to purchase a new plan. For postpaid carrier eSIMs (Verizon, T‑Mobile, AT&T), they can often re‑issue a new QR code.

**What's the difference between iOS 17 and iOS 18 for eSIM activation?**
The core process is identical. iOS 18 offers slightly improved UI with better labeling suggestions and signal strength indicators during network selection. See the iOS version differences section above.

**My old iPhone is already sold/reset — can I still get my eSIM back?**
Yes — for carrier eSIMs, contact your carrier with your new iPhone's EID and IMEI. For travel eSIMs, check your original confirmation email for a re‑download link or contact support. See the Quick Transfer section for details.
