## Android eSIM Compatibility: The Quick Answer for Your Phone

Unlike iPhones and Google Pixels (which have consistent eSIM support across most models), the rest of the Android world is a fragmented landscape. Some brands offer **esim android** on their flagship phones; others do not at all. Many mid‑range and budget phones lack the necessary hardware or firmware.

If you own a Motorola, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Huawei, LG, Sony, Nokia, Asus, Honor, Realme, Oppo, Vivo, iQOO, Poco, Infinix, Tecno, or itel – this guide will tell you exactly which models support eSIM, how to verify your own device, and what to do if your phone doesn't have eSIM.

The [GSMA](https://www.gsma.com/esim/) defines the technical standards that enable eSIM functionality across devices worldwide. This standardization ensures that **esim compatible android phones** from different brands can work with the same eSIM profiles.

**The single most reliable check:** Open your phone dialer, enter `*#06#`, and look for a 32‑digit **EID** number. If you see it, your phone supports eSIM – regardless of brand or model. If not, it doesn't.

For a complete **android phones with esim list**, see our [master compatibility guide](/compatibility/). The **esim android phones** that work reliably are mostly flagships from OnePlus, Sony, Motorola (Razr), and Huawei (global). Budget **esim compatible android phones** are rare.

## How to Check If Your Android Phone Supports eSIM (Universal Method)

Before diving into brand‑specific details, use this universal method to check **does my android phone have esim**:

1. Open the **Phone dialer** app.
2. Dial `*#06#` (just like a phone number) and press call.
3. Look at the screen. If you see a number labeled **EID** (Embedded Identity Document) – typically 32 digits – your phone has eSIM hardware.
4. If you only see IMEI numbers (IMEI1, IMEI2) and no EID, your phone does **not** support eSIM.

This code works on all Android phones – Motorola, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Huawei, LG, Sony, etc. It's the fastest way to get a definitive answer to **does my android phone have esim**. If you're shopping for a new phone, the tables below tell you which models are known to work.

## What to Do If Your Android Phone Lacks eSIM Support

If you dialed `*#06#` and saw no EID, your phone cannot use eSIM. Here are your best alternatives:

1. **Use a physical travel SIM** – Many eSIM providers also offer physical SIM cards shipped to your home. They take longer to arrive but work on any phone.
2. **Buy a local physical SIM at your destination** – Almost every country sells prepaid SIM cards at airports or convenience stores. This is often cheaper than travel eSIMs anyway.
3. **Upgrade to a newer phone** – Consider a Google Pixel 6a or newer (full eSIM support), a Samsung Galaxy S21 or newer, or one of the eSIM‑capable models listed in the tables above. Used prices are often under $200. You can check our [full list of eSIM compatible phones](/compatibility/) for more options.
4. **Use a portable eSIM hotspot** – Devices like GlocalMe or Numen Air create a Wi‑Fi network using an eSIM. Your phone connects via Wi‑Fi, so your phone doesn't need eSIM itself.

## How to Activate eSIM on Supported Android Phones

If your phone passed the `*#06#` test, activation is similar across brands:

1. **Buy an eSIM plan** – Start with a [free Roami eSIM trial](/free-esim/) to test.
2. Go to **Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Add Carrier** (exact wording varies by brand).
   - **Motorola:** Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Advanced > Carrier > Add eSIM
   - **OnePlus:** Settings > Wi‑Fi & Network > SIM & Network > Add eSIM
   - **Xiaomi (global):** Settings > Mobile Network > SIM cards > Add eSIM
   - **Huawei:** Settings > Mobile Network > eSIM > Add eSIM profile
   - **Sony:** Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Advanced > Carrier > Add eSIM
3. **Scan the QR code** provided by your eSIM provider.
4. Follow prompts to download the profile.
5. Turn on **Data Roaming** for the eSIM line when traveling.

For more detailed instructions, see our [how to activate an eSIM guide](/faq/how-to-activate-an-esim/). If you run into issues, our [eSIM troubleshooting guide](/faq/eSIM-Deep-Troubleshooting-Guide-2026/) covers 16 real cases.
