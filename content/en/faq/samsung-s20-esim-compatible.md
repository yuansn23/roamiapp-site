**The short answer:** If you own a Galaxy S21 or newer (S21, S22, S23, S24, S25), **yes** – your phone supports eSIM in all regions, including the US. If you're asking **"does Samsung have eSIM"** – the answer is yes, but it depends on your specific model.

## How to Identify Your Samsung Model for eSIM Compatibility

One of the biggest reasons Samsung users get confused is they don't know which version of their phone they have. The same model name can have eSIM in one region but not in another.

### Step 1: Find your model number

Go to **Settings > About Phone > Model Number**. You'll see something like `SM-S918B` or `SM-S918U`.

### Step 2: Decode your model number suffix

| Suffix | Meaning | eSIM Support |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| **SM-S9xxB, SM-S9xxE** | International / Global (Exynos) | ✅ Yes (on S20 and newer) |
| **SM-S9xxU, SM-S9xxU1** | USA (Snapdragon) | ⚠️ S20 no, S21+ yes |
| **SM-S9xxW** | Canada | ⚠️ Mixed – check with `*#06#` |
| **SM-S9xx0** | China / Hong Kong | ❌ No eSIM (China models) |
| **SM-S9xxN** | Korea | ✅ Yes (most models) |
| **SC-xx** | Japan (Docomo) | ❌ Often disabled |
| **SM-AxxE, SM-AxxB** | International A-series | ⚠️ Only A54/A55/A35 have eSIM |
| **SM-AxxU** | USA A-series | ❌ No eSIM on any US A-series |

### Step 3: Check your CSC code (Samsung-specific region code)

On Samsung phones, the **CSC code** determines which region's firmware you're running:

1. Go to **Settings > About Phone > Software Information**.
2. Scroll to **Service Provider SW Ver**.
3. Look at the last three letters of the CSC code:

| CSC Code | Region | eSIM Support |
|----------|--------|--------------|
| **XAA, XAR** | USA (unlocked) | ✅ Yes (S21+) |
| **TMB, VZW, ATT, SPR** | USA carrier (T-Mobile, Verizon, AT&T, Sprint) | ⚠️ S21+ yes on T-Mobile/Verizon; AT&T may restrict |
| **EUX, EUY, BTU, DBT** | Europe / UK / Germany | ✅ Yes |
| **INS, INU** | India | ✅ Yes |
| **CHC, CHN** | China | ❌ No eSIM |
| **KOO, KTC, SKC** | Korea | ✅ Yes |
| **DCM, SBM, KDI** | Japan (Docomo, SoftBank, au) | ❌ Often disabled |

**The bottom line:** If your model number ends in **"U"** (US) and it's an S20 – no eSIM. If it ends in **"B"** or **"E"** (international) – eSIM is available. If you have a China model (ending in **"0"** or CSC **"CHC"**) – no eSIM, no fix.

## How to Verify eSIM on Any Samsung Phone

### Method 1: Dial `*#06#` (most reliable)
1. Open the Phone app.
2. Dial `*#06#` and press call.
3. Look for a **32-digit EID** (Embedded Identity Document). If present, your device has eSIM hardware.
4. If you only see IMEI numbers (IMEI1, IMEI2), eSIM is not available.

This method works for **Samsung eSIM activation** check on any model, from S series to A series.

### Method 2: Check Settings menu
- Go to **Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager**.
- If you see **"Add eSIM"** or **"Download eSIM"**, your phone supports eSIM.
- If those options are missing, eSIM is disabled or not present.

### Method 3: Software update check
For some models (like S20 international), eSIM was added via One UI 3.1 (Android 11). Ensure you're on the latest version:
- **Settings > Software Update > Download and Install**.

### Method 4: Check your CSC code
On Samsung phones, eSIM availability is sometimes tied to the CSC (region) code. See the [model identification section](#how-to-identify-your-samsung-model-for-esim-compatibility) above for details.

### Method 5: Still unsure? Try a free trial eSIM
Nothing beats a real test. A **[free trial eSIM](/free-esim/)** takes 2 minutes to install and confirms your phone is ready before you spend any money on a full plan.


## How to Activate eSIM on Supported Samsung Phones

Once you've confirmed that your **Samsung phones with eSIM** includes your model, activation is straightforward:

1. **Purchase an eSIM plan** (for travel or daily use).
2. Go to **Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager > Add eSIM**.
3. **Scan the eSIM QR code** provided by your carrier (or enter activation code manually).
4. Follow on-screen instructions to download the profile.
5. Set your eSIM as default for calls, SMS, or mobile data.
6. **Turn on Data Roaming** when traveling.

### Activation Path by One UI Version

| One UI Version | Path to "Add eSIM" |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **One UI 6.0+ (Android 14+)** | Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager > Add eSIM |
| **One UI 5.0–5.1 (Android 13)** | Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager > Add eSIM |
| **One UI 4.0–4.1 (Android 12)** | Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager > Add eSIM (may be under "Mobile networks") |
| **One UI 3.1 (Android 11)** | Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager > Add eSIM (S20 international only) |

> If you don't see **"Add eSIM"**, your device may have eSIM disabled by carrier firmware – even if hardware exists. Skip to the [troubleshooting section](#troubleshooting-esim-not-working-on-samsung) below.

**Pro Tip:** Install your eSIM before you leave home while connected to stable Wi-Fi. The profile sits inactive on your phone until you turn on Data Roaming upon arrival – no need to wait until you land.

**Common Samsung eSIM activation issue:** Some users see **"Your carrier does not support this feature"** when trying to add an eSIM. This usually means:
- Your phone is carrier‑locked (check Settings > About Phone > Status > SIM Lock Status)
- Your phone is a US S20 (eSIM permanently disabled)
- Your phone has a China CSC (no eSIM support)


## Dual SIM on Samsung: Physical SIM + eSIM

Most eSIM-capable Samsung phones support **DSDS (Dual SIM Dual Standby)**:

- You can use **one physical nano-SIM** and **one eSIM** simultaneously.
- Both numbers stay active – calls and texts can be received on either line.
- You choose which SIM handles mobile data.

This is especially useful for:
- **Travel** – keep your home SIM active for 2FA texts while using a local data eSIM.
- **Work/personal** – separate numbers on one phone.
- **Testing carriers** – try a free eSIM without removing your main SIM.

Newer models (S23 and later) also support **dual eSIM** (two active eSIMs, no physical SIM) – but the physical+eSIM combo is the most common setup for travelers.

### How to Enable Dual SIM on Samsung

1. Go to **Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager**.
2. Under **Preferred SIMs**, choose which SIM handles:
   - **Calls** – choose your primary voice line
   - **Messages** – choose your primary SMS line
   - **Mobile Data** – choose which SIM uses data
3. Toggle on **Allow Data Switching** – this lets your phone temporarily use the other SIM's data if the primary loses signal.

**Important:** If you're using a travel eSIM for data and your home physical SIM for calls, set:
- **Calls** → Physical SIM
- **Mobile Data** → eSIM
- **Data Roaming** → OFF on physical SIM, ON on eSIM


## eSIM vs Physical SIM on Samsung – Which Should You Use?

If your Samsung phone supports both eSIM and physical SIM, here's how they compare to help you decide.

| Factor | eSIM | Physical SIM |
|--------|------|--------------|
| **Activation** | Scan QR code, done in 2 minutes | Insert card into SIM tray |
| **Buy before travel?** | ✅ Yes – buy online from anywhere | ❌ No – must buy at destination or order in advance |
| **Lost/stolen phone** | ✅ More secure – can't be removed by thief | ❌ Card can be removed and used |
| **Switching phones** | ⚠️ One‑time install (most travel eSIMs) – check provider policy | ✅ Easy – just move the card |
| **Multiple profiles** | ✅ Store multiple eSIMs, switch as needed | ❌ Only one carrier per card |
| **Availability** | S21+ only, certain A series and Z series | All Samsung phones |
| **Cost** | Travel eSIMs often cheaper than international roaming | Local physical SIMs cheaper for long‑term stays |
| **Regional support** | Works globally with travel eSIM providers | Depends on local carrier availability |

**Which should you choose?**
- **For travel (1-30 days):** eSIM wins – buy before you leave, activate on arrival, no hunting for SIM cards at the airport.
- **For long‑term stays (3+ months):** Local physical SIM usually offers better per‑GB rates and a local phone number.
- **For maximum flexibility:** Use both – keep your home physical SIM for calls/SMS and a travel eSIM for data (DSDS).


## Why Do Some Samsung Phones Lack eSIM? – Regional Differences Explained

Samsung's eSIM support varies by region due to carrier demands and firmware choices. Understanding these regional differences is key to picking the right **Samsung Galaxy eSIM** device.

### USA
- **S21 and newer** – full eSIM support on all carriers (Verizon, T-Mobile, AT&T, and unlocked).
- **S20 series** – eSIM disabled on **all** US Snapdragon models. This includes carrier and "unlocked" US versions.
- **Note 20 Ultra US** – no eSIM.
- **Z Fold/Flip US carrier models** – sometimes eSIM is locked; factory unlocked versions are safer.

### US Carrier-Specific Notes

| Carrier | eSIM on Samsung | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **T-Mobile** | ✅ Yes (S21+) | Supports eSIM on all S21+ models, including locked phones |
| **Verizon** | ✅ Yes (S21+) | Supports eSIM on S21+; may require app-based activation |
| **AT&T** | ⚠️ Limited | Supports eSIM on unlocked S21+; AT&T-branded may have restrictions |
| **Google Fi** | ✅ Yes | Works seamlessly on all eSIM-capable Samsung phones |

### International / Global (Exynos)
- Most international models (Europe, Asia, Middle East, Africa) support eSIM from S20 onward.
- Exynos versions of S20, Note 20 Ultra, and older flagships have eSIM enabled via software updates.

### Canada
- Mixed support. Some Canadian S20 models gained eSIM after updates; newer S-series work.

### China / Hong Kong
- Samsung does not enable eSIM on phones sold in mainland China (regulatory reasons). Hong Kong models often work.

### Japan
- Japanese carrier variants (Docomo, au, SoftBank) frequently disable eSIM – similar to Pixel situation.

> **Bottom line:** For guaranteed eSIM, buy a **Galaxy S21 or newer** from any region, or an **unlocked international S20** (Exynos). For a comparable Android alternative, see our **[Google Pixel eSIM compatibility guide](/faq/google-pixel-6-esim-compatible/)** .

## Samsung A Series eSIM Support – What Works and What Doesn't

The A series is complicated – most models lack eSIM entirely, but a few newer ones include it.

### A Series Models WITHOUT eSIM (Most Common)

The following models do not have eSIM hardware or firmware support: Galaxy A15, A16, A32, A33, A51, A52, A52s, A53, A71, A72, A73, as well as the A12, A22, and A13. The Galaxy A15, A16, and A32 do not support eSIM. These budget models rely solely on physical SIM cards.

**Important for travelers:** The Galaxy A15 5G and A16 5G have 5G but **lack eSIM**. 5G capability does not imply eSIM support – this is a common misconception.

### A Series Models WITH eSIM (Newer)

Starting in 2024, Samsung added eSIM to select higher-tier A series phones:
- **Galaxy A54** – supports eSIM (5G model).
- **Galaxy A55** – supports eSIM (successor to A54).
- **Galaxy A35** – supports eSIM (budget-friendly but includes eSIM).
- **Galaxy A56** (expected 2026) – likely will support eSIM.

For these, dial `*#06#` to confirm EID. Even if an A series phone has eSIM hardware, some carrier-locked versions may disable it – unlocked versions are safest.

Most Samsung foldables from the Z Fold2 and Z Flip3 onward support eSIM. This includes the Z Fold2, Fold3, Fold4, Fold5, Fold6, Fold7, as well as the Z Flip3, Flip4, Flip5, Flip6, and Flip7. Always buy factory unlocked for guaranteed **Samsung phones with eSIM** functionality.

Foldables also support dual SIM: one physical SIM + one eSIM, or dual eSIM (on newer models).


## Samsung Note Series eSIM Support

- **Note 20 Ultra (international Exynos)** – supports eSIM after One UI 3.1 update.
- **Note 20 Ultra (US Snapdragon)** – no eSIM.
- **Note 10 and Note 9** – no eSIM hardware.


## 5G and eSIM on Samsung

Starting with the Galaxy S21, every subsequent generation – including the S22, S23, S24, and S25 – fully supports 5G connectivity on eSIM. The international Exynos version of the S20 also supports 5G on eSIM. There is no performance difference between using a physical SIM and an eSIM for 5G.

### Troubleshooting 5G on Samsung eSIM

If 5G isn't working on your Samsung eSIM:

1. Check that 5G is enabled: **Settings > Connections > Mobile Networks > Network Mode** → select **5G/LTE/3G/2G (auto connect)**.
2. Check with your carrier – some carriers restrict 5G on eSIM or require a specific APN.
3. Ensure your plan includes 5G access. Some travel eSIMs are 4G/LTE only.
4. Restart your phone after enabling 5G.

**Remember:** 5G does NOT mean eSIM. Some A-series models (like A15 5G and A16 5G) have 5G but lack eSIM entirely. Always check with `*#06#`.
