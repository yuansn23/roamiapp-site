## Core Summary (Quick Answer)

**Yes – iPhone 11, iPhone 11 Pro, and iPhone 11 Pro Max fully support eSIM.** Following the first eSIM iPhones (XR/XS) in 2018, the iPhone 11 series further improved dual‑SIM stability and carrier profile support.

Here’s what you need to know in 10 seconds:

1. **Check your region:** All global models work. **Mainland China** versions have no eSIM (dual physical SIM instead). Hong Kong/Macau models support physical SIM + eSIM.
2. **Verify with *#06#:** Dial this code. If a **32‑digit EID** appears, your phone is eSIM‑ready.
3. **Activate in minutes:** Scan a QR code from any travel eSIM provider or carrier before you travel. Turn on Data Roaming upon arrival.

> **Pro tip:** Although the iPhone 11 only supports 4G LTE (not 5G), its eSIM works exactly like newer iPhones – no speed or feature limitations. It remains one of the most stable and mature eSIM‑capable iPhones.

## 🔍 How to Verify Your iPhone 11 Supports eSIM (3 Methods)

### Method 1: The EID Code (Fastest & Most Reliable)
1. Open your **Phone app** (dialer).  
2. Enter `*#06#` and press **call**.  
3. Look at the screen:  
   - **EID (32 digits) appears** → ✅ Your iPhone 11 supports eSIM.  
   - **Only IMEI numbers appear** → ❌ No eSIM (likely a China model).  

### Method 2: Check in Settings
Go to **Settings > General > About**. Scroll down to the **“Available SIM”** or **“Digital SIM”** section. If you see an **EID** listed, your device is eSIM ready.

### Method 3: Try Adding a Cellular Plan
Go to **Settings > Cellular > Add Cellular Plan**. If the option is grayed out or missing, your device does not support eSIM. If you can tap it, you’re good to go.

## 📲 How to Activate eSIM on iPhone 11 (Step by Step)

Activation takes less than 2 minutes. Do it **before you fly** – you only need Wi‑Fi.

1. **Buy an eSIM plan** – For travel, choose a [Roami free trial eSIM](/free-esim/) or any travel eSIM provider.  
2. **Open Settings** → **Cellular** → **Add Cellular Plan**.  
3. **Scan the QR code** sent to your email. (If you have a manual activation code, tap “Enter Details Manually”.)  
4. **Label your plan** – e.g., “Travel”, “Work”, “UK Data”.  
5. **Choose default line** – Decide which line (physical SIM or eSIM) will be used for **Cellular Data**, **Voice**, and **SMS**.  
6. **Turn on Data Roaming** for the eSIM line when you arrive at your destination.  

> 💡 **Important:** After you land, go to **Settings > Cellular > [your eSIM plan]** and toggle **Data Roaming** ON. Without this, your eSIM won’t connect. Also ensure your iPhone is carrier‑unlocked.

## ❌ What If Your iPhone 11 Does NOT Show an EID?

The only scenario where an iPhone 11 lacks eSIM is if it was purchased in **mainland China** (or some Hong Kong models sold through Chinese channels). These models have dual physical SIM slots and no eSIM hardware.

**What can you do?**

- **Use a physical travel SIM** – Many providers still offer physical SIM cards shipped worldwide.  
- **Upgrade to a newer iPhone** – Consider an [iPhone 12](/faq/iphone-12-esim-compatible/) or later (again avoid China models).  
- **Check if you actually have a Hong Kong model** – Some Hong Kong iPhones support physical SIM + eSIM. Dial `*#06#` again. If you see an EID, you’re fine.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can I use physical SIM and eSIM at the same time on iPhone 11?
Yes. You can have **one active physical SIM and one active eSIM** simultaneously – true **dual SIM dual standby**.

#### Does iPhone 11 support 5G eSIM?
No – the iPhone 11 only supports **4G LTE**. eSIM works perfectly on LTE, but you won’t get 5G speeds.

#### My iPhone 11 shows “Cellular Plan Cannot Be Added” – what’s wrong?
This usually happens if you have too many stored eSIM profiles. Go to **Settings > Cellular** and remove any old or unused eSIMs. Then restart your phone and try again.

#### Does iPhone 11 eSIM work in Europe?
Yes – but be aware that **UK‑based local eSIMs now charge daily EU roaming fees** after Brexit. For multi‑country trips, buy a **Regional Europe eSIM** instead.

#### Does iPhone 8 or iPhone X support eSIM?
**No.** The iPhone 8, 8 Plus, and iPhone X (released 2017) do not have eSIM hardware. The first iPhones with eSIM are XR, XS, and XS Max. Consider upgrading to an iPhone 11 or newer.

#### What about Jio eSIM on iPhone 11?
Yes – Jio (India) offers eSIM for iPhone 11. You need a postpaid Jio connection and must visit a Jio store for verification. After that, you can convert your physical Jio SIM to eSIM via SMS or the MyJio app.

#### Does the mainland China iPhone 11 have any way to enable eSIM?
No. The hardware completely lacks eSIM – no software update or jailbreak can enable it. Your only option is to use a physical SIM or switch to a non‑China model.

#### How many eSIM profiles can I store on iPhone 11?
You can store up to 8 or more eSIM profiles, but only one can be active at a time (alongside one physical SIM). You can switch between stored eSIMs in Settings.
