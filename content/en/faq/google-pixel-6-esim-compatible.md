## Core Summary (Quick Answer)

**Yes – Google Pixel 6, Pixel 6 Pro, and Pixel 6a all support eSIM, with no regional exceptions.**

Here’s what you need to know in 10 seconds:

1. **All models work:** Pixel 6, Pixel 6 Pro, Pixel 6a – every regional variant (US, EU, Japan, etc.) includes eSIM.  
2. **Verify with *#06#:** Dial this code. If a **32‑digit EID** appears (it will), your phone is eSIM‑ready.  
3. **Activate in minutes:** Go to **Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Add Carrier** and scan a QR code.  

> **Pro tip:** The Pixel 6 series is one of the most eSIM‑friendly Android phones – even Verizon‑locked units support eSIM (Verizon allows it).

## 🔍 How to Verify Your Pixel 6 Supports eSIM

Because all Pixel 6 models support eSIM, the check is mostly for confirmation:

1. Open **Phone dialer** → enter `*#06#` → press **call**.  
2. A **32‑digit EID** should appear. If you see it, eSIM is ready.  

You can also check: **Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Carrier** – look for **“Download a SIM instead?”** or **“Add eSIM”**.

## 📲 How to Activate eSIM on Pixel 6 (Step by Step)

Activation takes less than 2 minutes. Do it **before you fly** – you only need Wi‑Fi.

1. **Buy an eSIM plan** – For travel, choose a [Roami eSIM](/free-esim/) (free trial available) or any provider like Airalo, Visible, or Google Fi.  
2. **Go to Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network > Add Carrier**.  
3. **Scan the QR code** sent to your email. (You can also enter activation code manually.)  
4. **Follow prompts** to download the eSIM profile.  
5. **Choose your default SIM** for calls, SMS, and data.  
6. **Turn on Data Roaming** for the eSIM line when traveling.  

> 💡 For Google Fi users: The Pixel 6 is one of the best phones for Google Fi eSIM – just install the Fi app and activate.

## ❌ What If Your Pixel 6 Does Not Show eSIM? (Very Rare)

In almost all cases, the Pixel 6 has eSIM. However, if you bought a **Japanese carrier variant** (e.g., SoftBank, au), eSIM might be locked. Solutions:

- **Update your phone** – go to **Settings > System > System Update**.  
- **Factory reset** – sometimes resets carrier restrictions (backup first).  
- **Use a physical SIM** – Roami sells physical travel SIMs as a fallback.  

But for 99% of users, your Pixel 6 is ready to go.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

#### Does Google Pixel 6a support eSIM?
Yes – fully compatible, same as Pixel 6 and 6 Pro.

#### Does Pixel 6 Pro support eSIM?
Yes – all Pixel 6 series models support eSIM.

#### Does Verizon Pixel 6 support eSIM?
Yes – even Verizon‑locked Pixel 6 units support eSIM (Verizon allows it). You may need to contact Verizon to activate, but travel eSIMs work without carrier involvement.

#### Can I use eSIM and physical SIM at the same time on Pixel 6?
Yes – you can have one active physical SIM and one active eSIM simultaneously. This is a true **dual SIM compatible** setup.

#### Does Pixel 6 support 5G eSIM?
Yes – Pixel 6 supports 5G on both physical SIM and eSIM.

#### Why is my Pixel 6 not showing “Add Carrier”?
Make sure you’re on the latest Android version. If still missing, try resetting network settings: **Settings > System > Reset Options > Reset Wi-Fi, mobile & Bluetooth**.

#### Does Google Pixel 3 or Pixel 4 support eSIM?
**Yes, but with limitations.** Pixel 3 and 4 series support eSIM in most regions (US, Europe, etc.), but Japanese carrier variants (SoftBank, au) may have eSIM disabled. Pixel 4a, 4a 5G, and Pixel 5 also support eSIM. Always dial `*#06#` – if you see an EID, it works.

#### Does Google Pixel 7 support eSIM?
Yes – Pixel 7, 7 Pro, and 7a all support eSIM fully, just like Pixel 6.

#### What about Holafly on Pixel 6?
Holafly eSIM works perfectly on Pixel 6 – install via QR code in Settings > Network & Internet > Add Carrier.

#### Does Jio eSIM work on Pixel 6 in India?
Yes – Jio supports eSIM on Pixel 6. You need a postpaid Jio SIM and visit a Jio store for verification, then you can convert to eSIM via SMS or MyJio app.
