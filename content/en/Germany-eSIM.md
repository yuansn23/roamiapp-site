        What is PostIdent/VideoIdent and why is it such a problem for tourists?
      a: |
        Germany has one of Europe's strictest SIM registration laws. All prepaid SIMs require <strong>real-name verification</strong> — either in-person at a Deutsche Post branch (PostIdent) or via video call (VideoIdent). For tourists: (1) PostIdent requires finding a Post office during limited hours and queuing; (2) VideoIdent requires speaking German or navigating a German-only app; (3) <strong>Non-EU passports often fail VideoIdent due to glare or camera issues</strong>, forcing you to restart the process. <strong>Roami requires none of this</strong> — no PostIdent, no VideoIdent, no passport registration.
