        What is Codice Fiscale and why is it a problem for tourists buying SIMs in Italy?
      a: |
        <strong>Codice Fiscale</strong> is Italy's personal tax identification number — similar to a Social Security number in the US. <strong>All Italian operators (TIM, Vodafone, Wind Tre) require a Codice Fiscale to register a prepaid SIM card</strong>. Tourists don't have one. TIM stores are known to <strong>refuse selling the TIM Tourist card to foreigners</strong> who can't provide one. Vodafone and Wind Tre also require it for activation. <strong>Roami requires no Codice Fiscale, no passport, no registration</strong> — just buy and go.

        Will my eSIM work on high-speed trains between Rome, Florence, Venice and Milan?

Tunnels (especially the Apennine tunnels on the Rome–Florence route) are the only extended dead zones — signal drops for a few minutes in long tunnels, then reconnects automatically. Your Roami eSIM will pick up the strongest carrier as the train moves. Download the Trenitalia app before you depart — it works great with your eSIM data.
    - q: |
        Which carrier is best in Italy — TIM, Vodafone, or Wind Tre?
      a: |
        All three are solid, but they have different strengths: <strong>TIM</strong> has the best overall 5G coverage (95% of the population) and strongest rural signal. <strong>Vodafone</strong> has fast 5G in urban centers and competitive local plans — but reduces EU roaming data by 50%. <strong>Wind Tre</strong> is the most affordable locally but also reduces EU roaming. With Roami, you don't choose — your eSIM <strong>auto-switches</strong> to whichever carrier has the strongest signal at your location.

      a: |
        Yes — this is a <strong>common and frustrating issue</strong>. TIM, Vodafone, and Wind Tre's online top-up systems <strong>routinely reject non-Italian credit cards</strong>. Even if your card works elsewhere in Italy, the telecom billing systems often block foreign cards. You're then forced to buy physical top-up vouchers at tobacco shops (Tabacchi) — which can be inconvenient with limited hours. <strong>Roami accepts all major payment methods</strong> — Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
