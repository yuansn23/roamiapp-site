More importantly, WiFi only works in your room and lobby — <strong>you don't have data when you're out exploring</strong>. With an eSIM, you can check MRT schedules on the go, find hawker centre recommendations in real time, and share photos instantly. For the price of a coffee, you get connectivity everywhere.
