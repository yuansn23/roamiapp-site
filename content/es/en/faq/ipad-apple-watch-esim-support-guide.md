## Does Your iPad Support eSIM? – 3‑Second Check

**The short answer:**

| Your iPad Type | eSIM Support |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wi‑Fi only iPad** | ❌ **No** – no cellular hardware at all |
| **Wi‑Fi + Cellular iPad (7th gen and newer)** | ✅ **Yes** – eSIM built‑in |
| **Wi‑Fi + Cellular iPad (6th gen and older)** | ❌ **No** – physical SIM only, no embedded SIM chip |

> **Quick visual check:** Flip your iPad over. If the back has a **black plastic strip** at the top (for antenna) – it's a cellular model. If the back is all metal – it's Wi‑Fi only.

**Even if your iPad has a physical SIM tray, it also contains an embedded eSIM chip on cellular models from 7th generation onward.** You can use either – or both in dual‑SIM mode.

The [official Apple support page for eSIM on iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108072) provides additional details on compatible models. The technology behind digital SIM profiles is governed by the [GSMA standard SGP.22](https://www.gsma.com/esim/). For the complete list of **eSIM compatible phones** and devices across all brands, see our **[full compatibility list](/compatibility/)** .

### How to Identify Your Apple Watch Model

**Physical check:**
- **GPS + Cellular models** have a **red ring** around the Digital Crown (Series 4–10) or a **red dot** on the Digital Crown (Series 3 and earlier).
- **GPS‑only models** have no red markings – the Digital Crown is plain.

**Software check:**
- On the watch: **Settings > General > About > Model Number**. Tap the model number – if it starts with **A** and has a "Cellular" label in the Watch app, it supports eSIM.
- On the paired iPhone: **Watch app > My Watch > General > About**. Look for **"Cellular"** under "Available" – if present, the watch supports eSIM.

> An **Apple Watch cellular eSIM** is not a standalone digital SIM profile – it is a "companion" eSIM that shares your iPhone's phone number (or can be set up independently via Family Setup). You need an **Apple Watch eSIM plan** from your carrier. The [official Apple support page for Apple Watch cellular](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT205739) provides additional details on setup and carrier compatibility.


## How to Check if Your iPad Supports eSIM

### Method 1: Look for the Model Name
- Go to **Settings > General > About**.
- Look at **Model Name**. If it contains **"Cellular"** or **"Wi‑Fi + Cellular"**, your iPad supports eSIM (provided it's 7th gen or newer).
- If it says **"Wi‑Fi"** only – no eSIM.

### Method 2: Check for EID (The Definitive Test)
- Go to **Settings > General > About**.
- Scroll down. If you see a **Digital SIM** section with a **32‑digit EID** number, your iPad has the required embedded SIM hardware.
- For **iPad Pro 11 eSIM** or **iPad Pro 12.9 eSIM** owners, the EID will be present on all cellular models.

### Method 3: Look for "Add eSIM" in Settings
- Go to **Settings > Cellular Data** (or **Mobile Data**).
- If you see **"Add eSIM"** or **"Add Cellular Plan"**, your iPad supports eSIM.

### Method 4: Physical Inspection
- Flip your iPad over. If the back has a **black or dark plastic strip** at the top edge (for cellular antennas), it's a cellular model.
- If the back is **all metal** with no plastic strip, it's Wi‑Fi only.


## How to Check if Your Apple Watch Supports eSIM

- The watch must be a **GPS + Cellular** model. Look for a **red ring** on the Digital Crown (Series 4 and newer) or a **red dot** on older models.
- On the watch itself: **Settings > Cellular**. If you see "Set Up Cellular", it's ready.
- On your paired iPhone: Open the **Watch app** > **Cellular**. If you see options to add a plan, the watch supports eSIM.


## How to Activate eSIM on iPad

Activation is very similar to iPhone – but with one key difference: **iPad eSIM plans are data‑only** (no phone number for calls/SMS).

### Step‑by‑Step iPad eSIM Activation

1. **Purchase an eSIM data plan** from a provider that supports tablets. Many offer **iPad eSIM plans** specifically for tablets. We recommend [Roami eSIM](/free-esim/) for a free trial.
2. On your iPad, go to **Settings > Cellular Data > Add eSIM**.
3. Scan the QR code provided by your eSIM carrier, or tap **"Enter Details Manually"** and input the SM‑DP+ address and activation code.
4. Label your plan (e.g., "Travel Japan" or "US Data") – this helps when you have multiple profiles.
5. Set the plan as your default for data if needed.
6. **Critical step for travel:** Turn on **Data Roaming** for the eSIM line (Settings > Cellular Data > tap the eSIM > Data Roaming ON).

### Dual SIM on iPad: Physical SIM + eSIM
Cellular iPads can use **one physical SIM and one eSIM** simultaneously (or two eSIMs on newer models with iPadOS 16+). This is great for keeping your home carrier active while adding a local data plan.

**How to switch between SIMs on iPad:**
- Go to **Settings > Cellular Data**. You'll see both plans listed. Tap on the one you want to use for mobile data.
- You can also set which SIM is used for iMessage and FaceTime (under **Settings > Messages > Send & Receive**).

### Understanding iPad eSIM Plans (Data‑Only)
Most iPad eSIM plans are **data‑only** – they do not include a phone number for calls or SMS. This is fine for web browsing, email, streaming, and apps like WhatsApp or iMessage (which work over data). If you need a phone number on your iPad, consider using your iPhone's personal hotspot or a VoIP service like Skype or Google Voice.

### How Many eSIM Profiles Can an iPad Store?
- iPads with iPadOS 16+ can store **multiple eSIM profiles** (typically 5 or more, with newer Pro models supporting up to 8).
- **Only one eSIM can be active at a time** alongside a physical SIM.
- To switch between stored eSIMs: **Settings > Cellular Data > tap the desired plan > Turn On This Line**.

### Renaming eSIM Plans on iPad
- Settings > Cellular Data > tap the plan > **Cellular Plan Label** – choose a custom name (e.g., "Japan Trip" or "Work Data").


## How to Activate eSIM on Apple Watch

Activation differs because the Apple Watch does not have its own QR code scanner. You set it up via your paired iPhone.

### What Type of Apple Watch eSIM Plan Do You Need?

| Setup Type | What It Does | Who It's For | Time Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Companion Plan** | Watch shares your iPhone's phone number | Most users – calls/texts go to both devices | 3-5 min |
| **Family Setup (Standalone)** | Watch has its own phone number | Kids, elderly family members, or anyone without an iPhone | 5-10 min |

### Standard Setup (Sharing Your iPhone's Number)

1. On your iPhone, open the **Watch app**.
2. Tap **Cellular** > **Set Up Cellular**.
3. Follow your carrier's instructions – usually you log in to your carrier account and assign a plan to the watch.
4. The **Apple Watch eSIM plan** will be downloaded to the watch automatically (it may take 2-5 minutes).
5. Wait for the "Cellular Setup Complete" confirmation.

### Apple Watch Family Setup (Standalone Watch)

**What is Family Setup?**
Family Setup allows you to set up an Apple Watch for a family member who doesn't have their own iPhone. The watch gets its own phone number and works independently for calls, texts, and data.

**Requirements:**
- An Apple Watch with GPS + Cellular
- An iPhone 8 or newer (running iOS 16 or later) belonging to the family organizer
- A carrier that supports standalone Apple Watch plans (see table below)

**Step‑by‑Step Family Setup:**

| Step | Action | Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | On the parent's iPhone, open the **Watch app**. | 10 sec |
| **2** | Tap **All Watches** > **Add Watch**. | 10 sec |
| **3** | Choose **Set Up for a Family Member**. | 10 sec |
| **4** | Hold the watch near the iPhone – a pairing animation will appear. Scan it. | 30 sec |
| **5** | Follow the on‑screen prompts to configure settings (Schooltime, Location Sharing, etc.). | 2 min |
| **6** | When prompted, tap **Set Up Cellular**. | 10 sec |
| **7** | You'll need the watch's EID (shown on the watch screen) – enter it when prompted or scan the QR code your carrier provides. | 1 min |
| **8** | Follow your carrier's activation flow to add a standalone plan. | 2-3 min |
| **9** | Once complete, the watch works independently – calls, texts, and data without an iPhone nearby. | N/A |

**Carriers that support Family Setup (US):**
- **T‑Mobile** – supports Family Setup on most plans
- **Verizon** – supports Family Setup (requires a Number Share plan)
- **AT&T** – limited support – check with AT&T directly

> **Note:** An **Apple Watch cellular eSIM** cannot be used independently for data without a paired iPhone (except for limited functions like emergency calls). The watch is designed to complement your phone – even with Family Setup, the watch has its own number but still relies on the family organizer's account for management.

## Choosing eSIM Plans for iPad and Apple Watch

### For iPad (Data‑Only)
You have two options:
- **Travel eSIMs** – Perfect for short trips. You buy a plan for a specific country or region, install it on your iPad, and get data immediately.
- **Monthly or prepaid data plans** – Some carriers offer recurring data plans for tablets.

Either way, once the plan is installed, the iPad gets its own **eSIM internet** connection – full mobile data independent of Wi‑Fi or your phone. That's what makes a cellular iPad worth it for travel: you're not stuck hunting for café Wi‑Fi to check maps, email, or streaming on a larger screen.

We recommend starting with a **free trial eSIM** from [Roami](/free-esim/) to test your iPad's eSIM functionality at zero cost.

### For Apple Watch (Companion Plan)
- Apple Watch eSIM is **always tied to a carrier**. You cannot use a generic travel eSIM on a watch.
- The watch's eSIM plan is typically an add‑on to your existing iPhone plan ($5–$15 per month).
- **Family Setup** requires a carrier that supports standalone watch plans.

### Can You Use the Same eSIM on iPhone and iPad?

**Short answer: No.**

**Why:** Each eSIM profile is cryptographically bound to the device's **EID** (eUICC Identifier). The EID is unique to each device – your iPhone's EID is different from your iPad's EID.

**What you can do instead:**
| Option | How It Works |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Personal Hotspot** | Use your iPhone's data connection on your iPad (no extra plan needed) |
| **Separate eSIM plans** | Buy a data‑only eSIM for your iPad (most travel eSIMs support tablets) |
| **Multi‑device plans** | Rare – some carriers offer shared data plans across multiple devices (check with your carrier) |

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** If your iPhone has a generous data plan, skip the iPad eSIM and just use Personal Hotspot. It's cheaper and simpler.


## Advanced: Managing eSIM Profiles on iPad and Watch

### How to Delete an eSIM from iPad
1. Go to **Settings > Cellular Data**.
2. Tap the eSIM plan you want to remove.
3. Scroll down and tap **Remove Cellular Plan**.
4. Confirm deletion. This permanently removes the carrier profile – you cannot recover it unless you have the original QR code or activation details.

### How to Delete an eSIM from Apple Watch
1. On the watch, go to **Settings > Cellular**.
2. Tap the plan you want to remove.
3. Scroll down and tap **Remove [Carrier] Plan**.
4. Alternatively, on the paired iPhone: Watch app > Cellular > tap the plan > Remove.

### Managing Multiple eSIMs on iPad
- Newer iPads (with iPadOS 16+) can store multiple eSIM profiles (often 5 or more).
- **Only one eSIM can be active at a time** alongside a physical SIM.
- To switch between stored eSIMs: **Settings > Cellular Data > tap the desired plan > Turn On This Line**.

### iPadOS 18 eSIM Management Features
iPadOS 18 introduces several improvements for eSIM management:
- **Faster profile switching** – switch between stored eSIM profiles directly from Control Center.
- **Better labeling** – easier to rename and organize multiple profiles.
- **Simplified activation** – improved QR code scanning and manual entry flow.
- **Enhanced error messages** – clearer descriptions when activation fails (e.g., "QR code expired" instead of generic errors).


## Troubleshooting eSIM on iPad and Apple Watch

### Common iPad eSIM Errors

| Error Message | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Cellular plan cannot be added"** | iPad is carrier locked or eSIM is disabled | Contact original carrier to unlock; if Wi‑Fi only model, no solution |
| **"QR code is invalid or expired"** | QR code expired or damaged | Request a new QR from your eSIM provider |
| **"Profile download failed"** | Poor internet connection during setup | Connect to stable Wi‑Fi, restart iPad, try again |
| **eSIM installed but no data** | Data Roaming is off | Go to Settings > Cellular > eSIM > enable Data Roaming |
| **iPad says "No SIM" even after activation** | eSIM profile corrupted | Delete the profile and re‑install using the QR code |
| **"Unable to activate eSIM"** | Wi‑Fi issue or server problem | Restart, check Wi‑Fi, wait, then retry |

### Common Apple Watch eSIM Errors

| Error Message | Likely Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Cellular Setup Failed"** | Carrier doesn't support watch eSIM or watch is not cellular model | Verify watch model; contact carrier for support |
| **Watch shows "Searching" or "No Connection"** | Out of coverage or plan not active | Ensure you're in a supported coverage area; restart watch |
| **Cannot transfer watch eSIM to new iPhone** | Watch is still paired to old iPhone | Unpair watch from old iPhone first (this removes the eSIM), then re‑pair with new iPhone and set up cellular again |
| **Family Setup fails** | Carrier doesn't support standalone plans | Switch to a carrier that supports Family Setup (e.g., T‑Mobile US, Verizon) |
| **"No cellular plan" on watch after setup** | Plan didn't download properly | Restart watch, go to Settings > Cellular > tap plan > re‑download |

### General Troubleshooting Steps (iPad & Watch)

| Step | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Restart the device** – simple but often fixes temporary glitches. |
| **2** | **Update to latest OS** – iPadOS or watchOS updates often include eSIM fixes. |
| **3** | **Reset network settings** – On iPad: Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPad > Reset > Reset Network Settings. (This does not delete eSIM profiles but resets Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth.) |
| **4** | **Check for carrier settings update** – On iPad: Settings > General > About – if an update is available, a pop‑up will appear. |
| **5** | **Toggle Airplane Mode** – On/Off for 30 seconds to force network re‑registration. |

For deeper troubleshooting (including dual‑eSIM issues on iPhone/iPad), see our **[eSIM Deep Troubleshooting Guide 2026](/faq/esim-deep-troubleshooting-guide-2026/)** and **[iPhone Dual eSIM Not Working? 12 Fixes](/faq/dual-esim-not-working-12-fixes-for-iphone/)** – many principles apply to iPad as well.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the iPad support eSIM?**
Yes – all cellular iPad models from the 7th generation onward, including the iPad Pro, iPad Air, iPad mini, and iPad 10th generation, have built‑in eSIM.

**Does the iPad Pro 11‑inch have eSIM?**
Yes. The **iPad Pro 11 eSIM** works on all cellular versions. The same is true for the **iPad Pro 12.9 eSIM**.

**Does the iPad Air M2 or M3 have eSIM?**
Yes. Both the **iPad Air M2 eSIM** and **iPad Air M3 eSIM** are fully supported on cellular models.

**Does the iPad mini 6th gen have eSIM?**
Yes. The **iPad mini 6 eSIM** works without any issues.

**Does the iPad mini 7 (A17 Pro) have eSIM?**
Yes. The **iPad mini 7 eSIM** works out of the box. **Important:** The cellular model is **eSIM‑only** – there is no physical SIM tray.

**Does the iPad 10th generation support eSIM?**
Yes. The **iPad 10th gen eSIM** is present on cellular models.

**Does Apple Watch Series 10 have eSIM?**
Yes. The **Apple Watch Series 10 eSIM** is standard on the GPS + Cellular model.

**Does Apple Watch Series 9 have eSIM?**
Yes. The **Apple Watch Series 9 eSIM** is a standard feature on the GPS + Cellular version.

**Does Apple Watch Ultra have eSIM?**
Yes. The **Apple Watch Ultra eSIM** works just like other cellular Apple Watches.

**Does Apple Watch Ultra 2 have eSIM?**
Yes. The **Apple Watch Ultra 2 eSIM** is fully supported on the cellular model.

**Can I use the same eSIM on my iPhone and iPad?**
No – eSIM profiles are tied to a single device's EID. You need a separate plan for each device. Some carriers offer "multi‑device" plans, but those are rare.

**Does eSIM drain battery on Apple Watch?**
Slightly, but the watch is designed to handle it. Using cellular constantly will reduce battery life, but for typical daily use it's fine. Expect about 10‑15% less battery life on days with heavy cellular usage.

**Can I make phone calls from my iPad using eSIM?**
No – iPads do not have a Phone app. However, you can make calls using FaceTime Audio, WhatsApp, Skype, or other VoIP apps over the eSIM data connection.

**What about flip phones with eSIM?**
Modern flip smartphones like the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip series support eSIM. Traditional feature flip phones do not. For a detailed comparison, see our **[iPhone eSIM compatibility guide](/faq/iphone-11-esim-compatible/)**.

**How do I know if my carrier supports Apple Watch eSIM?**
Check your carrier's website or contact customer support. Look for "Apple Watch cellular plan" or "wearable plan."

**Can I use a free trial eSIM on my iPad?**
Yes – [Roami offers a free trial eSIM](/free-esim/) that works on any cellular iPad.

**How many eSIM profiles can my iPad store?**
iPads with iPadOS 16+ can store multiple eSIM profiles (typically 5 or more, with Pro models supporting up to 8), but only one eSIM can be active at a time alongside a physical SIM.

**Does iPadOS 18 change anything about eSIM?**
Yes – iPadOS 18 introduces faster profile switching from Control Center, better labeling, simplified activation, and clearer error messages.

**Does Apple Watch Family Setup work with any carrier?**
No – Family Setup requires a carrier that supports standalone Apple Watch plans. In the US, T‑Mobile and Verizon offer this; AT&T has limited support. Check with your carrier before buying.

**What if my iPad has an EID in Settings but still won't activate eSIM?**
Your iPad may be carrier‑locked. Check Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock – it must say "No SIM restrictions." If locked, contact your carrier for an unlock.

**Does the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5 have eSIM?**
Yes – the Galaxy Z Flip 5 supports eSIM. All Z Flip models from Flip3 onward have full eSIM support.

**Can I use a data‑only eSIM on my iPad for international travel?**
Yes – most travel eSIMs work perfectly on iPads. Just scan the QR code, enable Data Roaming, and you're connected.

**How long does Apple Watch eSIM activation take?**
The setup process takes 3‑5 minutes, but the watch may take an additional 2‑5 minutes to register on the network. If it takes longer than 10 minutes, restart both devices.

**What is Number Share?**
Number Share is a feature that allows your Apple Watch to share the same phone number as your iPhone. This is the standard setup for most Apple Watch users. It requires carrier support and is different from Family Setup (which gives the watch its own number).
