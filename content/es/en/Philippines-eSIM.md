        What is the Philippines SIM Registration Act (RA 11934) — and do I need to do it for Roami?
      a: |
        The SIM Registration Act (RA 11934) is a Philippine law requiring all SIM cards to be registered with a valid ID. For tourists, this means submitting: your passport (with entry stamp), a copy of your return flight ticket, proof of address in the Philippines (e.g., hotel booking), and a live selfie taken with your phone's camera. The process can take 1-2 hours and must be done after you arrive. Roami eSIM is a data-only travel plan that bypasses RA 11934 entirely. No passport, no flight ticket, no hotel proof, no selfie. Just buy online, scan the QR code, and you're connected within minutes of landing.

        Why does buying a local SIM at Manila airport take so long?

        Under RA 11934, every SIM purchase requires real-time registration. At Manila and Cebu airports, Smart and Globe counters must process each tourist individually — verifying passports, entry stamps, and hotel bookings, then capturing a live selfie.
