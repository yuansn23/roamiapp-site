faq:
- q: Can I use an eSIM in Uruguay if my phone is locked to a carrier?
  a: No, your phone must be unlocked to use a local eSIM. Check with your current
    carrier before traveling. For a list of compatible devices, see our <a href="/compatibility/"
    class="font-bold text-blue-600">eSIM compatibility</a> page.
- q: Will I lose my home number if I switch to a Uruguay eSIM?
  a: No. You can keep your physical SIM active for calls/texts while using the eSIM
    for data. Just enable 'Cellular Data' on the eSIM line in your settings.
- q: How do I top up my Uruguay eSIM data plan?
  a: Roami eSIMs come with a fixed data allowance. If you need more, you can purchase
    a new eSIM plan online and install it as a second profile. No physical top-up
    needed.
- q: Is 5G available everywhere in Uruguay?
  a: 5G is primarily available in Montevideo, Punta del Este, and other major urban
    centers. In rural areas, you'll typically get 4G LTE, which is still fast for
    most tasks.
- q: Can I use a Uruguay eSIM for tethering or hotspot?
  a: Yes, most Roami eSIM plans allow hotspot usage. However, check the specific plan's
    fair use policy to avoid throttling after heavy usage.

myths:
- myth: All eSIMs in Uruguay use the same network, so any operator works.
  truth: False. Antel and Claro have different coverage footprints. Antel is stronger
    in coastal and rural areas, while Claro is more affordable in cities. Choose based
    on your itinerary.
- myth: You need a local ID to buy a prepaid eSIM in Uruguay.
  truth: Not true for Roami eSIMs. They are available to international travelers without
    registration. Just purchase online and install the profile before your trip.
- myth: Uruguay's mobile network is too slow for video calls or streaming.
  truth: Incorrect. Uruguay has one of the fastest mobile networks in Latin America,
    with median 5G speeds above 40 Mbps in urban areas – more than enough for HD streaming
    and video conferencing.
- myth: Physical SIMs always offer better coverage than eSIMs.
  truth: No. eSIMs connect to the same physical towers as physical SIMs. The coverage
    quality depends on the underlying network (Antel or Claro), not the SIM form factor.
- myth: You must activate the eSIM immediately after landing, or it expires.
  truth: Roami eSIMs have a flexible activation window. You can install the profile
    before departure, and it will activate automatically when you connect to a supported
    network in Uruguay. No rush.
