before_buy_title: Things you need to know before buying a Nigeria eSIM
before_buy_sections:
- heading: 1. Main 5G/4G frequency bands in Nigeria
  content: 'Nigeria''s mobile networks operate on the following key bands: 4G LTE
    bands 1 (2100 MHz), 3 (1800 MHz), 7 (2600 MHz), 8 (900 MHz), 20 (800 MHz), and
    28 (700 MHz). 5G networks use n78 (3500 MHz) and n41 (2500 MHz). Ensure your device
    supports these bands for optimal connectivity.'
- heading: 2. KYC requirements for eSIM activation
  content: To activate a Nigeria eSIM, you must provide a valid passport or national
    ID for identity verification. Roami handles the KYC process digitally, so you
    only need to upload a clear photo of your ID during purchase.
- heading: 3. Fair Usage Policy (FUP) and data limits
  content: Most Nigeria eSIM plans have a Fair Usage Policy (FUP) that caps high-speed
    data after a certain threshold (e.g., 50 GB per month). Once exceeded, speeds
    may be throttled to 128 kbps. Check your plan details before heavy usage.
- heading: 4. Network coverage in rural vs urban areas
  content: MTN offers the widest coverage, including rural areas. Airtel and Glo have
    strong urban presence but may be weaker in remote regions. ipNX is primarily fixed
    broadband in cities. For travel outside major cities, MTN is recommended.
- heading: 5. eSIM compatibility and device support
  content: eSIM is supported on most modern smartphones (iPhone XS and newer, Google
    Pixel 3 and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer). Check your device's IMEI for
    <a href="/compatibility/" class="font-bold text-blue-600">eSIM compatibility</a>
    before purchasing.
