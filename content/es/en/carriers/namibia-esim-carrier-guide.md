faq:
- q: Can I use a Namibia eSIM for tethering or hotspot?
  a: Yes, most Namibia eSIM plans support tethering. However, check the plan's fair
    usage policy to avoid throttling after heavy usage.
- q: Will my eSIM work in all parts of Namibia?
  a: MTC offers the widest coverage, including remote areas like Etosha and Sossusvlei.
    Telecom Namibia is best in cities and coastal towns. For rural travel, MTC is
    recommended.
- q: How do I top up my Namibia eSIM?
  a: You can top up online through your eSIM provider's portal or app. Some providers
    also offer automatic top-up options to prevent service interruption.
- q: Is there a risk of data throttling with Namibia eSIMs?
  a: Some budget plans may throttle speeds after a certain data cap. Always read the
    plan details before purchase. Premium plans from MTC and Telecom Namibia typically
    offer consistent speeds.
- q: Can I keep my home number while using a Namibia eSIM?
  a: Yes, you can use your home SIM for calls and SMS while the eSIM handles data.
    This helps avoid international roaming fees for data.
