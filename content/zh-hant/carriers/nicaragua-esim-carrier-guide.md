## Things you must know before buying a Nicaragua eSIM


### Check your device compatibility {#check-your-device-compatibility}
Before purchasing an eSIM for Nicaragua, ensure your smartphone is unlocked and supports eSIM technology. Most modern phones from Apple, Samsung, Google, and Huawei are compatible. Visit our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page to verify your model.

### Understanding Nicaragua's mobile landscape {#understanding-nicaraguas-mobile-landscape}
Nicaragua has two main mobile operators: Claro and Tigo. Both offer prepaid eSIM options through Roami. Claro has the widest rural coverage, while Tigo excels in urban areas. 3G is still present in remote zones, but 4G/LTE is the standard in cities and tourist spots. 5G is not yet widely deployed.
