# Sweden eSIM: Reliable Network for Business Travel

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For the best balance of coverage and speed in Sweden, choose Telia eSIM – it dominates rural areas and the Stockholm metro. For ultra-fast 5G in cities, Telenor eSIM is a top pick. Both eliminate roaming fees and work with any <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> device.

## Things you must know before buying a Sweden eSIM


### Check Your Device Compatibility {#check-your-device-compatibility}
Before purchasing a Sweden eSIM, ensure your smartphone is unlocked and supports eSIM technology. Most modern iPhones (XR and later), Google Pixels, and Samsung Galaxy S20+ models work. Visit our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page for a full list.

### Understand Local Network Bands {#understand-local-network-bands}
Sweden uses 4G bands 1, 3, 7, 8, 20, and 5G bands n78, n28. All major eSIM providers support these, but older Chinese or US-only phones may lack band 20 (important for rural coverage). Check your device specs to avoid connectivity gaps.

### Activation and Setup Tips {#activation-and-setup-tips}
Most Sweden eSIMs activate automatically upon landing. To avoid issues: install the eSIM profile before your trip, enable data roaming in settings, and ensure your primary SIM is turned off or set to data roaming off. No physical SIM swap needed.
