# Indonesia eSIM: 24/7 High-Speed Data Service

> **💡 Quick Summary for Travelers:** For most travelers, Telkomsel eSIM offers the widest 4G/5G coverage across Indonesia, especially in remote areas like Komodo Island. If you prioritize speed in urban centers like Jakarta or Bali, XL Axiata eSIM provides excellent throughput. Both support instant activation and eliminate roaming fees – ideal for tourists needing reliable data for maps and social media.

## Things you must know before buying a Indonesia eSIM


### Device compatibility {#device-compatibility}
Most modern smartphones support eSIM, including iPhone XS and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, and Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer. Check your device's settings or visit our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page for a full list.

### Activation process {#activation-process}
After purchase, you'll receive a QR code via email. Scan it with your phone's camera to install the eSIM profile. Activation happens automatically once you land in Indonesia and connect to a local network. No physical SIM swap needed.

### Data roaming settings {#data-roaming-settings}
For the eSIM to work, you must enable data roaming in your phone's settings. This is standard for all eSIMs. Your home SIM can remain active for calls/SMS, but disable its data to avoid roaming charges from your home carrier.

### Coverage considerations {#coverage-considerations}
Indonesia is an archipelago with varying network quality. Telkomsel offers the widest coverage, including remote islands. XL Axiata is faster in cities but may have gaps in rural areas. Choose based on your itinerary.
