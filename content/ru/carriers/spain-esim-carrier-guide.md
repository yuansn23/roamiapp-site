## Things you must know before buying a Spain eSIM


### Device Compatibility Check {#device-compatibility-check}
Before purchasing a Spain eSIM, ensure your smartphone supports eSIM technology. Most recent models from Apple, Samsung, Google, and Huawei are compatible. Check our <a href=\"/compatibility/\" class=\"font-bold text-blue-600\">eSIM compatibility</a> page for a full list of supported devices.

### Network Coverage in Spain {#network-coverage-in-spain}
Spain's mobile infrastructure is excellent, with 4G covering over 98% of the population. 5G is rapidly expanding in cities like Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, and Seville. Rural areas may have slower speeds, but Vodafone and Movistar offer the best rural coverage.

### Activation and Setup Tips {#activation-and-setup-tips}
Most Spain eSIMs activate automatically upon landing. Simply enable data roaming in your phone's settings. No physical SIM removal is needed, and you can keep your home SIM active for calls. For troubleshooting, refer to our setup guide.
